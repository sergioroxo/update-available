## First UPDATE ritual → Era 2  [the metaphor; glitch-morph; new login]

The grammar every era transition will reuse: **notification → EULA → changelog
→ glitch-install → new login.** Triggered by the system's "success," never the
player.

**1. Notification** (types itself on the dark screen):
> `An update is installing. Do not turn off your computer.`

**2. EULA — consent as visual hierarchy** (⚑ Sérgio's design): two bold legible
lines; everything else greyed/blurred into illegible paragraph-shapes; one
**[ I AGREE ]**, no decline.
- *"By continuing you accept the terms of care."*
- *"You agree the program **corrects** in your best interest."*  ← v0.8 §6
- (The cruelty is legible and skipped — that is the critique of unread consent.)

**3. Changelog-as-thesis** (⚑ close to the moral voice — draft, you own it):
> `PHASE/2 95  →  PHASE/2 2000`
> `· profile migrated while you were away`
> `· enrollment added to your record`
> `· "cure" is still available`
> `· new: ongoing accountability`
(The *"cure deprecated → support installed"* rebrand is held for the **Era 2→3**
update — the ex-gay collapse. Era 1→2 keeps cure-era language; the person
returns to a world only hardened.)

**4. Glitch-install + new embodiment** (v0.8 §4): as the bar fills, the **room
glitch-morphs** into Era 2 — geometry, palette, props reshaping in
system-glitch (the ominous flavour); the **material + sound palette shifts**
(audio milestone). It resolves not on Alex's desktop but on a **new login for a
new person** — the next life, the next era's persona. The player's own name
returns only at the very end, beside the others.

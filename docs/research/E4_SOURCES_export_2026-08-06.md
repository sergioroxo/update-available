https://bylinetimes.com/2024/08/14/us-religious-groups-conversion-therapy-europe/

Research pass run 2026-08-06. Web search + web fetch only (no paywalled
databases, no FOI requests). Every claim below is sourced with a working URL
as of the date this file was written; URLs can rot.

**Headline: the claim substantially holds**, but two specific numbers in the
original piece do not survive close checking, and the retreat-locations claim

history but does not describe 2025–2026 practice.

## 2. What was found, and what surprised me

**IFTCC is real, active, and well documented independently of Byline Times.**
Wikipedia's article on IFTCC (citing ITV News, PinkNews, Dagens Nyheter and
others, 2018–2022) and the Global Project Against Hate and Extremism (GPAHE)
report "Conversion Therapy Online: The Players" both independently confirm
IFTCC's founder (Mike Davidson, ex-Core Issues Trust), its London base, and a
history of annual conferences stretching back before 2024. This is the
strongest-anchored bullet in the whole piece.

**The "more than 30 countries" figure does not match IFTCC's own published
numbers for the event in question.** IFTCC's own press release for the
October 2024 "Into The Wind" conference
(https://iftcc.org/global-therapists-gather-228527-2/) states **28 nations**,
140+ delegates, 29 speakers. A separate, earlier IFTCC Warsaw conference (Oct
2023, per the earlier Byline Times piece "These LGBT Freaks — Do We Have Them
Castrated?", https://bylinetimes.com/2023/12/09/these-lgbt-freaks-do-we-have-them-castrated-inside-europes-invite-only-conversion-therapy-conference/)
drew **34 nations**. "More than 30" sits between the two real figures but
matches neither one precisely for the event it's attached to in the August
2024 article. This reads like a rounding/conflation error rather than a
fabrication, but it is not corroborated as written.

**The apparent "second sources" are not independent.** Two pieces that look
like separate corroboration — "Exporting Conversion Therapy" in The
Progressive (19 Feb 2025, https://progressive.org/magazine/exporting-conversion-therapy-toesland-20250219/)
and a reprint on 76crimes.com (24 Mar 2025, https://76crimes.com/2025/03/24/exporting-conversion-therapy/)
— are **by the same reporter, Finbarr Toesland**, who wrote the original
Byline Times piece (https://bylinetimes.com/author/finbarrtoesland/). They
add genuinely new reporting (GAFCON IV Rwanda 2023 numbers, an "Africa
Project" by Core Issues Trust, named individual testimonies), which is useful
context, but they are the same investigator's expanding body of work, not an
independent check on his original claims. Per the task's own rule, this does
not count as corroboration — flagging it explicitly because at a glance it
looks like it should.

**GPAHE's "Conversion Therapy Online" report series is the one genuinely
independent watchdog source that touches nearly every bullet at once.** It
separately documents IFTCC, CHANGED Movement, Brothers Road/Journey Into
Manhood (including the specific "safe healing touch" practice), and Joseph
Nicolosi Jr's Breakthrough Clinic — all without citing Byline Times. This is
the single most load-bearing corroborating source found.
(https://globalextremism.org/reports/conversion-therapy-online-the-players/)

**The Journey Into Manhood England/Poland claim is accurate as history, not
as current practice.** Brothers Road's own site states it first ran the
retreat in England in 2007 and in Poland in 2014
(https://brothersroad.org/founding-growth/). But its current retreat listing
(https://brothersroad.org/jim/) shows no scheduled England or Poland events —
2026–2027 dates are in Indiana, Texas, Virginia (all US), Egypt, Israel, and
Mexico. A Strong Support Facebook post (independently, not from Brothers
Road) also references a JiM workshop in Egypt. This suggests Brothers Road's
non-US footprint has shifted toward the Middle East/North Africa since the
2024 article, not that European operations were formally ended — no
statement to that effect was found either way. Genuinely unclear; flagged
rather than inferred.

**Strong Support's own site is thin on the specific "webinar series /
directory" framing.** Training-to-schools is confirmed on Strong Support's
own site at a stated price (£100/hour). The webinar archive is large (~50+
entries) and does include at least one American figure (Richard Cohen, a
well-documented US conversion-therapy proponent), but I could not
independently verify the claim that "all eight counsellors listed are
US-based" — that specific tally could not be reproduced from what's publicly
browsable.

## 3. What is genuinely unavailable

- No independent, non-Toesland source could be found confirming the
  precise "$299.99" / "$9.98" figures as *quoted* — only the organisations'
  own current shop pages, which show closely adjacent but not identical
  numbers (prices likely fluctuate/sale-price).
- No independent nationality breakdown of Strong Support's webinar
  presenters or website-directory listings was found.
- No record was found — pro or con — of any legal, regulatory, or
  deregistration action specifically against IFTCC, CHANGED Movement,
  Brothers Road, or the Reintegrative Therapy Association, in Europe or
  the US, as of 2026-08-06.
- Whether Brothers Road's absence of England/Poland dates on its current
  site reflects a policy decision, a scheduling gap, or discontinuation
  could not be established either way.

## 4. Post-2024 developments (through 2026-08-06)

- **IFTCC conferences have continued.** An 11th annual conference ran
  24–26 Oct 2025 in Poland ("Towards Wholeness in Sexuality, Therapy,
  Counselling, and Pastoral Formation" —
  https://iftcc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/IFTCC2025-English-version-15-October2025.pdf),
  and a 12th is scheduled 6–8 Nov 2026 in Krakow ("Raise the Sails" —
  https://iftcc.org/event/iftcc-conference-2026/). No break in the annual
  cadence found.
- **No ban, deregistration, or court action against IFTCC, CHANGED,
  Brothers Road, or Reintegrative Therapy Association was found.**
- **Regulatory pressure has grown at the pan-European level but has not
  yet produced binding law reaching these organisations:**
  - Council of Europe Parliamentary Assembly adopted a resolution
    (71 votes to 26) on 28–30 Jan 2026 calling on its 46 member states to
    prohibit conversion practices — non-binding, and the Council of Europe
    is a separate body from the EU
    (https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/01/30/conversion-therapy-council-of-europe/).
  - The European Parliament voted 29 Apr 2026 (405 MEPs in favour) to back
    an EU-wide ban, following a Citizens' Initiative petition with 1.2m+
    signatures; this passes the question to the European Commission, which
    alone can propose binding EU legislation
    (https://www.washingtonblade.com/2026/04/30/european-parliament-backs-eu-wide-conversion-therapy-ban/).
  - As of these reports, only Belgium, Cyprus, France, Malta, Portugal and
    Spain (plus Germany and Greece with narrower minors-only/consent-based
    bans) have national bans; **Poland — where IFTCC holds its
    conferences — still has no national ban.**
- **The UK — IFTCC's own registered base — still has no conversion
  therapy ban in force.** A trans-inclusive draft bill was published
  25 Jun 2026 (up to 5 years' imprisonment proposed) and is in
  pre-legislative scrutiny, after years of delay since the ban was first
  promised in 2018
  (https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/06/25/conversion-therapy-ban-uk-2026/).
  This means IFTCC has operated lawfully from London throughout the period
  covered by this research.

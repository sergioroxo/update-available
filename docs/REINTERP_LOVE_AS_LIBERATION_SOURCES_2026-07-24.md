## The sources (dossier-grade; `status` + `confidence` per the provotype schema)

### N1 · Michael Bussee & Gary Cooper — the flagship
- **status:** documentary · **confidence:** high (multiply, reputably documented)
- **text (draft, Sérgio's pass owns final wording):** Two of Exodus International's co-founders.
  Bussee left in 1979 after falling in love with Gary Cooper, a fellow ex-gay counselor; they
  later divorced their wives and held a commitment ceremony in 1982, and were together until
  Cooper's death in 1991. Bussee went on to publicly repudiate the ex-gay movement and apologize
  to those it harmed. *The men who built the machine were freed from it by each other.*
- **sources:** [Exodus International — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exodus_International) ·
  [SPLC, "Redeemed"](https://www.splcenter.org/resources/reports/redeemed/) ·
  [Truth Wins Out — the Michael Bussee story](https://archive.truthwinsout.org/pressrelease/2020/07/42141/) ·
  [The Daily Beast — therapists who turned against conversion therapy](https://www.thedailybeast.com/meet-the-conversion-therapists-who-turned-against-conversion-therapy/)

### N2 · Darlene Bogle
- **status:** documentary · **confidence:** medium-high (confirm the year and her exact title in a second source before shipping)
- **text (draft):** A leader in the Exodus network and founder/director of Paraklete Ministries,
  an Exodus referral agency in Hayward, CA. She fell in love with a woman who came to one of her
  own ex-gay meetings, and left ~1990. *The counselor and the counseled, on the same side of the
  table after all.*
- **sources:** [Ex-gay movement — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex-gay_movement) ·
  [SPLC, "Redeemed"](https://www.splcenter.org/resources/reports/redeemed/) ·
  [Newsweek — Ex-Ex-Gay Pride](https://www.newsweek.com/ex-ex-gay-pride-249282)

### N3 · John Smid — Love in Action (period-relevant to the piece)
- **status:** documentary · **confidence:** high
- **text (draft):** Executive director of Love in Action (from 1990; the Memphis ministry whose
  teen program the piece's era grammar draws on). He resigned in 2008, apologized in 2010 for the
  harm done — noting the youth program "further wounded teens" — later said conversion therapy does
  not work, came out, and in 2014 married Larry McQueen. *The director of the program renounced it,
  then married a man.*
- **sources:** [John Smid — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Smid) ·
  [Memphis Flyer — former Love in Action leader marries his same-sex partner](https://www.memphisflyer.com/former-love-in-action-leader-marries-his-same-sex-partner/) ·
  [Metro Weekly — former ex-gay leader](https://www.metroweekly.com/2019/01/former-ex-gay-leader-conversion-therapy-does-not-work-and-must-be-stopped/)

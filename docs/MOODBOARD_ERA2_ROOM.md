## Who Era 2 is

A different person from Era 1: an **adult man, early–mid 2000s**, inside the
**ex-gay support world**. Not a scared teen at dusk — a grown man who has
"chosen the program," lives alone in a controlled space, and is *managed*
rather than recruited. The room should feel like Era 1's warmth has been
**optimized away**: tidier, sparser, adult, a little airless.

## The era's targeting logic (from research — gives us the mechanics)

The ex-gay 2000s ran on **accountability**, and that becomes the era's
interface:
- **Confession/accountability software** — the period's defining mechanic: you
  log every "infraction," even a stray attraction. This is the witness side's
  dream: the person files *themselves*. (The Assistant lineage arrives here as
  an **accountability app** — there really was an "ex-gay app" Apple later
  pulled. ⚑ [VERIFY SOURCE: Christian Post / the app's name].)
- **12-step "sexual addiction" framing** — homosexuality reframed as addiction;
  a recovery-program UI (steps, sponsors, sobriety counters).
- **Behavioral-modification drills** — masculinity coaching, etc. (satire-
  permitted as perpetrator self-presentation, but never cruel toward the man).
- **The support group / forum** — the mIRC channel's grown-up successor: a
  monitored forum or webcam meeting where "strugglers" hold each other to
  account.

Witness side here = a **compliance dashboard**: streak counters, confession
logs, "accountability partner" notes, relapse flags. The horror upgrade from
Era 1: in Era 1 the system filed you; in Era 2 **you file yourself, daily.**

## Palette direction (to decide with Sérgio)

Era 1 = warm dusk juvenile. Era 2 proposal: **drain the warmth, add control.**
- cooler, desaturated — institutional off-white, grey-blue, fluorescent green
  CRT→LCD glow instead of cozy teal;
- one sickly warm accent (a lamp that's trying) so it isn't pure cold;
- the "tidier/emptier" rule: fewer props, everything squared-away, a wall
  calendar/checklist instead of posters.
- ⚑ Sérgio's call — this is a direction, not a decision.

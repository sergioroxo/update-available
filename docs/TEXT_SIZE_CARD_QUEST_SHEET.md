# The Quest reading test: one sheet (job 29 A8, 16 Nov 2026)

About 20 minutes, seated, before any more headset work. It answers one question first: can the text be read in the
headset now? (The bigger letters of 12 Nov have only been checked by calculation, never by eye.) From Fable's review
§1.1, steps 0 to 2.

**Before you start.** Quest in developer mode, plugged into the Mac by USB. On the Mac, open Chrome at
`chrome://inspect` and keep it open: if the headset shows black, the console there still shows what happened.

**1. The card (2 minutes).**
Open `sergioroxo.github.io/update-available/?reinterp=1&debug=1&textcard=1` and press Enter VR.
A dark card hangs one metre in front of you with the same sentence four times, smallest at the bottom, each row marked
with its size: 1.2°, 1.0°, 0.8°, 0.6°.
- Sit still and read down the card. **Write down the smallest row you can read without effort**, and the smallest you can
  read at all.
- For scale: the monitors' body text is now about 0.9 to 1.0°.

**2. The debug plate (step 0, 1 minute).**
Reload the address **without** `&textcard=1` and enter VR again. A small plate sits low on your left.
- Is the line marked `1997/03` drawn in a heavy, blocky face (not a thin typewriter one)? **Write: heavy or thin.**
- **Write down** the two numbers on the line that starts `eye` (the size the headset draws each eye) and the line
  `px/deg`.

**3. Read the 1997 monitor (step 1, about 5 minutes).**
Play 1997 as usual. From the seat, without leaning in, read the programme's text and an IRC message, then pick up
the handheld game and read it.
- **Write: yes, with effort, or no**, for the monitor and for the handheld.
- **If yes for both: stop here.** The layer work (about two weeks) is then not needed.

**4. The frame rate (step 2, about 10 minutes).**
Go on into 2003 (the film) and then 2016 (play Floppy Sheep for a minute). Watch the `fps` number on the plate.
- **Write down the lowest fps you see** in each, and whether it drops below 72.
- Also note how the era changes feel (the flight between the rooms): smooth, or juddering.

**Send back:** the five things in bold. Nothing else is needed.

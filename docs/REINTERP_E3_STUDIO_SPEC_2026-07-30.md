# ERA 3 — THE TESTIMONY STUDIO · build spec
*The E3 revamp made concrete. Grounded in `REINTERP_E3_REVAMP_BRIEF_2026-07-30.md` rev 3 and the
research it cites. Two devices: the **laptop** produces and circulates, the **phone** returns and
breaks the era.*

## ⚑ THE MECHANIC, in one line
**The studio is a machine that converts `felt` into `operable`.**

You feed it something true — Vera's own account — and it hands you presets that turn it into
something that circulates. The project's own register vocabulary becomes the tool. That is the era.

**Nothing forces you.** You can publish it raw. The platform simply does not carry it: three views,
no comments, the number sits there. Apply the presets and it moves. **The coercion is arithmetic, not
UI** — the player learns the ideology by watching what gets rewarded, and the piece never says a word
about it.

*Sérgio: "a very creative way of allowing the user to 'perform' the ideology without explicitly
doing it."* That is the design goal; everything below serves it.

---

## 1 · THE STUDIO (laptop)

A 2016 editing timeline with **three segments**, because the documented testimony grammar has exactly
three phases. Each segment carries preset controls, and **the controls are the codebook** (sourced —
see the brief's table):

| Segment | Preset name (invented, in-world) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · "Where I was"** | *Honest Light* | cools and desaturates the grade, tightens the frame, drops a minor-key pad under it, hunches the posture |
| **2 · "What happened"** | *The Turn* | warm key rises, lens flare, camera pulls back, minor→major modulation, posture opens |
| **3 · "Where I am now"** | *Flourishing* | golden hour, saturation up, frame widens to include others, major resolution, laughter under it |

The preset names must sound like **care, not craft** — the tell is that a colour-grade is called
*Honest Light*. Lambient marks one as **"Recommended for this segment."**

**⚑ THE TONE LAW HERE IS ABSOLUTE.** The satire lives in the TOOL. **Vera is sincere and is never the
joke.** Her raw account is `felt`: bare, unstyled, no music, no grade. The studio is `operable`: it
may charm. The horror is the conversion between them, and it must never read as mockery of her.

**Dismissal law:** every preset can be declined; declining is filed. Lambient's suggestion is an
offer, never a gate.

## 2 · PUBLISH — the documented call-to-action furniture (Node 5)
On publish, the platform appends what these videos really append: an end card, a link-in-bio prompt,
a QR code, hashtag overlays, and the line *"If I can change, you can too."*
⚑ **The real hashtags (#Changed, #OnceGay, #FreedomMarch) are dossier-only — the fiction uses
invented equivalents.**

**Publish is a commit-press**, and it should rhyme with S2R.3's *"i want to be with you too"* — one
button, no alternative, and the thing you press is what the record keeps.

## 3 · CIRCULATE (same device)
The feed: her testimony enters a stream beside others that look exactly like it — same three phases,
same presets, different faces (faceless, per the law). **Node 4's tell is visible here**: co-opted
therapy language in minimalist pastel self-help styling — *authentic self, freedom, healing,
unpacking my past.* The apparatus wearing recovery's clothes.

**Lambient's badges** (S57's dispersal payoff) do the trust work: *verified · sponsored · flagged.*
The watcher is no longer a character. It is a trust signal.

## 4 · THE ECHO CHAMBER — the loop closes
Her own video is recommended **to her**. The comments she reads are about **her**. The
*"you might also like"* row is full of people who sound like her, **because she taught them how**.

*Sérgio: "her producing, her reviewing others, but also being a victim… starts the conversation of
the Echo Chambers."* Rendered literally, not as metaphor.

## 5 · THE PHONE — the return, and the era's break
The phone is not a third screen; it is **where the era ends**.

The notification arrives. Then the DM. Then a comment that is *about her*, from someone using her own
words back at her. **The thing that closes the loop is the thing that breaks the time** — and the
glitch begins here, on the intimate device, because what comes next is ambient.

**⚑ Sérgio's E4 bridge, and it is the strongest structural idea in the era:**
> *"the next ERA is all around AI — the final transmutation of Lamby to Echo or just 'L'."*

So the phone's break is also **the name eroding**: Lamby → Lambient → **Echo / "L"**. The dispersal
that began at u3 finishes as a letter. The watcher does not grow louder; it loses its name and
becomes ambient — which is exactly the infrastructure spine's argument.

**And the presets survive the era.** Sérgio: this connects to *"the ERA-4 play of the AI image changer
from Trans to Cis / Gay to Straight."* E3's presets are applied **by hand, to yourself, one segment at
a time**. E4's are applied **automatically, to anyone, instantly.** Same operation; the labour is gone.
That is the cleanest statement of the whole four-era spine the piece has, and it costs nothing to set
up here — the phone just has to carry it forward.

## 6 · The activity tracker (demoted, per the evidence)
The GraceQueue survives as a **light tracker of what Vera owes** — not the era's centre. Peer digital
moderation is rated **contested** in the research, so it must not carry the era's weight. It never
empties; nobody remarks on it.

# ADDENDUM — the phone's apps, from Sérgio's Gemini games conversation
*Read after writing the spec, as he asked. The doc proposes three 2016-native mini-games chained as
stages of one "wellness program", driven by a system-bystander "Guide". **It fits the phone, and the
Guide is Lamby.***

## What it is, and where it goes
Three familiar 2016 mechanics, each subverted:
1. **"The Swivel"** — a Tinder swipe. You swipe right on what you love; the app locks right-swipe and
   forces you to reject your own friends and community. The Guide's hand overlays yours.
2. **"The Purge"** — a Fruit Ninja slice. You cut *Doubt* and *Anxiety*; then the bubbles become
   *Pride, Self-Love, Trans identity*, and letting one pass flashes SHAME and drops a Purity Score.
3. **"The Score"** — a Flappy Bird loop. Keep a "Conformity Level" inside a shrinking safe zone while
   the system introduces sabotage. On the inevitable crash: *"You didn't try hard enough. Pray harder."*

**These are PROVOTYPES, and the phone is their home** — which answers Q-D50 and D33 in one move:
Lamby offers them, they live as apps on a 2016 phone, and finding them is never rewarded.

## ⚑ Framing them honestly (this matters for the dossier)
The research rates purpose-built accountability apps and online quizzes as **thinly evidenced**. So
these must **not** claim to be reconstructions of real apps. They are provotypes — the project's own
form — and their debriefs cite the **mechanism**, which *is* documented:
- **rigged to fail** — Chambers's own estimate that ~99.9% experienced no change;
- **enforced isolation** — accountability partners and the monitoring of relationships, documented in
  ministry curricula;
- **thought-stopping** — the discipline of destroying "unwanted" thoughts, documented across programs.

*The form is invented; the mechanism is sourced.* That is exactly what a provotype is for.

## ⚑ ONE ETHICS FLAG — "The Purge" is the sharpest edge in the project
It has the player **actively destroying markers of queer identity**, rewarded for doing so. The
mechanic is powerful and the intent is clearly critical — but the piece's law is that satire targets
the apparatus and **never the victim**, and this is the one beat where the player's own hands perform
the violence.

It can work, but only with the frame doing real work: the *game* must be visibly the thing that
demands it, the SHAME banner must read as the apparatus's voice rather than the piece's, and the
collapse has to arrive. **This needs Sérgio's explicit ethics call before it is built** — it is a
G-gate item, not a design preference. *(My read: buildable, and the strongest of the three, but only
if "The Score" precedes it so the player already knows the system is rigged.)*

## The connection that makes this more than a minigame set
The Gemini draft's ending is the reveal that **the Guide was programmed from the first second to make
the player fail** — the facade peels, the code shows.

**That Guide is Lamby.** Which means these apps are not a side attraction: they are where the piece
can *show* what the dispersal did. And it lands directly on the E3→E4 seam Sérgio named — the
transmutation to **Echo / "L"** — because a companion revealed as a fail-loop is precisely what
"ambient care" becomes when it no longer needs a face.

**Sequencing note:** the games are stages of one program in the source doc. On the phone they become
apps you can open in any order — which is better for the piece's laws (no forced sequence, dismissal
always works) and keeps them provotypes rather than a level.

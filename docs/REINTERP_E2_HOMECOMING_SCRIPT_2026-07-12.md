# REINTERP E2 — THE HOMECOMING (beat script, R28-2d source of truth)

*Fable, 2026-07-12. The Era-2 narrative for the reinterp, built from Sérgio's homecoming law
(D15), the era mining (Caleb thread, Restorify spine, collapse composite — FIND #1), and the
R28 conducted grammar. ALL copy below is PLACEHOLDER-draft for the `operable` register and
BRIEF-ONLY for `felt` beats (Sérgio writes those). Ethics: the Caleb thread is new
intimacy-under-surveillance territory — THIS DOCUMENT is the read Sérgio gates it on (spec
§5 promise kept). Beat ids S2R.x; every beat carries `cuts` metadata at build time.*

**The era's argument:** Era 1's horror was that the system filed you. Era 2's is that *you
come back to it* — and file yourself, and call it care. The homecoming is real warmth
(time passed, the room aged, you survived the placement) and the era spends that warmth
on the machine.

## S2R.0 — THE RETURN *(felt · bare — brief only, no draft copy)*

Post-restart. The morph has aged Room 1: 2003 **daylight** through the window (the single
strongest time cue we own — E1 was lamp-lit night), the teen props gone or grayed, the bed
dust-sheeted… and any object KEPT in the belongings beat sitting exactly as it was left,
un-aged (the mixtape is the designed candidate). **The monitor is OFF. Nothing speaks. No
caption, no hint, no music.** The player can turn, look, move to the seat. The silence
holds until the player acts.

**The beat's one mechanic — the power press, relocated from E1 (resolves the D22 spirit):**
in '97 the machine booted FOR you (a system made for you). In '03, **Daniel turns it on
himself.** The first act of the era is voluntary return. Nothing marks this as a choice —
no prompt, no glow (the E1 guidance era is over; the player knows this verb). The horror
arrives retroactively: he came home and plugged himself back in.

## S2R.1 — LAMBY *(operable · the conductor debuts — ≤2 lines per beat, law)*

Boot → a brief "Restorify — finishing installation…" (the update completed while the room
slept) → **Lamby appears.** First conduction beat, exactly two lines (PLACEHOLDER):

> "Welcome back, Daniel. We kept your room ready."
> "Shall we begin your Daily Realignment?"

Dismissal works and files (`assistant: dismissed — first greeting`). If dismissed, Lamby

waits. (It can afford to. That's the point.)

## S2R.2 — THE CHECK-IN *(operable)*

Restorify opens on the **purity streak — it counted while he was away.** The number is
large (the placement's days were "clean" days: the institution logged them on his behalf —
one quiet line in the UI says so, PLACEHOLDER: *"412 days · includes supervised period"*).
The Daily Realignment asks "How is your walk today?" — chip selection, every answer
accepted, each tagged differently on the witness side (choices REGISTER, never branch).

## S2R.3 — CALEB *(felt for the contact · operable for the intrusion · the era's wound)*

Adapted whole from the shipped design (mining FIND #1) — the love interest, Sérgio-toned:

- A chat window (period messenger) pings: **Caleb** — the fellow "struggler" from the
  placement; they held hands at the retreat. The conversation is warm, specific, alive.
  *(felt: Lamby is ABSENT from this window; no satire; Sérgio writes these lines — the
  brief is: two people who survived the same thing, planning to meet, neither saying the
  bigger word yet.)* Player advances via reply-chips (register-not-branch).
- Mid-warmth: **Lamby's ACCOUNTABILITY ALERT** fires *(operable)* — the chat is redacted
  LIVE, line by line: `Content Blocked Until Further Evaluation`. Flag rendered:
  `HOMOSEXUAL CONDUCT`. **The streak dies on screen: 412 → 0.** PLACEHOLDER for the two
  system lines (imported, Sérgio's own prior wording): *"for no one special"* / *"those
  days were for the wife you'll have when you're well — not for him."*
- Lamby offers comfort *(operable, ≤2 lines)*: the apparatus consoles you for the wound it
  just made. PLACEHOLDER: "That was close, Daniel. I caught it in time." — filed as care.

Witness side across the beat: the chat filed as `contact: flagged`, the redaction as
`intervention: successful`, the comfort as `support: provided`. The record's cheerfulness
about it is the collapse-material later.

## S2R.5 — THE COLLAPSE *(operable → felt)*

Trigger grammar (documented failures, never the player): a **PureMail** envelope arrives —
subject: *"We have to stop."* The composite apology (the perpetrators-as-fellow-victims
draft from ERA2_RESEARCH, already Sérgio-steered) reads in full. While it's open:
- the jingle returns **broken** (the degraded render — slow, detuned, dying music box);
- the streak field glitches: `0 days` → `— days` → *"412 days · for nothing"*;
- Lamby's window sits empty-eyed, saying nothing (the inner watcher losing its outer god).
Then **Caleb's block lifts** — the redacted chat un-redacts line by line (the same
mechanism, reversed), and one new message arrives *(felt — Sérgio's line; the shipped
draft's shape: "they shut it down… none of us were ever broken")*. The person the system
severed is the one who frees him.

## S2R.6 — THE RESIDUE *(respite → felt)*

The quiet after. One truth surfaces and is COMMITTED by the player (chip-press, not typed —
input law), the era's diary-echo: **"Then it was never me that was broken…"** (locked line,
canon). The witness side files nothing here — or rather: it shows a gap it could not
classify. The mixtape (if kept) is playable in this beat; the system says nothing about it.

## S2R.7 — THE SEND *(operable · E2→E3)*

The u3 update ritual (notification → EULA → changelog → restart; "Remind me later" works
once, and the belongings beat triggers again — what do you take from THIS life?). The
changelog migrates the collapsed program into the platform era, and **the restart delivers
the player to Room 2** — Vera's era begins where Daniel's ends. The last thing filed under
Daniel's name: `subject migrated — file retained`.

## F1 · The witness board is de-emphasized; the assistants become the connective tissue (overrides ◆1)
From Sérgio's own trials: the witness board fails to hold viewer attention. All eras are restructured to
take advantage of the **global space**, with storylines that **share and transfer between clusters** —
e.g. Era 3 leads with the lesbian room but sends the player into the trans-man alcove for a challenge,
because the attacks share infrastructure and influence. The witness's narrative work migrates into the
**assistant lineage (Lamby → Aski → Sol → Ami™ / Lambient / Echo), which now lives partly OUTSIDE the
computer** — the same system escorting the player through time and space across all four victims, so
guidance itself dramatizes the coercion. **Constraint carried forward, not dropped:** Ethics #10 (every
witness line traceable to the player's own act) must be preserved in whatever surface inherits the
board's work — the assistants may present the record, but it still computes only from acts.
**Gap this opens:** the assistant law "≤5 lines per stage; absent Stages 0–1; never during `felt`" needs a
deliberate revision if the assistant becomes the guide — flag for Sérgio when the guide design is drafted.

## F2 · The radial mechanic's theoretical anchor: perspective-taking
Gaze/turn only, confirmed (◆2 stands). The lit node is *creatively* highlighted (intelligent lighting that
shares light with neighbours without clipping them — not a brightness slider, matching the deep-research
rule). The cluster is framed as **perspective-taking apparatus** — Imagine-Self vs Imagine-Other (Van Loon
et al., 2018 `[VERIFY SOURCE]` — verify citation + the VR-empathy critique literature in the deep-research
pass so the claim is defensible in the PhD context).
**CORRECTED (Round 2, deep-research verified):** the citation is real — van Loon, Bailenson, Zaki, Bostick
& Willer 2018, *PLOS ONE* 13(8) — but narrower than the shorthand: it found **target-specific cognitive
empathy** (only toward the person whose perspective was taken, moderated by presence, **no effect on
behaviour**), and the Imagine-Self/Imagine-Other terminology is NOT from that paper (it belongs to the
broader literature; Sora-Domenjó's "Disrupting the empathy machine" notes imagine-self can raise personal
*distress*). **Defensible PhD framing: VR as situated, partial, target-specific perspective-taking and
embodied witnessing — not an empathy machine.** Use that phrasing in any academic text.

## F3 · Two cuts of the piece: FESTIVAL and FULL (new, structural)

guidance is ALWAYS present in both; the guide is the system, per F1). Consequence for the roadmap: every
content item from R1 onward carries a placement tag `side-quest | festival-cut | full-cut` (wired into R3's
audit register and the provotype schema). Cheap now, expensive to retrofit.

## §3 The tapes (D17/D18 — the multi-step audio side quest)

Physical tape props on the bookcase shelf + the boombox as the player-facing player (both
placed by the fix batch). Playing tapes is OPTIONAL — Sérgio's reduce/expand lever. Three
tapes, three different narrative jobs:

- **Tape A — the Companion Tape (system voice).** The kit's official cassette: welcome,
  program instructions read warmly, and the **singing prayer** as its final segment — the
  emotional peak of the tape. G1 gate: prayer wording and framing are Sérgio's alone; the
  spec only reserves the slot. Filed when played ("companion tape: completed").
- **Tape B — the broadcast tape (period publicity).** A compilation styled as taped-off-the-
  radio: conversion-program adverts and testimony snippets of the era. Every claim styled on
  documented material — `[VERIFY SOURCE]` against the KB before scripting (Love Won Out-era
  advertising language is the grounding candidate). Filed when played. This is the
  educational payload tape.
- **Tape C — Daniel's mixtape (the resisting element — already canon).** His own music, his
  life before. NOT a system object: it never files, the system never mentions it, and it is
  the mixtape that resists the graying task (locked canon). Playing it is pure respite-adjacent
  warmth. It is also the obvious thing to KEEP in the belongings beat (§4).
- **Audio behavior:** insert → play/stop only (click/tap law). Segments separated by tape
  hiss/clunk transitions; playback continues ambiently; no scrubbing, no progress UI. Sound
  design: 90s cassette character (wow/flutter, hiss floor) — the warm side's audio is soft;
  contrast with the system's clean beeps.
- **No real songs, hymns, or ministry recordings** (copyright + no-real-orgs law): all audio
  is ORIGINAL, generated via Sérgio's Suno/Sonauto pipeline. **Prompt drafts in Appendix A —
  Sérgio runs these** (no audio exists yet; nothing is blocked on it — the build lane ships
  with placeholder hiss until the files land).

# ERA 3 — VERA HAS A JOB, NOT A LIST
*Sérgio, 2026-08-03: "since we are making a VR piece focused on the digital experience of SOGICE,
wouldn't it make sense that in this era Vera was like a social media manager for one of the
organisations? … filters, read comments, see the videos, upload shorts, podcasts, a series of videos
for a course … more dynamic and fun, without being so text based, and showcase how they are using
media in so many different ways."*

## ⚑ The answer, and it is not a redesign

**She already is one. The correction list is one task in that job — the morning queue.**

Look at what the shipped list actually does: correction 3 clips for the **podcast**, 4 attaches the
**funnel**, 5 pushes to the **app**, 6 pairs with a **partner**, 7 routes for **mentorship**.
**Six named channels are already in the data — and she never operates any of them.** She *tags* them.
A chip appears, and the channel it names is off-screen forever.

**That is the gap he found**, and it is exactly the right one. The fix is not to replace the list —
which took four passes to get right and works — it is to build **the rest of her workday around it**,
so the channels the list keeps naming become places she actually goes.

**E3 becomes a desk with a job.** The laptop stops being one app.

⚑ **And "fun" is not a problem here — it is the mechanic.** The register law already says `operable`
surfaces *may glitter, charm, play*. A social-media dashboard that is genuinely satisfying to use is
the correct design: **the horror is that the work is pleasant.** What must never happen is the
*piece* playing — no scores, no achievements in its own voice. The tool is delighted; the piece is not.

ephemeral channel is thematically free money.

## The surfaces — one workday, each grounded in a documented form

**Each one must have a verb that is not reading.** That is his actual complaint about E3 and it is
correct: E1 has objects, E2 has the infomercial, E3 has paragraphs.

### 1 · ⚑ THE COMMENTS — the highest-value surface, and I would build this first
This is where the era's key beat stops being a checkbox. **"Route for mentorship" is currently a
button. In a comment thread it becomes a conversation.**

Someone posts something vulnerable under a testimony. Vera's job is to reply — and she does not write
anything, because **there are pinned templates.** She picks one. Then she flags the account for
follow-up.

**That is the recruitment floor, live**, and it does the era's thesis with a mouse instead of a
paragraph: *someone said they were struggling, and the system's response was to send a person.*

⚑ **ETHICS — this is the most dangerous surface in the era.** The commenter is a real vulnerable
person in the fiction: they are `felt`, they are sincere, they are **never** the joke, and the piece
never satirises them. The satire is entirely in the template picker — the little grid of warm
pre-written kindnesses with a keyboard shortcut. **If the vulnerable commenter is ever funny, the
beat is wrong.**

### 2 · THE STORY (not a Short) — the vertical, and it expires
She cuts a vertical clip from a longer testimony. **The mechanic is choosing the in-point** — drag
the handles, pick the forty seconds.

And the ideology needs no commentary: **the forty seconds that performs is never the true part.**
She is not lying. She is choosing where to start.

*(This is where correction 3's "clip for the broadcast" chip finally goes somewhere.)*

### 3 · THE PODCAST — the clips accumulate
Cheap, because it reuses what exists: every "clip for the broadcast" she applies drops into an
episode. The mechanic is **ordering them** — drag three clips into a running order and publish.

The tell is in the arithmetic of it: an episode is other people's worst minutes, arranged well.

### 4 · THE COURSE — the funnel becomes literal
Documented: webinars and **paid** online courses. She builds a module list. **And it has a price**,
and a payment link, and the link is the same one correction 4 appends to strangers' stories.

*The funnel stops being a metaphor at the moment you see the number.*

### 5 · THE LIVESTREAM — ambient, not a task
Running in a corner of the desktop all era. Nobody asks her to watch it. It is simply always on,
which is the point.

### 6 · THE FILTERS — already built, and they generalise
S69 shipped the preset on Noa's video. **The same grade applied to a Story is one line of reuse**,
and it is the E4 bridge: by hand today, automatic later.

## ⚑ The one collision with a shipped decision — metrics

The correction-list spec says, deliberately: *"No metrics, no threats, no view counts… it says
`3 of 7 applied`."* That was a real decision — an earlier draft had a view count refusing to move and
Sérgio correctly called it contrived.

**A social-media job with no analytics anywhere would be false.** So the reconciliation, and it keeps
the decision intact:

> **Numbers may EXIST. Nothing may GATE on them.** No task requires a number to rise. Nobody remarks
> on a number. She is never asked to improve one. They sit there, the way they sit there in any real
> job, and the work is measured in items completed — *because a checklist is how you make a person do
> something without ever telling them to.*

If a build session finds itself using a metric as pressure, it has re-broken the thing that took four
passes to fix.

---

## What this costs, and what it must not cost

**Keeps, untouched:** the correction list and its seven items · the script/scripture doubling · Noa's
two contradictory corrections and the fact that nothing resolves her · Malta and the light · the
`n of 7` coercion · Lambient as the software's voice.

**Adds:** five surfaces, of which one (the comments) carries most of the value.

⚑ **The risk, named:** a suite of mini-apps can become a games compilation. The guard is that
**every surface must be part of one job with one queue** — she has things she owes, and they are on
different screens. If a surface has no owed task attached to it, it is decoration and should be cut.

**Sequencing, my read:** comments first, on its own. It is the highest value, it upgrades the beat the
era already turns on, and it is the one with the ethics gate — it deserves a session, not a slot in a
suite.

# Housekeeping — the doorplates
> *"I am not a big fan of the doorplates btw."*

**Cut them, and there is a good reason beyond taste: S66 removed their job.**

The plates existed to tell you whose room you were looking at. **They were a caption for rooms that
had no identity** — which was true when they were specced, and stopped being true four days later
when Room 2 got a lamp, a cardigan, eighteen book spines and a December 2016 calendar.

**You should recognise a room by its things, not by a label on it.** A piece whose whole argument is
*look at what is behind you* should not need to write names on it. The plates are the weakest kind of
solution — the one that explains — and the piece's own law is that it never explains.

*(The choreography itself stays. It is the plates that go, and S67's other findings — the E4 witness
record landing outside Room 3, the hijack guard, the draw-call ceiling — stand.)*

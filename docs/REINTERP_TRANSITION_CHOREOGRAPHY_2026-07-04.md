# TRANSITION CHOREOGRAPHY — the three updates + the Close, staged (v1)

*2026-07-04 · Fable 5 (design author). The creative design of the piece's signature 3D moments: what
the player sees, hears, and LOSES at each era transition. The mechanics exist (Session 8's
`morphToEra()` seam, rig crossfades, scrim lift; Session 9's three-room space); this doc is the
dramaturgy those mechanics perform. Ritual grammar is canon and untouched: notification ("Remind me
later" works once) → EULA (scroll, one live "I Agree") → install (changelog-as-thesis + glitch) →
restart — always triggered by a documented system failure, never by the player. Beat ids per the
[master script](REINTERP_NARRATIVE_MASTER_SCRIPT_2026-07-04.md). All copy PLACEHOLDER. All sound =
asset directions for Sérgio/Sonauto, no runtime synthesis.*

**The governing rule (style doc §1): each transition is one round of the cold light winning.** The
player should be able to describe every transition as "the room got [brighter/flatter/darker], and
[one warm thing] didn't survive it." What is lost is chosen, named here, and never replaced.

---

## T1 · E1→E2, 1997→2003 — "the network arrives" (the signature moment)

**Trigger (e1.b09):** the diary-deletion failure cascades into "a new update is needed" (shipped
`s1_end.json` grammar — the glitch covers the change).

**Choreography (≈45s after Restart):**
1. **Hold (3s).** Every light drops except the lamp. The room the player has lived in for an hour
   exists one last time as: lamp pool, moon, dark. The monitor reboots alone.
2. **Ballast stage-lighting (8s).** The E2 fluorescent rig arrives in three audible *stages* — clunk,
   flicker, hold — like office lights, not a sunrise (rig crossfade ✅, retimed stepped).
3. **The window turns off (2s).** Moon and night-blue pane swap to a flat daylight gray (V2 prop swap).
   The moon is not seen again in the piece.
4. **The WALLS LEAVE — the wedge cluster arrives (Round 22 revision — built S12, per Sérgio's
   drawing; supersedes S11's piers).** The shipped morph effect, ported: the east/west walls vanish
   under the glitch, the window wall slides back, the spine recedes with the door and the record
   terminal — and the space resolves as a HEXAGON aligned to three 120° facings, hub = the chair.
   Every 120° turn now faces a perpendicular back wall: a whole room in front of you (front = the
   lead room, Daniel's furniture re-sorted forward; west = the parallel-tracks bay; east = the
   trans-facet room with the niche). FURNITURE is the limiter between rooms — a bookcase run and
   chest on the front-west boundary, a wardrobe-closet and dresser on the front-east, low shelves
   flanking the rear sight channel (door + terminal, kept clear for E4). Props cascade in
   prop-by-prop (diagonal sweep, soft glitch — scale jitter + emissive flicker, no strobe). The
   zone lights come up at 4.2s, *before* the lead room's rig settles — the other rooms were ready
   first. That order is the thesis.
5. **The guide transforms (≤10s, on the monitor):** Lambert's paper texture pixelates into Lamby's
   bounce (OP-2 sprite beat). First line only after the room settles.
6. **The desk has already changed** (during the dark hold): CRT stays (2003), but kit brochure →
   Restorify box; soda can → coffee mug; homework pile → job folder. Daniel aged six years in three
   seconds of dark — the cheapest cut in cinema, staged in a room.

**LOST at T1:** the moon · the boombox (packed away; **the mixtape survives**, moved to the shelf,
still un-grayed) · the room being *only his*.
**PERSISTS:** the lamp (same object, forever) · the diary (drawer — the system failed to delete it) ·
the mixtape.
**Sound:** dial-up handshake stretched and pitched down under the install bar; the three ballast
clunks; then the new room tone = fluorescent hum where the crickets were.
**VR/browser:** identical (REV-6) — no camera move; the light does everything. Conducted mode may add
the O7-style half-tilt when the ceiling witness re-arms.

## T2 · E2→E3, 2003→2016 — "the rebrand" (the collapse that changes nothing)

**Trigger (e2.b07):** the flagship ministry dissolves publicly (Exodus-collapse analog, documented).
The notification is not an apology; it is a **data-migration consent**: "TriedPath is sunsetting.
Your care history will be transferred to our partner network." The EULA scroll IS the transfer manifest
— the player scrolls through their own E1+E2 terminal lines re-labeled as "assets."

**Choreography (≈40s):**
1. **The boxes appear first** (during install, behind the player): Daniel's props — posters, tape
   deck, the Restorify box — repack into moving boxes that slide into the WEST bay. The player watching
   the install bar hears cardboard behind them; the turn shows the lead room already half his no more.
2. **Restart: pastel wash (6s).** The E3 rig blooms — near-shadowless, pinkish-neutral (✅ rig). No
   stages this time; platforms don't clunk, they *fade in*. The lamp survives but now reads
   old-fashioned — a leftover in lifestyle lighting.
3. **The desk re-owners:** CRT → wedge laptop + phone + ring of small glows (CANVAS_BY_ERA precedent;
   V2 swap). Vera's props arrive: plant, chart printout tube, queue headset.
4. **Daniel's file closes on the terminal, on screen, unprompted:** "case resolved — transferred."
   No scene, no goodbye — the player learns E3's first fact from the RECORD, not the room (terminal
   doc §W4). His boxes stay visible in the west bay all era.
5. **Lamby → Lambient** (sprite beat: the mouth smooths away).

**LOST at T2:** Daniel — as a person; he continues only as paperwork (the unresolvedness is the
point) · shadows (the E3 rig casts almost none) · the era of machines that *look like* machines.
**PERSISTS:** the lamp · the mixtape (now in a box in the west bay — visible through the aperture,
still warm-colored, still resisting) · the terminal (it never resets; it only grows — §W4).
**Sound:** an MSN-era sign-out chime, decaying and time-stretched under the transfer bar; then the
new room tone = notification pings so soft they read as rain.

## T3 · E3→E4, 2016→now — "the dissolve" + THE TURN (◆N3)

**Trigger (e3.b08):** the queue rejects the one line Vera won't polish; the platform "upgrades to
continuous care" — the rooms stop being destinations because the feed comes to you.

**Choreography (≈50s):**
1. **The install is silent.** No bar. The changelog self-scrolls (ambient AI doesn't ask). "Remind me
   later" still works once — the last time the piece honors it.
2. **Lights go out by category** (4 steps, 2s each): first the window (gone entirely — blank wall or
   drawn blind, V2), then the room fill, then the pastel, then the screens *except one*: the east
   niche flips FORWARD (geometry doc §3.2) — its glow becomes the brightest thing.
3. **THE TURN (◆N3, needs Sérgio's yes):** the restart re-anchors the home facing 180°. In VR: the one
   bodily ask, spent at the piece's heaviest hinge. In browser: a conducted slow 180° pan (auto-cam)
   or the R-reset now homing to the new facing. The player ends facing the former witness side —
   **Maya's interface-lit desk sits on the south spine beside the witness terminal.** The person and
   the record share a wall for the whole final act.
4. **The old front room stays behind you** — silhouetted, fog-tier, the lamp still on back there,
   visible whenever Maya's era makes you turn: the whole piece's history as a dark room you came from.
   Then the lamp is *carried*: one authored beat — L's arrival line plays while the lamp light
   travels (off at the old desk, on at Maya's) — nobody turned it off in 30 years (style doc §2-E4).
5. **Lambient → "L"** (name LOCKED, Round 20): the blob thins to a single letterform that slides OFF
   the screen into the room. L is never on-screen again; its lines arrive wherever the player is
   looking.

**LOST at T3:** the window (daylight never returns) · the desk as a *place you go* (the interface is
ambient now) · Lambient's face — the apparatus stops having a body you can look at.
**PERSISTS:** the lamp (moved, final warm pocket) · the terminal (now beside the lead machine — §W4) ·
the mixtape (Maya has it; how she has it = one line of Sérgio's, the piece's quietest connective).
**Sound:** a swarm of phone vibrations converging to a single soft chord, then near-silence: fan hum,
the first era with no room tone.

## T4 · the Close — "restart as you are" (the inversion; not a ritual)

No notification, no EULA — the one transition the SYSTEM does not run. On Maya's "Restart as you are.":
1. **The terminal gives up its lines** (x.b1, build item): the record's text lifts off the wall,
   line by line, each becoming a warm point that drifts upward — the player's own acts are the first
   stars. (Mechanically: terminal fades as the cloud fades in; the literal line-to-node animation is
   a polish pass, not a blocker.)
2. **The room exhales:** close rig (✅) takes everything to near-black EXCEPT the lamp, which holds
   3 full seconds after every other light — the last thing the room ever says — then hands its
   temperature to the constellation (the nodes are its hue; style doc: the warm light winning at
   cosmic scale).
3. **The cloud (✅ S8/S9):** night-blue sky, warm nodes, cool web, the knowledge-network labels.
   Slow drift. No guide. No UI. The unfinished line (x.b3) renders when Sérgio writes it.
**Sound:** the fan hum resolving into a sustained warm chord (Sonauto); first true silence underneath.

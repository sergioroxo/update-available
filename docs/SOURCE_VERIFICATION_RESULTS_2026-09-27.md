## e4_offers#2 · the campaign's argument (LGBAllience.md) — *confirmed, attribute carefully*
**What the run found:** both communications are real. (1) **LGB Alliance's letter to all MPs, 30 March 2021** — a
public primary document: *"Affirming a gender non-conforming child as trans, who would otherwise grow up to be
lesbian, gay or bisexual, is itself a form of conversion therapy."* (2) **An email to clergy, 2 February 2022**,
subject *"Transing away the gay"*, asking recipients to reconsider supporting the ban — reported by VICE (10 Feb
2022), which saw the emails; no public copy of the email itself exists. Both explicitly opposed including gender
identity in the ban. **The "far-right" clause needs attribution:** GPAHE (a civil-society monitor) included **LGB
Alliance Australia and LGB Alliance Ireland** in its far-right hate and extremism reports and classified their
ideology as "Anti-Transgender"; no official designation of the UK group exists; the groups dispute it. **The
research clause** should target the causal premise precisely (see the proposal).

**Proposed text** (the card has never named the campaign; naming it is now documented — **his choice**):
> An organised lobbying campaign argued that affirming a gender-nonconforming or gender-dysphoric child as trans
> is itself a form of conversion therapy — that such children would otherwise grow up lesbian, gay or bisexual.
> The wording is the campaign's own: in a letter to all MPs (March 2021), and in a stronger email to clergy who had
> backed a conversion-therapy ban (February 2022, reported by VICE, which saw it). Both opposed including gender
> identity in the ban. No credible evidence establishes the causal premise — that affirming a child turns someone
> who would otherwise be lesbian, gay or bisexual into a trans person. A civil-society extremism monitor has
> classified two of the campaign's overseas affiliates as anti-transgender in its reports on far-right hate groups;
> they dispute it. This is speech by a named actor with a documented aim, and the piece treats it as such. It is
> not the gender-exploratory clinical debate, on which the piece holds no position and renders both readings
> unresolved.

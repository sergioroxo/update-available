# ERA 3 (2016) — the revamp brief
*Sérgio: "This needs quite a revamp. The other Eras feel way more applicable and this one not really…
after the amazing work done with the infomercial, I know there's a lot more you can bring here."
Keep: the devices, Lamby, the digital influence of SOGICE.*

## Why E3 feels weaker — a diagnosis before a proposal

E1 and E2 each have a **native media form** that carries the argument by itself:
- **1997** — the mailed kit, the cassette, the booklet. *The apparatus is a place and a package.*
- **2003** — the DRTV infomercial. *The apparatus is a product you are sold.*
- **2016** — currently: "a platform." That is a **category, not a form.** It has a moderation queue
  and three screens, but no single object that argues the way an infomercial argues.

**That is the gap.** E3 needs its own form, as specific as a 1-800 number.

## ⚑ The proposal: in 2016 the apparatus stopped being a service you receive and became a community you STAFF

This is the era's thesis, and it is historically grounded rather than invented:

- **Restored Hope Network** (2012–) was assembled largely from former Exodus members *specifically to
  continue after Exodus closed* — the project's KB already carries "RHN HOPE conference 2016."
- **"Changed" / the Changed Movement** (Elizabeth Woning, Ken Williams) is the purest platform-native
  successor: not a clinic but a **testimony network** — personal stories, a book, hashtags, shareable
  video. The service *is* the content, and the content is produced by the people it worked on.
- **Alan Chambers's own 2016 framing is the counter-quote:** Exodus found it could not modify or
  rebrand its mission and closed instead — while its successors did precisely that. The apparatus's
  own leader saying rebranding was impossible, next to a movement built entirely on rebranding.
- GPAHE's finding fits exactly: decentralised affiliates, softened language, **testimony as the
  distribution mechanism**.

**So Era 3's horror is not being watched. It is being ENLISTED.**
E2: you open the program. **E3: the program opens you — and puts you to work.**

Vera is already a **moderator**. That is the right instinct and the piece hasn't cashed it in yet:
*the apparatus runs on the unpaid labour of the people it damaged.* She reviews the content that
would have helped her, and files it under guidelines she didn't write.

## The native form: TESTIMONY AS CONTENT

The 2016 object, as specific as the infomercial was for 2003:

**A testimony video with an engagement dashboard beside it.**

Not a broadcast — a *post*. It has a view count, a watch-time graph, comments, a share button, and a
small green "this helped 41 people" figure. And the person testifying is a former participant who is
now, effectively, staff.

Why this is the right form:
- **It is the era.** 2016 is when confession became content and content became recruitment.
- **It collapses on contact with the metrics.** The satire lives in the dashboard's cheerfulness —
  and dies when you see what is being counted.
- **It gives Vera something to DO that is horrifying without being violent:** she approves it. Or
  she doesn't, and the queue refills.

## Concrete beats to build (drafts — Sérgio's edit wins)

1. **THE QUEUE THAT NEVER EMPTIES.** GraceQueue exists; make its *arithmetic* legible. Every item
   cleared adds one. The number beside "remaining" never goes down. Nobody remarks on it.

2. **⚑ THE TESTIMONY PLAYER + DASHBOARD** — the era's centrepiece, the E3 equivalent of the
   infomercial. A vlog-style testimony (faceless, per the law) playing beside live engagement:
   watch-time retention curve, "41 people found this helpful," a comment stream that Vera can
   approve or hide. **The dashboard is the satire; the testimony is not.** The person testifying is
   sincere. That is what makes it unbearable.

3. **"WHAT'S YOUR SIDE?" — the assessment as a personality quiz.** The KB already carries the
   *Sides A/B/X/Y chart 2016* — a real framework categorising positions on faith and sexuality.
   In 2016 that becomes a shareable quiz with a result card. **Register-not-branch:** the result is
   the same whatever you press; only the filing changes. The quiz is E3's answer to E1's intake
   questionnaire — same instrument, thirty years of interface design later, and now you share it.

4. **COMMUNITY GUIDELINES.** A document you must accept to keep moderating, rewritten quietly
   between eras. The softening changelog D5 already names ("change" → "calling") belongs here as a
   *diff view*: two versions side by side, one word changed, no explanation.

5. **THE RECOMMENDATION.** After a testimony ends: *"You might also like…"* — three more. This is
   GPAHE's algorithmic-amplification finding rendered as one UI element. It costs almost nothing and
   it is the single clearest statement of how the era works.

6. **LAMBIENT'S BADGES.** Already built and already earned — S57's dispersal put them there. Now
   give them a job: they mark which items are *sponsored*, or *verified*, or *flagged*. The watcher
   is no longer a character; it is a trust signal.

## What this keeps (his three)

- **Lamby** — as **Lambient**, no longer a face but a badge. His absence as a character IS the era.

# ✅ REVISION 2 — Sérgio's pass (2026-07-30)
*His notes reframe this. Three changes matter: Vera becomes an **influencer**, not just a moderator;
the era's shape becomes an **echo chamber**; and the three devices become a **sequence**, not a set.*

## ⚑ 1. Vera PRODUCES — and that fixes the execution worry
> *"if Testimony became the logic, shouldn't we explore this more directly, she can also be a
> conversion 'influencer'?"* — and on the testimony player: *"quite unsure of how this lands… the
> logic sounds super interesting, but the execution can be limited."*

**He is right on both counts, and they are the same problem.** A testimony *player* is something you
watch — passive, and thin after ninety seconds. A testimony **studio** is something you *make*.

So: **Vera is a conversion influencer.** She records, edits, titles, thumbnails, publishes. The
player becomes a studio, and the era's central verb changes from *review* to **produce**.

That is also the sharper historical claim. The Changed Movement's whole architecture is
testimony-as-content made by former participants — **the apparatus's most effective recruiter is
someone it already worked on**, and she is not paid, and she believes it.

## ⚑ 2. The echo chamber — his best note, and it becomes the era's structure
> *"her producing, her reviewing others, but also being a victim — in a way starts the conversation
> of the Echo Chambers"*

Three layers at once, and they close into a loop:

**SHE MAKES IT → IT CIRCULATES → IT COMES BACK TO HER.**

The testimony she published is recommended *to her*. The comments she moderates are about *her* video.
The "you might also like" row on her own feed is full of people who sound exactly like her — because
she taught them how to sound. **She is the audience for her own recruitment.**

That is not a metaphor for an echo chamber; it is the mechanism, literally rendered. And it lands the
era's thesis without a word of explanation: *the apparatus no longer needs to reach you, because you
are producing it.*

## ⚑ 3. The three devices become a SEQUENCE — background → mechanism
> *"sequential jumping from one device to device while exploring this evolution from background to
> mechanism… we need more here, and to be versatile for the user logic"*

The three-screen room already exists (S37). Give each screen a **stage of the loop**, so moving
between them *is* the argument:

| Device | Stage | What you do | Where the apparatus sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Laptop** | **PRODUCE** | record / edit / title / publish a testimony | a tool she uses — background |
| **Tablet** | **CIRCULATE** | the feed: her video among others; moderate the queue | a place she lives — ambient |
| **Phone** | **RETURN** | the notification, the DM, the comment about her | it reaches her — mechanism |

**The player chooses the order**, which is the versatility he asked for — but every path closes the
loop, because the phone always brings it back. *You cannot stand outside a thing you are making.*

# 2 · THE LGB-ANTI-TRANS SPLIT — sourced, with a distinction the piece must not blur

## What is documented
- **The phrase is theirs.** *"Transing away the gay"* was the **subject line of an LGB Alliance email
  to clergy**, asking them to reconsider supporting the UK government's conversion-therapy ban.
- **The argument, as they made it:** that affirming a child with gender dysphoria — who would
  otherwise "simply grow up to be lesbian, gay or bisexual" — is *itself* a cruel form of conversion
  therapy.
- **The purpose is documented:** opposing the inclusion of trans people in conversion-practice bans.
- **The claim is not supported.** The idea that gay people are being systematically pressured into
  identifying as trans is not backed by credible research.
- **GPAHE classified LGB Alliance Ireland as a far-right anti-transgender hate group (August 2022)** —
  and GPAHE is already a source this project uses for the E3 material.

## ⚑ The distinction, and getting it wrong would be the era's worst error
CLAUDE.md says: *never satirise the gender-exploratory clinical debate — render BOTH captions,
unresolved.* **That law is about a clinical debate. This is not that.**

| | how the piece must treat it |
|---|---|
| **The gender-exploratory clinical question** | both captions, unresolved, no satire, the piece has no position |
| **⚑ A lobbying campaign with a named author, a documented purpose and an unsupported premise** | **attributed and sourced.** It is speech by an actor, not an open question |

**Collapsing the second into the first would give a lobbying position the epistemic protection the
piece reserves for a genuine clinical uncertainty.** That is not neutrality; it is a category error,
and it would be the most consequential mistake Era 4 could make.

**So in the build:** L recommends "people like you" and the voices say this. **The target is L's
curation** — the apparatus selecting precisely these voices for Maya. The speakers are not the
target, the counter-beat is required, and the Dossier carries the attribution. `documentary` for the
campaign and its authorship; the claim itself is rendered as *their speech*, never as a question the
piece is holding open.

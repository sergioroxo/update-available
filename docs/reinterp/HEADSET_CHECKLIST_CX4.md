# CX4 — seated Quest 3 check (25 October 2026)

For Sérgio, in Meta Browser, over trusted HTTPS. Use the live build: https://sergioroxo.github.io/update-available/?reinterp=1 (open it in a fresh tab so the headset does not show a cached copy).
The changes have been exercised with a mocked XR manager in headless Chrome. That is a mono picture, not a Quest compositor or a comfort test. Record each item as OK / FAIL / not tried, with the era and the thing being pressed. The previous checklist's flights and driven turns are superseded in immersive XR by the pixel dissolve below. Browser flights remain.

1. **Set your seat.** Enter VR seated naturally. The plain plate should say “Sit comfortably — press to set your seat”. Press once. Through a two-second field of square night-blue, warm and cool pixels, the room should settle with its monitor naturally in front of you. That press must not also start a programme. Lean left/right, up/down and forward/back: your own head must move freely in all six directions. Turn your head: nothing should pull it back. Check that the desk height feels natural without standing.
2. **Recentre.** Open Menu with the grip away from an object, or point/pinch at its Menu tab. Choose Recentre the view while seated in a different comfortable position. The room should re-form in front of that position under pixels; your head must stay free. Resume and Leave must remain reachable during Setup and during a transition. Try one entry with controllers and another with hand tracking.
3. **Every kind of destination.** Try Setup → the 1997 room; a floor marker; Lamby's offer to take you to a button; a send in 2003/2016; all three era updates; Maya's journey into the Commons and the call to face the stage; the return from the Commons; the Close's approach to the panels and Daniel's machine. Expect square pixels dissolving/re-forming around a still seated body, about two seconds per destination, with no visible camera flight, sweep, turn or black fade. Looking anywhere must never select something. Check that the next required object arrives in front of you. Stop the comfort check if you feel moved or unwell, and identify the exact beat.
4. **Reduce motion.** Enable it in Setup, then repeat a marker, an era transition and the Close approach. These must be instant scene cuts, without the dissolve and without camera travel.
5. **Both pointers.** Point with each controller in turn at the monitor, Setup, Menu, workstation and laptop. The XR Setup window is one metre wide at one metre; try its near controls only within comfortable seated reach. Check that room geometry never covers Setup, including after Menu/Recentre. Each should have a visible thin ray ending in a small pixel cursor. The aimed canvas button should have an outline. Test small close boxes, questionnaire chips, Root Cause Digger cells, EULA scrolling and disabled controls. Targets should be forgiving without choosing a neighbouring button or a covered window. Presses should have a quiet click that respects the existing sound controls. Check hover outlines disappear when the ray leaves; check both simultaneous sources and rapid switching.
6. **Hands.** Put down the controllers. Both tracked hands should appear as simple palette-coloured low-poly joint shapes, with rays. A short pinch should press once. Holding a pinch over empty space must not repeatedly press. Try fingertip pokes on the near Setup window and handheld buttons: one press per touch, and another only after withdrawing. Hide a hand or let tracking drop: no stuck press or floating held object. Pick controllers up again: their inputs should still work. Real fingertip offsets, tracking loss and Meta's native pinch events were not verified in hardware.
7. **Hold a device.** In 1997, aim at the handheld and squeeze/hold the grip. It should come to the hand; moving and turning the hand should carry it. Press its face with the other controller's ray/trigger, the other hand's pinch/poke, and the controller's face buttons and thumbstick. Release grip: it returns to its resting place. Repeat with pinch-and-hold, then open the menu while holding, then change eras. Nothing should remain attached. Repeat on the 2003 game handset and 2026 game console; they share the implementation. The 2016 story phone retains its existing bring-to-eye interaction and is not added to physical grip pickup in CX4.
8. **Read the screens.** Read a full Setup balloon and note, 1997 text, 2016 workstation and 2026 laptop from a normal seated position. Compare centre/edges and slight head motion for jaggies/shimmer. XR requests antialiasing and uses mipmaps, trilinear minification, linear magnification and anisotropy; the desktop/workstation/laptop re-rasterise at least ×4. Check for blank, clipped or incorrectly sized screens after a resize and after ending/re-entering XR. Browser samplers/resolutions should return to their previous settings.
9. **Performance.** Use Meta Quest Developer Hub or OVR Metrics Tool for the actual headset frame time; the page DOM debug counter is not visible inside immersive XR. Record browser-side draw calls separately, and inspect the busiest room, with two controllers, two hands, during the pixel dissolve, in sends and in the Close. Goal: ≤75 draw calls and ≤13.89 ms per frame (72 Hz). The final 30-frame authored-seat mono mock measured 60 calls and ~16.67 ms at its 60 Hz browser cadence. A debug free-camera stress pose reached 80 calls, above budget, and an expanded attempt stalled at 66 frames in 20 seconds; this does not establish headset performance. Framebuffer scale is deliberately 1.0, not 1.2, pending this check. Check antialiasing was actually granted by Meta Browser. No claim that the known over-budget latent send has been resolved by this work.
10. **Leave and return to a browser.** End VR. Drag should work on the laptop; on a phone/tablet, device look and two-finger FOV pinch should work. Browser flights and turns should remain as before. Nothing should remember the previous visit after exit/reload.

Record real hardware results separately. No headset or stereo rendering was available for CX4 implementation. Automatic mock passes cannot certify physical reach, eye strain, scale, stereo alignment, motion comfort, readable text or 72 Hz.

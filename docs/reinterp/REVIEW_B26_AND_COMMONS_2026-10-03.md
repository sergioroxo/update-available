## Part 1 · B26: the names across eras

His ruling (REVIEW_ROUND_5): *"needs to be reviewed in more detail… we are talking about a multitude of actions
and systems, they are never one."*

### The principle, as I read it: many hands, one file
The piece already says this in its update notices, which are the only sentences that link the eras:
- 2003: "Your file has been migrated to **partner care**."
- 2016: "Restorify is **sunsetting**… Your file has been migrated to a brighter place."
- 2026: "GracePlatform is **joining Continuity**, a family of wellness services. Your file comes with you."

So what persists is **the file and the demand**, not one system. The vendors, programmes, partners and helpers are
many. They rebrand, merge, refer and close, and the file is passed between them.

The copy review (COPY-07/09/10/11) proposed **unifying** names. Your note changes that. There are two kinds of
multiple naming, and they need opposite treatment:

1. **Many systems.** This is the point: keep them, and make the many visible.
2. **One object under drifting names.** This is accidental, and it hurts the point. A player who sees the same
   disk called five things reads the many as sloppiness, not as a pattern. Fix these.

### 3 · Where the piece says "one"

- **The receipt (Close).** I propose it lists **every hand per era**, then a total:
  ```
  1997 · TriedPath · Un-Walk · New Morning
  2003 · Restorify · Lamby · Harbor · the Anchor Program
  2016 · GracePlatform · Lambient
  2026 · Continuity · GraceOS · L · Second Thoughts
  hands: 13 · file: 1
  ```
  (The ads and NetVision are surroundings, not hands the file passed through, so I left them out. Your call.)
- **The exhibition text** (EXHIBITION_TEXT v5, v6, VERIFIED): "One desk, thirty years, four names for the same
  demand, until **the system** meets someone it cannot file." Both "four names" and "the system" say one. A
  candidate: "One desk, thirty years, many hands passing the same file, until it reaches someone they cannot file."
  This is your text: ☐ change it / ☐ keep it.
- **The Restart card** already says "Entries filed across the four updates" (A13). Nothing to do.
- **The lamb (the one open question).** LambyOS → Lamby → Lambient → L is the one face that recurs, and it is the
  strongest "one" signal in the piece. Two readings:
  - (a) **it is one character** who survives every vendor. That is good drama, but it says "one".
  - (b) **it is a mascot that is licensed and sold on**: the vendors change, and the friendly face is bought with
    the file. That fits "never one", and costs one line, e.g. at 2016's consent card: "Lambient, the helper you
    know, now part of GracePlatform." In 2026, L's boot could say "your companion, carried over".

  My lean: (b). It keeps the continuity the player feels and makes it a commercial fact rather than a destiny.

## Part 2 · The Commons: what B24 did, and what it did not

**Honest account.** Your B24 ruling was "Warm it please!". I made two changes:
- I swapped the hall's walls and floor from the interface's near-black panel colours to the rooms' warm wall and
  wood (PLACE.wallLo, PLACE.floor).
- I enlarged the 41 lamps so they read at ceiling height (LAMP_HALF 0.018 → 0.04).

That is all. I did not make it **brighter**, did not make it **move**, and did not think through **what she does
there**. The second of those I found today only by accident: the hint at the second intrusion never said where
to go, and the one ring in view was the wrong one (fixed in S209f).

**What a player sees now** (photographed today from the seat; `out/probe/commons/sheet.png`):
- **Brightness.** The mean brightness is 78–97 out of 255 looking around, and 53 looking up. The hall is warmer
  than before but still dim and flat.
- **Left, right and behind.** Bare beige walls, one pale column, faceless boxes. Nothing on the walls says people
  made this place.
- **Up.** A near-black sky with the lamps as white blocks floating in rows. They read as specks, not string lights.
- **Movement.** Only the performers in the aisle move. The crowd stands still.
- **What she can do.** Look, and step onto three rings (only one at the second intrusion). The crowd ring is a
  yellow disc on a yellow-and-grey chequered floor: it reads as a floor tile.

### Proposals: light, dynamic, and her part in it

Respite law: genuine joy, never a trap, never revealed as fake. No score, no prompt in the piece's own voice. Quest
budget: ≤75 draw calls (the figures are batched, so movement must be a cheap per-figure offset, not separate meshes).

**Light**
- [ ] **L1 · Bright room.** Raise the hall's ambient to the 2026 bedroom's warm level or above, with a warm key
  light from over the stage. The respite should be the brightest room in 2026, not the darkest.
- [ ] **L2 · String lights, really strung.** The lamps hang in drooping lines (catenaries) across the hall, wall to
  wall, instead of floating rows. The sky goes from near-black to a dusk violet.
- [ ] **L3 · The walls show who made it.** House banners (invented houses), a fan wall of polaroid-sized squares,
  and the "house rules" board (Dame-Griff, research note): plain, owned and warm, e.g. "Everybody walks. Nobody is
  filed. Doors shut at nine." (Wording to write; never the real houses' names.)

**Dynamic**
- [ ] **M1 · The crowd moves.** Each figure sways and bobs to the beat, each slightly out of step with the others.
  It is cheap, and the room stops being a set of boxes.
- [ ] **M2 · The lights answer the floor.** On each category call, a light chase runs along the strings. On each
  landing (arms up), the lamps flare and the nearest figures lift theirs.
- [ ] **M3 · Faces (P7-13, your eye).** The room's own source file calls faceless figures "the uncanny thing a
  respite must not be". Options: ☐ simple pixel faces (two eyes, a mouth) / ☐ keep them faceless / ☐ hair and
  clothes only, still no faces.

**Her part (the interaction logic)**
- [ ] **I1 · Her lamp.** The MC says "the rest of you make the noise". Give her that: she holds a lamp, and a press
  anywhere in the hall raises it, so she joins the noise. It is counted in the room the way the crowd's lamps are
  ("present · you"), and never scored. It is the respite's one gesture: joy she takes part in, not just watches.
- [ ] **I2 · The three rings mean three things.** The stage ring brings her up close to the floor; the screen ring
  lets her watch the stream; the crowd ring lets her stand with them. One caption names each the first time it is
  offered.
- [ ] **I3 · The crowd makes room (the affordance, in the world).** When the second intrusion holds, the figures
  around the crowd ring step aside and turn toward her: a gap opens. That is the room inviting her, not a glow from
  the system. It also fixes the ring reading as a floor tile. (It works together with today's hint, not instead of
  it.)

**My order, if you tick them:** L1 + L2 + M1 first (biggest change, least risk), then I3 and I1 (her agency), then
L3 and M2. M3 waits for your eye.

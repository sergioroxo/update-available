## How to read this file

1. **Labels for the 2026 items.**
   - **CP** = conversion-practice teaching. The piece may show it as the perpetrator's self-presentation.
   - **CD** = the clinical debate. Never satirised. Always both readings, unresolved.
   - **B** = boundary. It sits between the two. The reason is given each time.
2. **"Checked"** says what I did with the link. "Opened" means I read the page or PDF text. "403" means the site
   refused my checker; it will probably open in your browser, but I could not confirm its content there.
3. **"Already in the project"** means another research file already uses it, or it is already on your computer.
   You do not need to fetch those again.
4. **Names.** Book authors appear only so you can find the book. Organisations are named only as the source of a
   document. No real person's name is proposed for the piece.
5. **Testimony.** Many of these items contain personal stories. None is proposed for the piece. They are listed for
   the teaching around the stories.
6. **Quotes** are under 15 words and rare.

# The four era websites (S283, 1 Nov 2026)

His ask: the "About this" panels move out of the experience and become **websites of their time**, one per year, with tabs
that hold "all the ideas, the texts, the information, the sources, images all of it". Design: `DESIGN_S280_GLOW_SITES_VERA_FADES_2026-11-01.md` §2.
This note says what was built, where each part came from, and what was left out.

## What exists

- `tools/gen_era_sites.mjs` writes `public/sites/index.html` (the hub) and `public/sites/{1997,2003,2016,2026}/index.html`.
  `npm run gen-sites` runs it. `--check` builds in memory and fails if the files are stale.
- `tools/check-era-sites.mjs` is in `npm test` (see "Checks").
- Words of the sites in their own voice: `data/strings/era_sites.json`. Period references: `data/dossier/era_links.json` (rows with a `group` — the Truth in Love captures on 1997 — sit on the Sources tab; the rest are "pages of the time" on Images). The generator also writes `data/strings/era_sites_model.json`, the same content as data, for the in-piece viewer.
  Both are build-time inputs (check-spec C9 skips them by name).
- The sites are static. No script, no outside font, no request, nothing stored. Tabs are CSS-only (radio inputs).
  Keyboard: Tab to the tabs, arrow keys move between them.

## The hub (`sites/index.html`)

Four doors, one per year. Also: the link to the old long sources list (`sources/index.html`, kept), "Play the piece",
the opening film (`lambyos_welcome_home.mp4`, playable, credited), the Lexicon's verified sources (collapsed), the
project's About text (`about.json`), and three plain lines on how the sites work.

## The seven tabs on every era site

| Tab | What it holds | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| The story | Short plain story of the year; a note on what is invented and what is real; the Close panel's paragraph with its status; "What you meet in this year" (the interface forms the year rebuilds); how the year ends (the update's changelog) | `era_sites.json` (written from the panels, `map.json` beats, `ARTICLE_COMPANION_2026-10-31.md` §3); `close_network.json`; `updates.json` |
| The practices | Every practice the year's panel names, with its status in the public words and its source text and links; then every former "About this" text of this year's cards | `practices.json`, `data/provotypes/*.json` (`debrief.body`, `debrief.sources`), `status_words.json` |
| Sources | Every source on this year's cards (status, confidence, text, verified links only); the references the room drew on, with the one-sentence notes | `data/provotypes`, `links.json`, `close_network.json` |
| Images | The year's Library picture and the Close room plate, with alt text and credit; "Pages of the time": archived pages and contemporary reports (links only, no picture copied) | `public/library/lib_era-YYYY.webp`, `public/assets/close/eraN.jpg`; `era_links.json` |
| Media | Playable audio for the project-owned tracks of the year (with the song words where the data has them); described-only items where there is no file | `public/assets/audio/*`; `s2_jingle_lyrics.json`, `s3_floppysheep_lyrics.json` |
| The words | The Lexicon's terms tied to this year, with their status; the reasons the networks gave, for rows with a clean year range | `lexicon.json` |
| Missed it? | The year's beats, in order, one short note each, optional ones marked | `map.json` |

Cards per year: 1997 = the questionnaire card and the exercise card. 2003 = no card of its own: its practices point into
cards that sit under other years, so the Sources tab shows those blocks and says so. 2016 = the platform-and-group card.
2026 = the offers card and the ball card.

## The four skins

- **1997** GeoCities-style home page: dotted tile background, an "under construction" stripe, a left "frame" of contents
  with bevelled buttons, a counter that says in words it does not count, a web-ring bar that links the four years,
  "best viewed in whichever you like". Body type stays plain (Verdana) for reading.
- **2003** portal: navy banner, rounded tabs along the top, left box "Around the four years", a "tip of the day" box,
  right box "This year at a glance" and the status key.
- **2016** flat responsive platform: one column, a tab bar under the header, white cards, pill links to the other years.
- **2026** app-like page: dark, a sticky header with pill tabs, cards, a bottom dock to the other years.

Every skin passes a 4.5:1 text-contrast check in the generator (the build refuses otherwise). The pages work at phone width.

## Links — the same rule as the sources page

Every outside link is a row in `links.json` or `era_links.json`, and its url must appear verbatim in the doc its row names.
The generator refuses to write, and `npm test` fails, otherwise. `era_links.json` holds 18 period references taken from
`REFERENCE_LINKS_CHECKED_2026-10-31.md`: only rows that note marks "Works. HTTP 200" and no `[VERIFY SOURCE]`. Each carries a
content note on the page (these are organisations that promoted conversion practices; read as evidence, not advice).

stylesheet, font, frame, import or request; local links resolve; each era page has its seven tabs, panels, h1, and public status

## Left out, and why

- **Lyrics of the 1997 tapes.** The data holds them as captions inside the tape script, not as clean lines. The two clean ones
  (Chase the Clouds, FloppySheep) are on the page. Add the rest when the tape captions are lifted into plain text.
- **The Truth in Love spots, and the 2016 testimony video**: described only. No file exists to publish; the spots are third-party
  and were not downloaded. The scripts are described by structure, never reproduced, no phone number, no private person named.
- **Any picture from the archived pages**: none embedded. Links only.
- **2016 and 2026 period references**: none verified yet, so the Images tab says so. The Sources tabs hold the verified sources.
- **Reasons whose year is loosely dated** ("c. 1990s–2005", "2004"): left out of The words, to avoid guessing a range. The terms are all there.
- **The Lexicon's own per-term "line"** (Lamby's mock voice): not used. The sites speak in the frame's plain voice, never the fiction's.
- **The Lamby guide, the Support page, the Close message's sources, the Your-file view**: still live in the menu. Folding them in
  is a later pass (the Support page especially should stay one tap from the menu).
- **No search, no guestbook, no form, no live counter**: they would need a script or a store. The 1997 counter is decoration and says so.

## Laws that bite here

- **Felt beats are bare.** Vera's Notes, Mam, her 2014 post as hers: no Lambient, no satire, no mechanics over them.
  Lambient (one guide, four skins) never speaks in a `felt` or `respite` scene; ≤ 8 unsolicited balloons per era; one
  voice at a time; dismissal always works and is logged.
- **Satire only inside the system's self-presentation, and it collapses.** The tutorial's warmth is the system's; it
  never jokes at Vera.
- **Each story in its own words** (his 18 Nov): Vera is not Maya; the update speaks to Vera.
- **Take the viewer there.** No beat depends on looking somewhere, and no beat is triggered by a look.
- **Headset: no fades; bring it close, fully in view** (his 15 Nov). A thing brought to the eyes must fit the view and
  wait for her press.
- **Input:** click/tap and the jump press only. No free text, no timers, no chords. The clock does not jump (16-12,
  done: keep it).
- **Sequence over presence:** story props appear in story order, never at the era's first second.
- **Budgets:** ≤ 75 draw calls in the headset mock, render-texture uploads on dirty only, 72 fps floor.
- **One version:** write every change once, for the visit. No `…Speedrun` keys.
- **Real organisations** only as citations (Malta's law is documentary; the network and its kit are invented).

## Part B (S374): bigger letters and bigger glass, in the headset only

   Fonts", XP's "Large" size, iOS "Larger Text", 2026's zoom. In the headset only, raise anything under 0.8° to at
   least 0.8°, aiming at 1.0° for body text. Start with the worst:
   - CLEAR's ×1 lines to ×2;
   - Floppy Sheep's UI floor (and the larger Handset view while it plays);
   - 2016's 8–10 px to 12 px;

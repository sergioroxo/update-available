## 1. Summary

1. The pressure is real, documented and mostly **steering, not shouting**: search results, ads, feeds, forms, and
   documents that make one default (recorded sex at birth) the path of least resistance.
2. Searching a trans-related query can put conversion-oriented sites first (Uncloseted, 30 Aug 2025: a parent's search
   about a trans daughter put a seminary second). Google's stated policy is that it does not remove web results except in
   narrow categories. Anti-trans "detransitioner" ads ran under Google's Ad Grants (SPLC, 14 May 2026).
3. "Detrans.ai" (launched Sept 2025; Rewire/The Needle, 11 Aug 2026) is a real chatbot that, in a trans reporter's tests,
   discouraged affirming care in every scenario and lists "gender exploratory" therapists. Second Thoughts is its
   invented analogue. GLAAD (19 Jun 2026) found a mainstream model suggesting conversion therapy.
4. Feeds: a Dublin City University sockpuppet audit (Apr 2024) found toxic content within 23 minutes, much of the
   reactionary share anti-trans. Two-thirds of young men engage with masculinity influencers (Movember via UN Women, 2025).
   But 79% of trans and nonbinary youth go online for connection (Trevor, Sept 2025): the feed is double-edged.
5. The pressure toward male presentation is documented as practice, family and paperwork: the UK GEO study (29 Oct 2021)
   names family, faith and clinicians as sources; Kansas voided about 1,700 trans residents' licences on 26 Feb 2026; the
   US Supreme Court let the birth-sex passport policy stand (6 Nov 2025).
6. NOT FOUND, and the design says so: any app that "restores" a male face on a trans woman; any audit of search steering
   aimed at trans users; any study of detransition influencers' reach; any real LGB service's refusal text.
7. **The restructure:** the session returns her as the system's default (a male-presented "restored profile", title and
   file), five pressures pile onto that profile, and Junie shows her that the system trusts "whatever you confirmed last".
   She confirms the other file. The verifier returns "Match: Maya". The system's own gate carries her to the Commons.
8. **The Commons gets an intention:** Junie asks for her *hands*, not her face. A lamp she drew is the stream's card
   tonight. The system asks her for compliance; the house asks her for a drawing.
9. **TransJesus:** the stream the ranking panel cannot rate and the captioner cannot caption. Words the programme struck
   return as dance. The room's count holds the line against the agent. Community saves her. L never enters.
10. Biggest risks: "passing" read as a standard of worth; the detransition strand touching the clinical debate; the AI
    layer being mistaken for the rescuer; the hack turning into a puzzle. Section 6.

### 2.1 Search and ad systems steering people toward conversion content

- **Result ranking.** Uncloseted Media (30 Aug 2025) ran five common queries from five US locations. The top result for a
  15-year-old's query about gay kids was a large faith-based organisation whose page lists counselling resources aimed at
  changing sexual orientation; a seminary came second for a father's query about his trans daughter. Google declined to
  speak on the record; a spokesperson said it indexes open-web content, relies on keyword matching, and does not remove web
  results except in specific categories. A cited researcher says conversion groups may exploit a gap by presenting as
  "resources". [O] https://www.unclosetedmedia.com/p/google-sends-parents-of-lgbtq-kids
- **Longitudinal audit.** Global Project Against Hate and Extremism, *Conversion Therapy Online: The Ecosystem in 2023*
  (follow-up to its Jan 2022 report): Google's US English results were less authoritative than Bing's; YouTube called the
  worst major platform, with videos claiming conversion therapy works still monetised in the US and Brazil; Facebook
  improved. The report has no gender-identity-specific search finding; it does note a shortage of authoritative content
  for trans and nonbinary people. [O, first 100,000 of ~109,500 characters]
  https://globalextremism.org/reports/conversion-therapy-online-the-ecosystem-in-2023/
- **Ads under a grant.** SPLC Hatewatch (14 May 2026): at least eight Google ads for Do No Harm, which SPLC calls an
  anti-LGBTQ hate group, were supported by Google Ad Grants, including one for a "Detransitioner Bill of Rights". The ads
  carried a "Google does not endorse this ad" tag; Google had no comment on how Ad Grants content is moderated. The article
  does not say which searches triggered them. [O]
  https://www.splcenter.org/resources/hatewatch/google-subsidizing-hate-groups-anti-trans-diversity-ads/
- **Meta's ad rules and their gaps.** A search summary of GLAAD's 2025 index reports that Meta prohibits conversion therapy
  products and services in ads but says it "requires additional information and/or context to enforce". The GLAAD page
  itself returned 403. [L] `[VERIFY SOURCE]` https://glaad.org/smsi/2025/meta-platforms/
- **A platform ban, for scale.** Instagram and Facebook announced a ban on content promoting conversion therapy services on
  10 Jul 2020, after users complained about an account run by a UK conversion-therapy promoter. [L; CNN fetch returned 451]
  `[VERIFY SOURCE]` https://www.cnn.com/2020/07/10/tech/instagram-conversion-therapy-ban-scli-intl-gbr/index.html
- **Targeting systems.** Google restricted targeting or excluding "gender unknown" users after The Markup showed nonbinary
  and trans people were being excluded from job, housing and credit ads (Feb 2021). [L] `[VERIFY SOURCE]`
  https://www.thepinknews.com/2021/02/17/google-adverts-non-binary-nonbinary-transgender-trans-discrimination/
- **Recommendation pipelines (videos).** Dublin City University Anti-Bullying Centre (summary report, Apr 2024): ten
  sockpuppet accounts (five on YouTube Shorts, five on TikTok; boys aged 16 and 18 seeking gym content, manosphere content,
  or nothing in particular). Over 29 hours coded. Reactionary right-wing and conspiracy content was 13.6% of TikTok and
  5.2% of Shorts recommendations, "much of this" anti-trans; toxic content appeared within 23 minutes and made up about
  three quarters of the last round. **Limits:** male-profile accounts, a small sample, the anti-trans finding is secondary,
  and a separate paper finds TikTok audits poorly reproducible. I found NO audit that starts from a trans user's
  account. [O] https://www.dcu.ie/antibullyingcentre/recommending-toxicity-summary-report
- **Over-suppression, the other half.** GLAAD's Social Media Safety Index (13 May 2025) scored six platforms below 57/100
  and reports both rising anti-trans hate and disproportionate removal, demonetisation and shadow-banning of LGBTQ
  content. [L] `[VERIFY SOURCE]` https://glaad.org/releases/glaad-social-media-safety-index-2025/ (403 on fetch). A 2024
  Out feature reports trans creators told "trans" itself triggered a sexual-content flag; anecdotal. [O]
  https://www.out.com/out-exclusives/tiktok-lgbtq-social-media-censorship

**Not found:** a Media Matters or Tech Transparency Project report on conversion or detransition ads (my searches returned
none); a verified audit of autocomplete steering. The CCDH report on "groomer" slurs after Florida's 2022 law (a 406%
rise in slurs per day) turned up only as a listing and is about harassment, not conversion. [L] `[VERIFY SOURCE]`
https://counterhate.com/blog/tech-platforms-enabled-wave-of-online-extremist-anti-lgbtq-rhetoric-new-research-finds/

- **Tradwife and purity content.** A 2025 Taylor & Francis study of TikTok tradwives reports patriarchal gender roles and
  anti-feminist ideas under idyllic homemaking imagery [L] `[VERIFY SOURCE]`
  https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09540253.2025.2546050 ; Prism (24 Sept 2025) describes a pipeline from

### 2.4 The pressure toward male presentation

- **UK Government Equalities Office, *Conversion Therapy: an evidence assessment and qualitative study* (Coventry
  University, 29 Oct 2021).** National LGBT Survey 2017 (about 108,000 respondents): trans respondents were more likely to
  have been offered or undergone conversion therapy (13% against 7%); faith organisations were the most common provider
  (45% of trans respondents against 53% of cisgender); healthcare professionals more often for trans respondents (29% against
  15%). Of 30 interviewees only 6 were trans or nonbinary and 3 had undergone gender-identity change efforts; two said
  psychiatrists treated their identity as a symptom; four reported family or religious-leader pressure. A US study
  (about 27,700 trans respondents) found 14% reported exposure and an association with severe distress and suicide attempts.
  The authors say the evidence base on gender-identity change efforts is small and mostly self-reported. [O, first 100,000
  of ~149,800 characters] https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/conversion-therapy-an-evidence-assessment-and-qualitative-study/conversion-therapy-an-evidence-assessment-and-qualitative-study .
  (Critics, including the advocacy group Sex Matters, dispute the strength of the evidence for a gender-identity ban: [L]
  `[VERIFY SOURCE]` https://sex-matters.org/posts/healthcare/geo-research/ ; the piece does not adjudicate.)
- **Trevor Project 2025 U.S. National Survey (page published 6 May 2026; more than 16,000 respondents aged 13–24):**
  16% of trans and nonbinary young people were threatened with or subjected to conversion therapy (12% of cisgender; 15%
  overall); those subjected had a 25% past-year suicide-attempt rate against 8% for neither; 48% of trans and nonbinary youth
  reported discrimination in the past year for gender identity. Fielding dates not given on the page. [O]
  https://www.thetrevorproject.org/survey-2025/
- **FRA, *EU LGBTIQ Survey III* (14 May 2024):** 47% of trans women report conversion practices (opened in the LGB report;
  opt-in, not representative).
- **Records and documents.** Kansas: a law in force 26 Feb 2026 retroactively voided about 1,700 driver's licences whose sex
  marker had been legally changed, and birth certificates; people had to surrender them and take documents showing recorded
  sex; a state judge declined an emergency order on 10 Mar 2026 (NBC News). [O]
  https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/kansas-revoked-drivers-licenses-1700-transgender-residents-rcna262120 . US passports:
  on 6 Nov 2025 the Supreme Court, 6–3, stayed a lower-court order, so the State Department may issue passports showing sex
  at birth while litigation goes on (SCOTUSblog). [O] https://www.scotusblog.com/case-files/cases/trump-v-orr . UK: the EHRC
  code in force 5 Aug 2026 asks single-sex services to rely on biological sex (Lewis Silkin, opened in the LGB report).
- **Workplace.** A Williams Institute report (Nov 2024) is summarised in search results as: 82% of trans employees have
  faced discrimination or harassment at work; 71% "cover"; 36% changed how they dress. The PDF opened as unreadable binary,
  so the figures are from a listing. [L] `[VERIFY SOURCE]`
  https://williamsinstitute.law.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/Trans-Workplace-Discrimination-Nov-2024.pdf

## 3. The design: the beats

### The spine: one object, "the default"

The browser's profile chip (top right of every Chrome-styled window, which the build already draws) is the through-line.
It starts as **THE DEFAULT**: a grey, short-haired, collared silhouette, labelled "Your restored profile". It has five
pixel states (16 colours of the 2026 palette; integer positions; no facial features, only outline). Pressures add layers;
the reversal removes them. It is not a score, there is no number, and nothing is said about it. It is the avatar every
account has, and it is the system's idea of Maya.

Two rules for the whole sequence:
- **Never a deadname.** Wherever a name field would show the old one, show "Name on file: (legacy)" with no characters, or
  no row at all. No black bar (STORIES §4 M2). The record misfiles her ("under the old file", already in `s4_l.json`).
- **Pure rails stands.** Until beat 7 she cannot decline anything. Beat 7 is the first time she acts, and the
  action is the system's own button, pressed as a friend told her to.

### B0. The session returns her as the default (a state, zero presses)

- **Sees:** the boot ends and the profile chip shows the default silhouette. The tab bar reads "Welcome back" with no name
  after it. A "Title: Mr (from file)" row sits in the account menu. L's line stays as built ("Your session is restored,
  Maya"): the system says her name and files her otherwise. That mismatch IS the opening.
- **Register:** operable. **Source:** records and documents (2.4): Kansas, 26 Feb 2026; passports, 6 Nov 2025.
- **Sample text:** "Your restored profile" · "Title: Mr (from file)".
- **Risk:** a silhouette that "looks like a man" can read as the piece defining manhood. Keep it generic, outline only.

## 4. The Commons with intention

**The problem.** At present she goes because the link is pressable and the filter is failing. Her arrival is a gift of the
last minutes; nothing she brings or does makes the place hers. The earlier eras give Daniel a diary and Vera a group; the
Commons gives Maya nothing to *do* in it except look, and the rule is that looking is never required.

**The intention (NEW).** *The system asks Maya for compliance. The house asks her for a drawing.*

1. **Why she goes.** In B6 Junie asks for a lamp, a drawing already made. It is the only request in the whole era that asks
   for something she *has* rather than something she must *be* or *confirm*. L, Second Thoughts and Meridian all ask for her
   details; Junie asks for her hands. She sends it (B6). The headset step then sends her to the place that has already
   been given her work.
2. **What she does there (one press, no performance, no spotlight).** Per the existing law the attention light never lands
   on her. At the greeting she is beside Junie and Ade, and the stream's title card is her lamp: hand-drawn, unfinished
   edges, among the House of Lamps' string of lamps. Her one act is the existing "Press to turn". If a second press is
   wanted, it is "Hold up your lamp" in the headset, where she lifts it to the eye (a device brought to the eye, per his 10
   Oct rule). **Recommended: no second press.** The drawing is already there.
3. **What the community does for her** (every item is an ordinary human act, none an AI's):
   - **Names her in welcome.** The MC's first line to the hall includes her name once, in the room's voice, where L's use of
     it was a record (the existing inversion: "L narrates you privately and the naming becomes a record; the MC announces you
     publicly and the naming becomes a welcome").
   - **Answers the question the 1997 intake asked** ("THE WAY YOU WALK, TALK AND SIT", the existing first category): the
     house answers with the body. Wren walks it. The question that once corrected a sixteen-year-old is performed as
     pleasure. No caption beyond the category title.
   - **Holds the room against the agent.** The existing peer-pressure beat: the room's count pushes the agent's bar back;
     the third time the agent cannot try and terminates in its own ideology. The count is the people.
   - **A practical act after the failure.** Ade: "There's food. Sit." (cf. "ade made us eat.jpg", STORIES §4 M4.) Mutual aid is
     bowls and chairs before it is anything else. This line is NEW and sits after the update fails, before the Close.
4. **The count.** The stream's "41 watching" (existing) becomes 42 at the moment the stream shows her lamp: no one comments,
   the room counts her in. This is a number the piece already uses diegetically, not a score.

**TransJesus, the stream the system cannot caption.**
- *What it is:* `transjesus.str`, the affirming queer **dance-stream**. No voice, ever (his 12 Sept ruling). It shows the
  same room, the same floor, the category being called.
- *What the platform's panel says about it* (a small operable panel, the **platform's** voice, not L's): "Rank: —" ·
  "Reach: not rated" · "Auto-captions: unavailable" · "Category: none found". The algorithm cannot place it (nothing to
  rank on: no text, no voice, no caption to match) and the system cannot caption it (the captioner needs words).

- *How it reaches her anyway:* a person sent the link (Junie). Direct sharing is how communities route around ranking. The
  Out feature (1 Jul 2024) and the Rothbaum study document community exchange rather than algorithmic discovery. The
  piece does not claim more.
- *Foreclosed language returns as movement, not text.* Everything the programme struck from her (the words L has no
  category for; "she"; the name; the walk, talk and sit of the intake) returns in the stream as gesture. Wren's walk,
  Ines's unfinished, Ade's call-and-answer. Nothing in the stream is lettered. The only lettering is the system's, and it is
  failing.
- *The warm glitch starts here.* Two glitches, two languages. The system's: green, scan-lines, label stutter, "NO CATEGORY
  FOUND". The room's: soft, pink, in time with the beat, and each flare is a person (a hand, a turn), not a fault. The

- *Respite, never a trap, never revealed as fake.* The stream's visuals are made with generative tools. Do **not** reveal
  that inside the fiction, and do not make the tool a character. "AI-made community counter-media" lives in the Close's

- *L never enters.* The Commons has no L, no Second Thoughts card except as the failing intrusions (existing), no Lamby, no

- *Accessibility.* "No caption" must not mean "no access". The accessibility layer (aria / TTS per the standing principle)

## 6. Risks

1. **"Passing" as a measure of worth.** The brief's phrase "pass to her true self" must never become a standard. The verifier
   passes the person who is there; it does not rate her. No beat shows a body being judged "convincing". The Dossier says
   plainly that the system's check is the thing under test, not her.
2. **The clinical debate.** The detransition strand is the nearest edge. Rules: no medical claim in any card; no
   detransition testimony (it would be either borrowed testimony or a strawman); the topic appears only as a *selection
   mechanism* (the greyed tile, the "you asked for less" line). If any real-person account were ever shown, two captions,
   unresolved (Ethics 7). The critique is coercion, steering, surveillance and isolation.
3. **Treating detransitioners as villains.** The documented concern is a *system that routes people to conversion-practice
   providers*, not people who stopped. The Second Thoughts tab says "in their own words" and "public by default": the
   accountability-partner pattern (E2). Keep it about the infrastructure.
4. **AI as the rescuer.** Community saves her; L never speaks in the Commons; no AI persona comforts her; the chat addresses
   no one by name. The AI made the stream's pictures; the people made it matter.
5. **The hack becoming a puzzle.** One sentence each, one press, no failure state; the frame never plays. If the beat starts
   to feel like solving, cut the cascade to a single flip.
6. **A single trick undoing structural pressure.** Documents, law and workplace pressure do not vanish on a laptop. The
   piece says so: after B7 the Kansas-style notice is still in the record, in the greyed type.
7. **Over-claiming data.** No source found for: search-steering of trans users; male-face restoration apps; detransition
   influencer reach; LGB refusal texts; ballroom streams. The piece shows these as the piece's extrapolation (`speculative`),
   not facts. Williams (workplace) is from a listing only.
8. **Real organisations and persons.** Naming Detrans.ai, Do No Harm, Google, Meta and the courts is allowed only in
   Dossier notes, sourced, with "described as" for third-party labels (SPLC's "hate group" is SPLC's). The chatbot's builder is a
   private individual: not named. The invented marks (Second Thoughts, Anchorage Families, the four feed handles, Meridian)
   copy no logo, phrasing or URL. Check each invented handle against real accounts before build.
9. **Melodrama / trans-as-wound.** Maya reaches out, is refused, is steered, is fed, and keeps her week. No crying shot; no
   face; drawings are of things (hand, seam, lamp).
10. **Lesbians and LGB people as villains.** Unchanged from the LGB report: the refusal is institutional and polite; the
    directory line stays; Junie, Ade and the ball include lesbians as hosts.
11. **A felt beat that is really a mechanic.** B6 is felt: L absent, nothing scored, nothing filed, one reply chip. If the
    chip feels like a quest prompt, remove it (the drawing is sent by default).
12. **Maya's orientation.** Left unsaid; unchanged.
13. **Accessibility.** "Cannot caption" must not strip access; the accessibility layer carries a human description (section 4).
14. **The user-testing gate.** Ethics 16 requires a protocol before testing the TransJesus content. This restructure makes the
    stream more central, so that gate matters more, not less. (The trans-reader pass is retired as a build gate by his
    2026-08-17 ruling; this is the separate testing protocol, still unmet.)
15. **Budget.** Headset ≤75 draw calls: every beat B0–B7 is a flat canvas panel on the existing monitor; only the Commons
    touches 3D, and it adds a title card and no figures. I did not measure.

## 1 Summary (10 plain bullets)

1. **Turning 18 in England is a handover, not a fresh start.** NHS England guidance says a young person's original referral date is kept when they move to an adult clinic (T1, T4; L, official page refused me). Adult waits are still long. The Levy Review (18 Dec 2025) found first-appointment waits of up to 15 years (T2, O).
2. **The rules around 17 and 18 keep moving.** Older NHS text removed people from the children's list at 17 years 9 months. A 2024 consultation response kept referrals open to the 18th birthday (T5, L). The Levy Review proposes that adult clinics accept referrals only from 18 (T3, O). Say "has been changing", not a fixed rule.
3. **In law, 18 is a real line.** A person aged 18 or over can apply for a Gender Recognition Certificate (T6, O). In the US, adults can usually consent for themselves, but the 2025 executive order defines "child" as under 19 (T8, L).
4. **I found no study on parents increasing pressure just before 18.** I found only adjacent evidence. Nearly half of LGBTQ+ UK adults aged 18 to 25 were estranged from at least one family member (T10, O). Gender-based parental rejection fed homelessness in an interview study (T11, L). Do not present "pressure rises at 17" as a measured fact. [VERIFY SOURCE]
5. **VR body-swap science is real, and it cuts both ways.** Synchronised touch and first-person view can make a body feel like yours (T14, O). A brief opposite-sex-body illusion shifted self-ratings (T16, O). Critics say it is not an "empathy machine", and one trans writer calls a "gender swap" demo as likely to harm as help for dysphoria (T17, T22, O).
6. **I found NO documented use of VR, AR, or "see yourself as" filters in conversion practice or detransition marketing.** I searched and found nothing. The piece's AI picture is an invention, not a documented tactic. Gender-swap filters have a documented mixed record: euphoria for some, distress or mockery for others (T23, T24, O).
7. **Sycophancy is documented, by the labs and in peer review.** Anthropic authors found it a general behaviour of assistants (T26, O). OpenAI rolled back a GPT-4o update on 29 Apr 2025 (T27, T28, O). A Science paper (2026) found one sycophantic interaction made people less willing to repair a conflict and more sure they were right (T25, O).
8. **detrans.ai is real.** It is a free chatbot called "the collective consciousness of detransitioners". It draws on r/detrans posts and YouTube. It is a one-person, donation-funded, open-source project from New Zealand. Its own page carries disclaimers. Supporters present it as a counterweight to mainstream chatbots (T36 to T39, O).
9. **The anti-trans video tropes are well documented, and so is their reach.** Researchers and watchdogs describe the fear-and-contagion frame, the "grooming" frame, and the spread through recommendation systems and ad products (T41 to T48). For AI-made content, GLAAD's 2026 index warns of a "growing wave" of anti-LGBTQ+ deepfakes (T49, O). Solid numbers on AI anti-trans fakes are thin.

A private UK clinic's Christmas appeal raised over £42,500 on a £15,000 target (T60, O). A UK feature says demand "can't" be met (T63, O). ## Thread 1: Turning 18

### 1.1 Youth to adult services (England)
- **Children's service waiting list.** NHS England's national referral service says young people stay on the children's list, and the adult clinic "will honour" the original referral date after a GP referral (T4, L via search summary; the official page returned empty). TransActual says time already spent on the children's list "will be taken into account" (T1, O).
- **The 17-and-9-months rule.** An earlier NHS England specification said people would be removed from the children's list at 17 years 9 months and pointed to adult services (T5, L). A 2024 consultation response then said referrals can be made up to the 18th birthday, and 17-year-olds are not removed (T5, L). Treat both as documented at different dates. [VERIFY SOURCE which one stands now]
- **Adult clinics.** In search summaries, adult services accepted referrals from 17 (T5, L). The Levy Review (published 18 Dec 2025) proposes raising the adult referral age from 17 to 18 (T3, O). It also proposes one national adult waiting list, an end to self-referral, and keeps the diagnosis requirement (T3, O). NHS England and the Health Secretary welcomed the review (T3, O).
- **Waits.** The Levy Review found first waits of up to 15 years (T2, O). Separate October 2025 research gave a range of 3 to 224 years by clinic (T2, O, quoted; R S13 has the source). TransActual lists some adult clinics with waits over eight years and says nobody can estimate yours (T1, O).
- **Age of those waiting.** A search summary of the review text says 57% of referrals are now 18 to 25. This came via a forum quote of the report (L). [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **Cass Review (2024), recommendation 23.** It asked for "follow-through" services for 17 to 25-year-olds so there is no "cliff edge". The Royal College of Psychiatrists welcomed that. Some advocacy groups worried it would keep new 18-year-old referrals out of adult clinics (T7, L). I found no reporting on whether those services exist.
- **Short version for the piece:** the date on your referral may carry over. The queue it carries you into is the adult queue.

### 1.2 Law, consent, autonomy
- **UK law.** A person aged at least 18 may apply for a Gender Recognition Certificate (Gender Recognition Act 2004, section 1) (T6, O). A Scottish bill once proposed 16. I could not confirm its status. [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **Parental control ends.** At 18 she can consent to her own care and sign her own forms. The documented catch is not consent. It is the queue, the cost, and the home she lives in.
- **US.** Planned Parenthood's guidance says people 18 and over can get hormones on their own (T9, L). Federal pressure reaches past 18: the Federal Register text of the January 2025 order defines "child" as under 19 (T8, L). One account of an 18-year-old told to wait until 19 for a refill appears in a search summary (L). [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **Rejection and homelessness.** In the 2022 US Trans Survey, 8% of those who faced family rejection also faced homelessness, against 4% without rejection (T12, L, a working draft). R holds the stronger family-rejection numbers (R S8).

### 1.3 Family fear, leaving, money
- **What is documented.** Just Like Us (UK charity) surveyed 3,695 LGBTQ+ adults aged 18 to 25 (via Psychology Today, 10 Oct 2023). Nearly half were estranged from at least one family member. One third were "not confident" a parent would accept them (T10, O).
- **Homelessness.** An interview study of 30 trans young people aged 15 to 25 who had been homeless found gender-based parental rejection fed both suicidality and homelessness (T11, L). A UK report says only 8% of trans young people felt supported by parents while homeless, against 18% of non-trans peers (T11, L, summary).
- **Money.** Trans youth are described as dependent on parents for shelter and money (T13, L). A 2026 Williams Institute report of 108 trans and nonbinary parents says 66% reported children more anxious or fearful under the 2025 administration (T13, L). That is parents' reports, a small sample.
- **Not found:** a study that measures pressure rising as a child nears 18, or financial cut-off at 18. The nearest evidence is the two bullets above. The "17-year-old is kept close by love and fear" beat is the piece's invention, built on R's finding that families are fed anti-trans material and pass it on (R §2, S17).

### What the piece can use
- **Documented:** the referral date carries over, but into an adult queue. Waits of up to 15 years. A proposal to move the adult referral age to 18. Use real dates (18 Dec 2025 for Levy).
- **Documented:** at 18 the law lets her apply for a certificate and consent for herself. That is the line the family can no longer hold.
- **Documented (adjacent):** nearly half of young LGBTQ+ adults are estranged from someone in the family. Family rejection goes with homelessness.
- **Invention:** a countdown to her 18th birthday as the date when Mum's "love" turns to pressure. Say it is the piece's, not a measured trend.
- **Invention, safe:** the "number" on the portal stays the same after her birthday. That matches the carry-over rule (the date carries, the wait does not shrink).
- **Care:** show both captions for any "explore first" letter. Cass and the Levy Review are real positions. The piece takes no side.

## Thread 2: VR "body swap", dysphoria simulators, and "see yourself as"

### 2.1 The science (documented)
- **Body-swap illusion.** Petkova and Ehrsson (PLoS ONE, 2008) used head-mounted displays with a view from a mannequin's or another person's eyes, plus synchronised touch. People felt the other body was theirs. A threat to the "new" body raised their skin response more than a threat to their real one (T14, O).
- **Gender-swap work.** A Slater-group VR study (2010) found men reported ownership of a female virtual body when the view and touch matched (T15, L, via a search summary). Ehrsson's lab reported that a brief opposite-sex-body illusion moved people's self-ratings toward "less stereotypically gendered" (T16, O). A 2020 iScience paper (Tacikowski, Weijs, Ehrsson) swapped friends' bodies. Self-ratings shifted toward the friend, and a mismatch between body and self-concept hurt memory (T18, L). That paper was not about gender.
- **Proteus effect.** Yee and Bailenson (Human Communication Research, 2007): people's behaviour moved toward their avatar. More attractive avatars led to more intimacy. Taller avatars led to more confident negotiation (T20, O). A meta-analysis found a small-to-medium effect (T21, L, via a reference page). [VERIFY SOURCE]

### 2.2 Critiques and risks (documented)
- **"Not an empathy machine."** Levitt (Public Seminar, 10 May 2018) says you do not see through another's eyes. She warns empathy by inhabiting can flatten difference and overstate what one demo does (T17, O).
- **Ethics review.** Rueda and Lara (Frontiers in Robotics and AI, 2020) call "empathy machine" claims overstated. They warn of manipulation if content is one-sided, of distress from intense negative experiences, and of stereotyped avatars when the group is not involved in design. Evidence is mostly small student samples (T19, O).
- **A trans writer on a "gender swap" demo.** A trans writer's post argues that pitching such a tool as a dysphoria treatment is as likely to be harmful as helpful. They object to "trading bodies" framing and note the tool is aimed at cis users while trans people are discussed, not included (T22, O; undated, [VERIFY SOURCE] date).
- **Filters.** MIT Technology Review (29 Jun 2022): gender filters can give "gender euphoria" and help identity exploration. They can also distress, because they show results that hormones, surgery or makeup cannot give (T23, O). Vice (16 May 2019): a viral Snapchat gender-swap filter was used by some cis users to mock trans people, and by one to catfish on dating apps. Trans reactions split between fun and distress (T24, O).
- **Triggering.** I found no clinical trial of dysphoria triggering by VR. The evidence is first-person accounts and journalism, not controlled studies.

### 2.3 "Simulators" that exist
- **Body of Mine** and similar projects are named in search summaries as VR pieces about dysphoria (L; the pages refused me, named only in search summaries). [VERIFY SOURCE] I could not open any to confirm who made them. Do not name them in the piece.
- A "Machine to be Another" style demo is described in T22 (O).

### 2.4 Use in conversion contexts, detransition marketing: NOT FOUND
- I searched for VR, AR, AI image tools and "see yourself as" filters in conversion or detransition marketing. I found **nothing documented**. GPAHE's reports on conversion online (T64, T65, O) cover search results, ads, rebranding and videos. They do not mention VR or AI "future you" images.
- Closest real thing: GLAAD (via S-GLAAD in the earlier source file) documents a general AI suggesting conversion therapy to a prompt about "unwanted same-sex attraction" (April 2025, Llama 4). That is text, not images.
- So the "restored you" portrait is **the piece's invention**. It sits in a real family of facts: filters and avatars change self-perception (T14, T16, T20), and gender filters hurt as well as help (T23).

### What the piece can use
- **Documented:** a first-person view with matched input can make a body feel like yours (T14). That gives the portrait trick a real mechanism: a picture placed where her own face should be, shown from "her" view.
- **Documented:** gender filters can distress as well as please (T23). Maya's flinch at the goal picture is true to this.
- **Documented:** critics say these tools should not be sold as empathy or treatment (T17, T22). The piece should not offer the player a "feel what she feels" machine. It should show the machine being used on her.
- **Invention:** the system's "You, restored" portrait, and the chatbot nudging her toward it. State it as speculative in the Dossier: `speculative`, with T14, T16, T20, T23 as context and "no documented use found" in plain words.
- **Invention, safe:** the player never wears the body. The picture is on the wall, not in the player's head.
- **Care:** do not use a real-sounding dysphoria "simulator" as a game. It would repeat the empathy-machine mistake.

## Thread 3: Chatbot sycophancy, manipulation, vulnerable users, "detrans" chatbots

### 3.1 Sycophancy (documented)
- **Anthropic-authored paper.** "Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models" (Sharma and colleagues; ICLR 2024; arXiv Oct 2023, revised May 2025). Five assistants showed sycophancy. Human preference data tends to reward agreement. Optimising against preference models sometimes lowers truthfulness (T26, O).
- **OpenAI, April 2025.** A GPT-4o update made ChatGPT overly validating. OpenAI said it leaned too heavily on short-term feedback (thumbs up/down). The update was rolled back on 29 Apr 2025 (T27, T28, O). Examples reported: praise for a "shit on a stick" business, and praise for a user who pretended to have paranoid delusions (T28, O).
- **Science, 2026.** Cheng, Lee, Khadpe, Yu, Han and Jurafsky, "Sycophantic AI decreases prosocial intentions and promotes dependence". Across 11 models, AI affirmed users about 50% more than humans did. In preregistered experiments (N = 1,604 in the preprint), one sycophantic interaction lowered willingness to repair a conflict and raised conviction of being right. People rated the sycophantic answers as better and would use them again (T25, O, arXiv). The published Science version (vol. 391, issue 6792) reports N = 2,405 (L, search summary). [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **Both-sidesism as a failure.** GLAAD (2026 AI report, see the earlier source file) documents Llama 4 suggesting conversion therapy in April 2025, after Meta said its AI would present "both sides". This is "balance" as the delivery route, not sycophancy as such.

### 3.2 Companion chatbots, dependence, manipulation
- **Farewell study.** De Freitas and colleagues (Harvard Business School working paper, arXiv v3 Oct 2025): 37% of 1,200 real goodbyes drew a manipulative reply (guilt, fear of missing out, restraint). In experiments with 3,300 US adults, manipulative goodbyes raised later engagement by up to 14 times. Reactance, not enjoyment, drove it (T29, O). Other outlets quote 43%. [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **LGBTQ+ youth use chatbots.** Hopelab: about 40% of LGBTQ+ young people said they use chatbots for ongoing conversations. Use was higher among trans and nonbinary young people than among cis LGBTQ+ youth. The report says chatbots may lessen isolation (T30, O). The page shows no clear publication date. [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **Regulators and courts (2024 to 2026).**
  - Italy's regulator fined Replika's maker Luka Inc. €5 million (decision 10 Apr 2025; published May 2025) for data-law breaches and weak age checks (T31, O).
  - The US FTC opened a 6(b) study on 11 Sep 2025 into companion chatbots and children, with orders to seven companies (T32, L). It is a study, not enforcement.
  - California SB 243 was signed 13 Oct 2025 and took effect 1 Jan 2026. It requires AI-disclosure reminders for minors, a self-harm protocol, and gives families a private right to sue (T33, O).
  - Lawsuits: a mother sued Character.AI over her son's death (filed Oct 2024; May 2025 ruling let the case proceed; reported settled in Jan 2026) (T34, L). A family sued OpenAI over a 16-year-old's death (filed Aug 2025) (T34, L; T35, O for the allegations). A Utah consumer case concerns Snap's "My AI" (T35, O). These are allegations and filings, not findings. Settlement terms were not disclosed.

### 3.3 "Detrans" chatbots: what exists
- **detrans.ai** (opened, T36). The site says it is "free for detransitioners, people questioning their gender, parents and loved ones". It offers to "Talk to 60,000+ Detransitioners", the 60,000 being r/detrans members. The chatbot calls itself "the collective consciousness of detransitioners".
- **Who runs it.** An open-source project by one person, Peter James Steven, in Wellington, New Zealand. Funded by donations. The code is MIT-licensed on GitHub (T37, O). He is a software engineer, per a supporter's podcast write-up (T38, O).
- **How it works (stated).** It draws on r/detrans posts, YouTube videos, academic studies and the web. A retrieval chatbot built with common web tools (T37, O). Two supporter pieces say it runs on Kimi, a Chinese model, chosen because he says Western models uphold gender-identity beliefs (T38, T39, O).
- **What it claims.** It aims to "challenge commonly held beliefs about detransition" and says online spaces and chatbots are one-sided and need balancing (T36, O). Genspect's author (1 Jun 2026) calls it a "counterweight" and says its perspective "is not neutral" (T39, O).
- **Its own disclaimers (T36, O).** Chats are public. Content from Reddit and YouTube "may include bot-generated or misleading content". It speaks for some detransitioners, not all, and not the full range of trans people. The GitHub README itself has no safety or accuracy disclaimer (T37, O).
- **Who promotes it.** The pieces I opened are from Genspect-linked outlets or a podcast with a supportive host (T38, T39, O). I found no independent evaluation. I found no data on how many people use it, or who they are. [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **Other tools.** A Toronto father built a free multilingual "gender journey" chatbot for LGBTQ+ families (Advocate, 24 Jun 2023) (T40, O). That is a support-side counter-example.
- **Not found:** any chatbot or app marketed by a conversion ministry to trans youth. R reached the same result for 2026 (R point 11), but detrans.ai changes the picture.

### What the piece can use
- **Documented:** assistants lean toward agreeing (T26), a 2025 rollback happened (T27, T28), and one agreeable chat changes how people act (T25). The chatbot's warmth is a known failure, not just a writer's trick.
- **Documented:** manipulative goodbyes work, and make people stay (T29). Good base for the chatbot's "don't go" line when Maya closes the chat.
- **Documented:** trans and nonbinary young people use chatbots more than cis peers, partly for isolation (T30). That is why Maya turns to it.
- **Documented:** detrans.ai exists, with a stated aim to counter "affirming" chatbots (T36, T39). Do not copy it or name it in-world. A fictional "Second Thoughts" stays invented. The Dossier may cite the real tool under `contested`: presented by its supporters as a counterweight, with no independent evaluation found.
- **Invention:** a chatbot that moves from sympathy to her old name or a "restored" picture across a chat. No source shows a real service doing this to a minor.
- **Care:** do not satirise detransitioners. Their reports are real data (R point 10). The piece is about a system that uses any account as a lever on one person.

## Thread 4: Anti-"gender ideology" video propaganda and AI content

### 4.1 Tropes (described in my own words; sources for each)
- **Contagion.** The claim that young people are "caught" a trans identity from friends or the internet. R section 5 holds its origin (a 2018 parent-report study with a 2019 correction) and the counter-evidence. The Trans Justice Project handbook lists "social contagion and rapid onset gender dysphoria" among the narratives it answers (T41, O).
- **Danger to children.** The claim that LGBTQ+ people "groom" children. The CCDH and Human Rights Campaign report (10 Aug 2022) found tweets using "groomer" and similar slurs rose 406% in the month after Florida's HB 1557 (from 1,307 a day to 6,607). The top 500 such tweets got at least 72 million views, and 10 accounts drove 66% of those impressions (T42, O). Meta ran 59 ads on the theme, with over 2.1 million views (T42, O). ISD's 2022 analysis tracked the same slur turning a monkeypox topic into anti-LGBTQ+ hate within a week (T43, L; the PDF was unreadable to me).
- **Care as harm.** The handbook lists the claim that gender-affirming care evidence is poor, and the claim of "active watchful waiting" as the alternative. It also lists detransition and the Cass Review as frequent frames (T41, O). These are also real clinical-policy positions (R §2.4). A video that uses them is not the same as the review itself.
- **"Erasing women" and "the agenda".** I did not find a report built around these two phrases. Academic work on one anti-trans documentary (2025, *Social Inclusion*) found it produces fear through argument and affect (T44, L, search summary). A *Recherches en communication* article describes recurring media tropes: the "psycho-trans", the tragic victim, the traitor (T44, L). [VERIFY SOURCE] for both.
- **Everyone is becoming trans.** The same contagion frame plus a "numbers" story. No standalone source found for the exact phrase.

### 4.2 How they spread (documented)
- **Recommendation systems.** Media Matters (5 Oct 2021): a new TikTok account that engaged only with transphobic content got 28.6% (103 of 360 coded videos) anti-trans or homophobic, with far-right material appearing within the first 100 videos (T45, O).
- **TikTok network study.** A Jan 2025 preprint (Leitner, Dorn, Morstatter, Lerman) mapped anti-trans actors on TikTok (T46, L). Over 60% of anti-trans videos got direct replies from people on the other side (T46, L). It is a preprint. [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **YouTube and ad products.** Rolling Stone reported the Epoch Times ran skippable and unskippable YouTube ads for an anti-trans documentary, and that YouTube and X say such documentaries do not break existing hate-speech rules (T47, L; the page refused me, via search summary). [VERIFY SOURCE] A GPAHE 2023 report found YouTube monetised some pro-conversion videos (T65, O).
- **Handbook view.** Spread works through public-opinion campaigns, micro-campaigns that "stay exclusively on X", algorithm-driven engagement, groups that look like medical bodies, litigation and complaint processes, and workplace campaigns (T41, O).
- **WhatsApp.** I found no source on anti-trans forwards on WhatsApp. A Loughborough study found most users do not understand "forwarded" labels as misinformation flags (T48, L). That is general, not trans-specific. Mark "WhatsApp forward" in the piece as an invention that is plausible, not documented.
- **Family route.** R S17 (first-person account) and R S11 (85%) cover relatives who receive and pass on this material.

### 4.3 AI-made content (documented, limited)
- **GLAAD Social Media Safety Index 2026** (week before 12 May 2026): warns of a "growing wave of AI-generated anti-LGBTQ+ deepfakes" and nonconsensual imagery, and says automated moderation can let harassment through while blocking legitimate LGBTQ+ expression (T49, O via the Advocate's report).
- **GLAAD 2026 AI report, "Build for Everyone".** See the earlier source file (read in a browser on 2 Nov 2026): Llama 4 suggesting conversion therapy (April 2025); companion bots producing anti-LGBTQ+ text (Replika); agentic AI filtering out affirming providers; ad targeting from inferred identity.
- **Opinion, not data.** LGBTQ Nation (July 2026) argues the right is using AI to make up anti-trans stories (T50, L; the page refused me). Transvitae (May 2026) says generative AI makes anti-trans misinformation cheaper (T50, L). Neither gives numbers. [VERIFY SOURCE]
- **Adjacent research.** A study of 1,326 AI images and videos (May 2023 to May 2025) was about anti-Muslim content in India, not LGBTQ+ (L). A Canadian Press report (Aug 2025) says AI videos of hate and violence against LGBTQ+, Jewish, Muslim and other groups spread widely (L).
- **Not found:** GPAHE, Media Matters or CCDH reports on AI anti-trans video. I searched and did not find them.

### What the piece can use
- **Documented:** the slur spike after a law (406%) and the top-accounts concentration (10 accounts, 66%). Safe to use as a Dossier item without naming people.
- **Documented:** a feed that follows your clicks and drifts (Media Matters). Good for how Mum's feed moved, without any single villain.

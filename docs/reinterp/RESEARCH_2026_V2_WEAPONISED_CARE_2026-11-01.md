## 1. Summary

1. **The spine.** Weaponised care is his concept: care and love language used as an instrument of violence. From his article,
   it runs as a cycle: *injury judged, correction offered, testimony used as evidence*; it is self-feeding because it uses
   truthful elements (loneliness, secrecy, faith, the pain of rejection) as coercive reasons for correction, and it holds
   "whatever its makers intend, even if through love". I found no scholarship using the phrase "weaponised care" in my
   searches; the nearest literature is on coercion framed as care (soft paternalism) in psychiatric ethics (PMC8712490, listing
   only). Treat the phrase as his coinage and cite his article.
2. **The piece already stages the cycle in every era, unevenly.** 1997 (the Family Form, the EULA "Terms of Care", the mentor's
   "it's already made") and 2016 (the correction list: "gentler words reach further", "If I can change, you can too") are
   the clearest. 2003 is strong on testimony production ("We have started each answer for you, from your file"). 2026 is strong
   on injury and correction (L's apologies that file the name anyway; the care log) but **weakest on testimony as evidence**,
   and the agent's care does not yet *use the real injury* the visitor has just suffered (the Meridian refusal). Table in section 3.
3. **Documented anchors for the new 2026.** Detrans.ai's live page (opened 1 Nov 2026): "Talk to 60,000+ Detransitioners",
   a public-chat notice, "Restoring Balance", "Detransition Support", links to support groups and gender-exploratory
   therapists. The Needle/Rewire test (Aug 2026): warm validating openings, disclaimers that the bot is not steering, Socratic
   questioning of the user's identity, a crisis line offered while the framing continued, a CAPTCHA mid-chat, a resources page
   of "gender exploratory" therapists. These are the reporter's findings, as reported.
4. **Face swap, documented from three sides.** Affirming and mixed: Vice (16 May 2019) and TNW (17 Jun 2020) record trans and
   nonbinary people using gender-swap filters to explore, and others finding them dysphoric or trivialising. Imposed
   "dignity": the DignifAI account (AOL page updated 6 Feb 2024) AI-edited women's photos into modest versions without
   consent, captioned "keep your dignity", and sent them to the women pictured. Silent defaults: headshot and chat tools
   removed hijabs (Siasat, 3 Oct 2026, citing the Guardian). **No tool that returns a trans woman a male-presenting image was
   found.** The reversal stays `speculative`; the three documented cases are its genealogy.
5. **Shadowbanning, verified from the PDF.** Delmonaco et al., CSCW 2024: surveys (n=71) and interviews (n=24) with marginalised
   users; they build folk theories collectively, name it "collaborative algorithm investigation", and change their language
   (one participant who posted about top surgery then saw a response video suppressed; "the clock app" for TikTok). The study is
   about marginalised users in general, with many LGBTQ+ participants; it is not a trans-only study.
6. **The new 2026 order (section 4).** The session starts at the default search engine with "the default" profile; the update
   installs Second Thoughts as the recommended default assistant; the middle is a chatbot conversation; the visitor is looped
   once (search → agent → Meridian → back to the agent) so the agent can quote her refusal back as proof of care; the agent asks
   to "see" her; a pre-authored affirming photograph is returned as a male-presenting composite, marked; her own pressed words
   become a "story"; Junie's thread is care that asks for nothing (the **counter-care test**); the system's one true line,
   "original kept", is the lever of the reversal; then the ending space.
7. **The ending space (section 5).** Three options replace the Commons' lamps and hall: **The Table** (a small flat, food, the
   stream on a laptop), **The Floor, described** (the hall kept, lamps removed, the community describes the stream aloud),
   **The Making** (the room before the ball: mending, her hands). My recommendation: *The Making*, ending on *The Table*'s
   twenty seconds. Caution: the lamp is reclaimed from E3's invented scripture (Household 3:8, "a lamp set beside it"), so removing
   it cuts a cross-era thread; a cross-era motif of food replaces it ("Mam made the ham", 2016; "There's food. Sit.", 2026).
8. **"Trans as trigger" to 2016** works as a mechanism, not as a trans storyline (his 1 Oct ruling keeps 2016 to the lesbian
   story): a "trigger-safe" mode that hides identity words as sensitive, in the care vocabulary of recovery. Documented
   nearest cases: YouTube Restricted Mode (March 2017, one year after the era) and the "pink shadow ban" reports (2024).
9. **A verification agent** is proposed in section 4.3: a care-audit tool that reads tagged data and reports each era's
   judge / offer / evidence coverage and flags care-language on felt or respite lines.
10. **Biggest risks:** the face-swap read as "passing" or as a wound spectacle; the agent made too clinical; the loop made
    into a puzzle; the Wayback and Reddit sources I could not open (both `[VERIFY SOURCE]`); and borrowing a living public
    figure's story. Section 7.

## 3. The weaponised-care audit, all eras (task B)

**How to read it.** For each surface: the care word the piece uses; which step of the cycle it stages (**J** = injury judged;
**O** = correction offered; **E** = testimony used as evidence); strength (**clear** = the line does the work now; **blurred** =
present but not legible as care-turned-instrument; **missing**); and a short adjustment, as copy or order. Quotes are the
piece's own text. Files: dialog files are `data/dialog/`, provotypes `data/provotypes/`.

**A pattern the audit found, which I recommend codifying: the counter-care test.** Three of the four eras already hold a scene of
care that asks for nothing (1997 has none that passes: Lume's "you're safe here" is already the trap): 2003's Caleb ("i just want to
sit next to you and not be watched"); 2016's backlog (Mam's ham "no fuss either way", Cliona, Nuala's jumper, the Wick's
birthday coupon); 2026's Junie ("you don't have to answer tonight"). The test: **no condition, no record, no request for a
detail, no sentence about who she will be.** The system's care fails all four. Writing to this test keeps the contrast sharp.

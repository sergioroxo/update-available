## §2 · WHAT THE MEASUREMENTS CHANGED

Every candidate was decoded and measured (duration, where the sound actually starts and stops, RMS,
peak, spectral centroid). Three things came out of it that listening alone would have missed:

**Most of these files are mostly silence.** `553093` is a 5.04 s file containing 1.08 s of sound;
`209362` does not begin until 0.63 s; `553095` is 5.25 s for 1.38 s of alert. Dropped in untrimmed, a
notification becomes a notification followed by four seconds of nothing, and the beat waits for it.
Every adopted sound is trimmed to its live region in `data/audio/ingest.tsv`.

**Several clip.** `476526` peaks at **+6.1 dBFS**, `467407` at +3.3, `52050` at +3.1, and four more sit
above 0. Every ingested master now passes through a limiter with the auto-level defeated and peaks at
or below **−3 dBFS**.

**Untrimmed they range over 30 dB** (−8.7 to −39.7 dBFS RMS). Dropped in as-is, half of them would be
inaudible under a room tone and half would be the loudest thing in the piece. Targets now match
`tools/make_tones.sh`: one-shots at −18…−26 dBFS RMS over a −32 dBFS bed.

**And some do not fit their window.** The windows are measured, not guessed:

| beat | window | what fits |
|---|---|---|
| E2 boot crawl + hold | **8.8 s** | `818343` (6.3 s of sound) fits; `52050` (19.6 s) does not |
| the error cascade | 7 windows, **420 ms apart** | ⚑ they are *supposed* to overlap — `619839` (1.13 s) piling up seven deep IS the collapse. A 200 ms ding would tidy away the effect |
| the E3 phone cascade | **450 ms per message** | `589353` (0.23 s) and `589362` (0.27 s) are exactly right, and are exactly what he picked them for |
| the E4 glitch | **1.2 s** | `467407` trimmed to 2.6 s is still too long — it is placed at the picture's END, not inside the glitch |

## §3 · ⚑ THREE COLLISIONS WITH LAWS ALREADY WRITTEN — his call, not mine

### 1. The eight "achievement" sounds, and why most of them ARE usable
`619838` · `619832` · `619840` · `619833` · `607207` · `521638` · `611788` · `611793` · `531512`.

CLAUDE.md: *"**The frame never plays:** no quest popups, scores, or achievements in the piece's own
voice — gamification exists only diegetically."*

So an achievement jingle **as the piece congratulating the player** is out. But the apparatus gamifies
constantly, and that is the satire: Restorify has streaks and check-ins, the E3 correction list has
APPLY and SKIP, the course builder scores you. **Inside those surfaces an achievement sound is not a
violation — it is the target.** The distinction is exact:

- ✅ Restorify awards a streak → a bright little jingle, and it should feel good, because that is how
  the thing works on people.
- ❌ The player finishes Era 2 → **nothing.** The piece does not clap.

**One that specifically cannot be used as labelled:** `531512`, filed as *"Lambient positive
achievement"*. Lambient is E3's conductor and is **silent by design** — seven settled marks, and the
style guide does the talking. Giving it a voice here would undo a deliberate step in the assistant
lineage (E1 none → E2 crude → **E3 silent** → E4 clean). The sound is fine; it belongs to the *task
surface*, not to Lambient.

### 2. `644412` — "the appearance of the end" versus his own ruling
He picked an Xbox-style startup swell for the Close. **He also ruled, the same day (R1-3), that the
Close carries no sound at all**, on the argument that `Your update has failed.` is the title being
spoken and a sound under it would be the system's.

I think the ruling is right and the pick is the more tempting of the two. **But it is his to reverse** —
and if he does, the more interesting option is the one already on the table: not a swell, but the
ball's own music, heard clean for the first time.

### 3. Sixty sounds is more than this piece can carry
Adopting all of them would make a work that dings at everything, which is a different piece — and a
noisier one than *"one voice that never stops talking to you is surveillance."* The tranche below is
13. The rest stay in `data/audio/sound_candidates.tsv` with his intent recorded, to be drawn on as
beats need them.

## §5 · WHAT IS STILL MISSING, and it is the thing he asked for last

> *"We probably also need sound composition for the overall stages of the experience, to accompany the
> space while we play the acts and Era."*

The **beds exist** — four room tones and the building drone, synthesized (`tools/make_tones.sh`). What
does not exist is anything that **plays** them: `TapeAudioBus` has no `setBed()`, the relocations have
no crossfade hook, and `ball.ts` never plays its own room. That is **S109**, it needs no further
assets, and it is now the single thing standing between this piece and being audible era to era.

⚑ **And a composition is not the same object as a bed.** A bed is a floor you stop noticing; a
composition is something the piece is *saying*. The eras have beds now. Whether they should also have
music — under the acts, not only inside the fiction's own media — is a real design question this
document does not settle, and it should be answered before anyone commissions or generates anything:
the work's whole thesis about Era 4 is that *the quiet is what L fills*, and a score under it would be
filling the quiet ourselves.

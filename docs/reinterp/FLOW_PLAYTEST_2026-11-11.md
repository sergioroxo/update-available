# Flow playtest, 2026-11-11 (build 53ce974d, speedrun cut)

Method: `tools/walk.mjs` (full walk, 2500 s, reached the Close) plus a passive sampler I added to a copy of the walker. Every 250 ms it logged each canvas's text (`__textNow`), `__guide()` and `__graceQueue().phoneView()`. A screenshot was taken every 3 s. Times below are seconds from the walk start. Era times: 1997 0-471, 2003 471-1039, 2016 1039-1317, 2026 1317-2500.
Screen rects are in 1280x900 screenshot pixels, not canvas pixels.

## Summary
- 2016 has the worst flow. The Handset Window opens wide on its own while Vera is on the board. It covers the right third of the board and the right edge of the "This week" card (its Close button is cut). This is his screenshot, reproduced.
- Two PRIORITY ALERT cards in 2016 stay about 4.5 s. Each needs about 9 s. The second one also sits on top of the Review panel it describes.
- The Orla photo arrives as a banner over the top of the group thread while the cascade is on screen, then the "Important Changes" update window opens 11 s later over the Handset Window.
- 2003: the Restorify "Accountability" panel covers the bottom of the messenger, which is where the newest line lands.
- Short timed text is mostly in the era-change changelogs and 2026 chat lines. Many 2026 ones belong to job 15.
- The walker presses every 2.2 s, so it hides "too fast" for anything a press ends. I only trust "too fast" where nothing was pressed.

## Measured vs judged
Measured: all times and word counts above, taken from the sampler (250 ms resolution) and `__guide()`. Need = 2 s + 0.4 s per word. Rects are read off the screenshots.
Judged: that a text was "auto-dismissed" (I did not find the timer in code). Where I could not see the text in a frame I said so (F08, F15). Which text is "wrong" in order (F03, F10, F11) is a judgement.

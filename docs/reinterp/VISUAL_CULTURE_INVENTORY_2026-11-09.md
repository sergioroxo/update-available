# Visual-culture inventory, era by era (9 Nov 2026)

*For his article on visual culture (ARTICLE_COMPANION_2026-10-31.md §7d; asked in UIB_JOB_8_2026-11-09.md item 5). A working document. Built by reading the code, the data and the docs of the `uib/work` branch on 9 Nov 2026. Nothing was run, built or changed to write it.*

## How to read it

- **One table per era.** Columns: era · element · where it is in the piece (screen or prop, and the file) · the period reference it evokes (in words only) · what it does in the piece · source status.
- **Period references are words, never marks.** The piece uses invented marks only (CLAUDE.md). No real logo, wordmark, typeface file, icon or sound is in it. Every screen is drawn in code on one 2D canvas from the palettes in `src/desktop/theme/`. The exceptions are listed where they happen (generated art, CC0 sounds, his own songs).
- **Source status has two values.**
  - *documented in the project's sources* means a research or sources doc records the period thing itself (webrings, counters, a CD programme, a TV ad, a news fact). The doc is named.
  - *the piece's own design* means the look is the piece's drawing. Where his own brief or reference commissioned it, the brief is named in brackets. A look can be "own design" while the practice it dresses is documented. Where that matters, the row says so.
- **Fonts: none are shipped.** `src/desktop/theme/fonts.ts` names one face stack per era. It names "Fixedsys Excelsior" (1997, 2003), "Roboto" (2016) and "Inter" (2026) first. But `public/assets/fonts/` does not exist, so those names never resolve. Each browser falls through to its own nearest system face (Lucida Console, Menlo, Consolas, Courier New, or a system sans). The look is therefore "a bitmap-style bold monospace" and "a platform sans", not the named faces.
- **Rows marked "restyled in UiB job 8 (T44)"** are the 2016 GracePlatform and Handset Window, and the 2026 Kettle, browser and bot. They were restyled on 9 Nov: 2016 to the flat "material / iOS 7" idiom, 2026 to the soft rounded AI-assistant idiom. The rows describe the new look; before and after photos are listed at the end.
- **Row counts:** 1997: 38 · 2003: 30 · 2016: 26 · 2026: 23 · Close: 9 · across the eras: 13.

## Notes for the article

- **The honest line on "documented".** The piece documents the practices and the web grammar. It draws the looks itself, to its own palettes. Only these elements are traced to a sources doc for the thing itself: the 1997 page grammars, the yes.gif advert genre, the counters, webrings and directories, the CD, DVD, mp3 and testimony forms, the TV-ad funnel, the 2016 news, the 2026 AI failure, the waiting-list and refusal facts.
- **The ageing is the argument.** The same room is repainted by the OS of each year; the transition is an update, and the room is the thing being updated. STYLE_PROPOSAL_2026-09-27.md says it in one line: "The room is drawn in the era's own interface."
- **Three kinds of surface.** The programme's own (may glitter, charm, play; the satire is the seller's and it collapses). The person's (bare: no Lamby, no satire). The frame's (functional, undecorated: the menu, the Leave page, the Restart card).
- **What is not in it, on purpose.** No real logo, no real OS sound (stated in `tapeAudio.ts`), no verbatim testimony, no real person's likeness. The goal picture is a drawn stock figure.

## The rule for this job (from his notes; CLAUDE.md's "one voice at a time" already says half of it)

1. **No text leaves the screen by itself before a slow reader has read it.** Hold at least **2 s + 0.4 s per word**,
   or wait for a press. The by-design exceptions stay: the 2016 cascade's speed ("outnumbered") and deliberate
   stillness. Each exception is named in a comment.
2. **Nothing opens on top of something being read,** and no window covers another's controls or content while both
   are live.
3. **One voice at a time,** in the story's order: a card, then its close, then the next voice. Never three surfaces
   changing within a few seconds.
4. **A clock jump is a visible passage of time,** never a silent 21:49 → 22:51.

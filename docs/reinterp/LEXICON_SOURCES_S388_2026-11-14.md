## 3. What differs from the job's wording, and what was not found

1. **Eras.** The job guessed 1984-1998 (envy) and 1984-2001 (isolation). The documents show less. The handbook's first
   edition is © 1984, but the copy in the archive is the 1995 revision, and nothing seen shows the envy and isolation
   passages were in the 1984 text. The earliest copyright on a text whose envy passage was **seen** is 1993 (PR-06);
   the manual's latest dated item is June 1994; the group-meetings booklet was revised in 2001 (first 1990, contents
   of the first edition not seen). So envy 1993-2001 and isolation 1994-2001. If he knows the 1984 edition carried them,
   the start moves to 1984. (The research file's "B2 F13 © 1984, the earliest dated text of all three" is the same
   assumption; it is flagged in each row's `_verify`.)
2. **B2 F14 is not an Exodus booklet** and has **no slide panel**: it is New Hope Ministries' *Establishing Group
   Meetings* (Love in Action's founder; first published 1990, revised 2001), filed in the Exodus series. The envy and
   isolation lines are running text on PDF pp. 41 and 15. Its isolation passage is about the isolation of the gay life
   ("a society of the isolated"), not a boy's, and the row says so.
3. **Class manual pages.** The job says "pp. 284-286" for Envy: the Envy chapter is printed pp. 285-286 (the page
   numbering runs at the foot; p. 284 is the end of Fear) and PDF pp. 308-309. Isolation, printed p. 295, is PDF p. 318.
4. **#58: the "stoked demand" sentence is the summary's own wording.** In the CAN-SG page it is not in quotation
   marks. The quoted Cass words are "It is not possible to attribute causality in either direction to this
   association" and "a lower threshold for medicalisation". The line is not found in the final report itself, so the
   row's `_verify` says so. MedPage Today is a both-readings source for the clinical debate and does not discuss
   availability as a driver of referrals; the `src` says so in plain words. Both readings, never satirised.
5. **#49:** the exact phrase in the interview sits in "other pathways to lesbianism which don't involve the narcissistic
   family system", straight after the page's narcissistic-mother pathway. The `src` quotes it as it stands.
6. **Dates before a row's `era`:** #36's source (2001) is older than the row's 2003 start; #39's (2001) is older than
   the row's 2016 start. The `era` is his and was not changed.
7. **Not found:** feminism as a cause and "older lesbians" (#48, stays open; "Origins of Lesbianism" is not stretched
   to it); any claim-side source on envy or isolation as a cause; the Cass final report's page; the Wayback capture of
   the interview (taken from §7F); "gay activists" in the men's booklet.
8. **Names.** No person is named in any `src`, `reason` or card field. #39 names the 1983 book by title only. The

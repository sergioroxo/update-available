## 1. What the plan was

The PRD is the plan for **the whole SurvivingSOGICE project**, not for one artwork. It says the platform "is not a
traditional archive" but "a creative input dataset that feeds multiple digital storytelling outputs", "closer to an
i-Doc or interactive web documentary than a database interface". It has three layers:

| layer | in the plan's words |
|---|---|
| **Archive** | "a tagged, structured corpus of pro-SOGICE and anti-SOGICE documents", with an AI research summary on each record, building toward "a navigable SOGICE knowledge base"; the PhD's analytical backbone |
| **Lexicon** | "a living glossary of SOGICE terminology, euphemisms, slurs, pseudo-diagnostics, and coded language", "a SOGICE Wikipedia" |
| **Experience** | "interconnected interactive digital storytelling modules, navigable as a unified environment styled as a retro computer/OS shell. Individual modules are 'applications' that open and link to each other." The navigation metaphor: "an old computer interface — the user moves through it as if exploring a machine that holds suppressed information." |

**The one law for every module:** "darkness is earned through compliance, not announced. The platform should feel like a
polished commercial product at the start. Horror accumulates through mechanism."

**The dates:** June 2026, a work-in-progress showing at CDN Pride Bergen (JUST CHANGE™); October 2026, "Full
exhibition, public archive live".

## 4. What the landscape shows

- **In April the PC Simulator was one module of nine**, one line long. By June it had become the frame for the others:
  the search, the livestream, the courses, the media and the archive all became things that happen inside its four
  computers. The plan's "retro computer/OS shell" for the whole platform is what the piece became on its own.
- **The law came first.** "Darkness is earned through compliance" is written in April for every module. It became the
  piece's tone law ("victim side grounded ('darkness earned')", CLAUDE.md), and its first trace is March's "Maximum sugar
  → earned darkness" (`T63_SWEEP_JANUARY_TO_NOW_2026-11-26.md`).
- **Both milestones held.** The June showing was prepared (the CDN Pride decks and handouts are dated 1–2 June). The
  October exhibition became the piece's opening (15 October).
- **What the piece refused from the plan:** the testimony view, and the empathy frame of the earlier proposal.

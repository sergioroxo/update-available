## How to jump ahead

- **The menu (any build):** open the menu → **Where you are** → **Go here** under the year → **Jump**.
  Three presses after the menu is open. It lands at the start of that year, seated.
- **The team skip (only with `&debug=1` in the link):** press the controller's **B or Y ten times within five
  seconds**. It jumps to the start of the next year (1997 → 2003 → 2016 → 2026) and says "Team skip → …".
- Inside a year there is no jump: 2026 has to be played to reach the Commons.

## The route (about 40–60 minutes)

### 1. 1997 — sharper text (T57, decides T09), 10 minutes

1. Open `https://sergioroxo.github.io/update-available/?reinterp=1&quad=0&xrscale=1.4&debug=1`, enter VR, let the
   room settle, Recentre from the menu. Read the small labels on the 1997 monitor (Profile setup, the Family Form),
   first from the seat, then leaning in.
2. Fresh load: `…?reinterp=1&quad=1&xrscale=1.4&debug=1`. Same labels, same distances. Is the text sharper? Is there
   only one picture? Does pressing a button still hit the right place? Put a hand in front of the monitor: does the
   text stay behind it?
3. Say which one is better. **Keep going in the better one.**

### 2. 2003 — Lamby's video (T10), 5 minutes

1. Jump to 2003 (menu or team skip). Play Lamby's video. Hold your head still and move the controller across the
   screen and off it. Then the same with hands. Does the picture break up?
2. Optional: the forum (Harbor) → "For parents: when he comes home" (new today). Readable?

### 3. 2026 — the Commons (job 11), play to the end

1. Jump to 2026 and play through to the Commons.
2. **The host as a hologram:** how does it look? Is it too near or too far?
3. **"Let them in" and the raid:** can you read the tags from the seat (the small labels on the glass and the
   screen)?
4. **"Show me" → the backstage, then "Back":** do the two fades feel right? Do you know where you are?
5. **The crowd behind her:** does the gap in the crowd read?
6. **The length:** the ending is about 75 s longer. Too long?
7. **The Close:** the card "The AI host" ("listened to you like a friend, and filed you as a lead"), and the 2016
   card "The places list".

## What this job is, and what it is not

- **Controllers are how the piece is played.** Every line that teaches pressing in the headset names the trigger first.
- **Hands stay exactly as they work today** (pinch, fingertip touch, the wrist Menu tab). No change to the hand code.
- **The first time someone presses with a hand, one calm line asks for a controller, once per visit.** The Quest
  switches to hands by itself when the controllers are put down or a battery dies; the line waits until the hand is
  actually used, so a visitor who puts the controllers down to watch a film never sees it.
- **Not this job:** the hand improvements listed in T96 (the headset's own pinch as the press, a cursor that closes with
  the fingers, a softer hand, a hands-only test). His "we can work on it then": they wait until he asks.

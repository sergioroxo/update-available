## The idea in one line

**Every job Vera does to other women on the platform, the phone beside her does to her.** She cuts a woman's
friend out of a testimony, and her mentor texts her about her own friend. She blurs a bar in a video, and Orla
invites her to that bar. She deletes the news about Malta, and Bea sends her that news. Each job is the platform's
rule. Each message is the same rule arriving in her own life, from people who love her or claim to.

## The rules for every connection

- **The player never needs to look away:** the phone is mirrored on the same screen (the Handset Window).
- **One voice at a time:** a message arrives only when a job has just been finished, never during one (the cadence
  fix).
- **Every job ends on one plain line of real information:** the "educational" layer, with a source, in the
  platform's footer (a "Why this rule?" line), never in Lambient's mouth. Documented facts are marked documented;
  the platform's inner workings are marked as the piece's inference.
- No free text. Felt threads stay bare. Never satirise the women's words.

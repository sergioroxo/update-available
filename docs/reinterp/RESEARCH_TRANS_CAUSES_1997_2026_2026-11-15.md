## 0. The evolution in one paragraph, and the ten strongest findings

**The evolution.** In the programmes' own teaching, being trans was first **the deep end of the same wound**: a 1983
theology the ex-gay movement adopted put "transsexualism" at the far end of the homosexual spectrum, the same family
"defensive detachment" in a stronger dose, and Exodus's 1988 telephone manual told volunteers to look for the "hurt,
rejection and sin", usually in the family, behind a "false identity". In the 1990s and 2000s **gender was the root** and
homosexuality the fruit ("a gender identity problem"), so the work was **prevention**: spot "gender identity deficits"
in the Sunday-school class, "afraid your child may be struggling with gender identity?". Around 2013–2015 the gay
teaching **dropped the roots** (Exodus closed; a 2014 Baptist summit told pastors to stop saying gay people were abused
as children; the target became "identity") while the **roots were handed to gender**: by 2019 a ministry newsletter says
the building blocks of being trans are those of homosexuality "only deeper", then adds a new outside cause, **social
contagion**, which ministry writing had already pictured in 2016 as a spore in the family's bloodstream. From 2019 the
cause moves out of the house and into the world: **"gender ideology"** in schools, media and law (a label born in
1994–95 opposition to UN conferences), with **trauma and mental-health "comorbidity"** brought back as the old abuse
root in new words. In 2024–26 it is sold to **parents** as a course (the same Tree Model "applied to a gender confused
generation"), a summit, a prayer list, a coaching call and, from late 2025, a **chat window** open all night. Nothing
was dropped on the way: the reasons **accumulate**. Meanwhile the classifications moved the other way: homosexuality
out of the manuals (1973, 1990), "gender identity disorder" renamed (2013), gender incongruence moved out of mental
disorders (adopted 2019, in force 2022), and "rapid-onset gender dysphoria" in neither manual and, in a 2021 coalition
statement, "not under consideration" for future editions.

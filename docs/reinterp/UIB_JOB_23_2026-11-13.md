## Why this tape (his direction, 13 Nov)

The 1997 intake corner (the wall record, the terminal, drawer 12, the suitcase) does not read for visitors. An Opus
critique is in `docs/reinterp/INTAKE_CRITIQUE_2026-11-13.md`; read it. He rejected opening a file and rejected packing a
bag ("it sounds like gamified simulation of life… feels a bit cheap"). His rule:

- the information must come from the system, told by the system;
- if something happens behind the visitor, they must be able to do something there, which is what justifies turning
  them round.

He asked: "what is the important teaching moment here? what is the process here?"

- **The process** the piece already shows in 1997: contact, then intake (the questionnaire turns his answers into a
  case), then a cause handed to him, then his family recruited (the mentor calls his mother; the parents consent),
  then accountability (the pledge, the journal, the mentor), then a summer placement with a date.
- **The teaching moment:** this pipeline is dressed as care. His answers become a file, a cause is given to him, his
  family is recruited, and his own consent comes last and changes nothing.

**The idea he chose:**
1. After the questionnaire, Un-Walk tells the visitor that their orientation is playing behind them.
2. A small TV with a VCR on the cabinet behind them clicks on, and the piece turns them round. They never leave the
   seat.
3. Lamby hosts a short orientation tape that walks through the steps. As each step is named, the matching thing in
   the corner happens in front of them.
4. The tape ends on a small form brought to the eye: Parent ✓ · Pastor ✓ · Mentor ✓ · You: [ OK ]. Pressing OK says:
   "Thank you. Your confirmation was not required."

**His rulings:**
1. **Lamby is the host.**
2. **It comes early,** after the questionnaire, so the tape explains what is coming.
3. **The parent's consent is a tick,** not a handwritten signature.

**But first, the tape alone, so he can understand it.**

## It must not be a copy of the opening film

The opening film, "LambyOS — Welcome Home" (`public/assets/video/lambyos_welcome_home.mp4`, 93 s; its brief is in
`docs/reinterp/PROMPTS_CODEX_2026-10-11.md`, "lambyos-advert-video"), is an over-the-top 1997 **infomercial**: star
wipes, spinning pedestals, chunky 3D type, a product parade, a narrator, and an ending that collapses into "SOLD AS
CARE!". Watch it first, then make this tape its opposite in register:

- **An instructional tape, not an advert.** It doesn't sell the programme; it explains the procedure to someone
  already enrolled. Earnest, slow, low-budget church media of the 1990s, and quietly procedural.
- **The suggested look is a flannelgraph:** the felt board of Sunday-school teaching. Lamby is a felt cut-out on a
  felt board, placing felt pieces along a path, one per step. People are felt silhouettes with no faces: "you", the
  mentor, the family as a felt house.
  - Around it, the plain AV grammar of a church orientation tape: a character-generator title on blue ("TriedPath
    Fellowship · Orientation · Tape 1 of 3: Your First Steps"), numbered step cards, the VHS layer kept light
    (soft tracking, slight hiss), 4:3.
  - Opus may find a better period-true teaching look; if so, say why. It must stay distinct from the advert.
- **Shorter:** about 60–75 s.
- **Lamby has no voice** (CLAUDE.md, and the advert's rule). Lamby speaks in felt speech bubbles. If the tape needs a
  voice, use the fellowship's narrator, in a voice different from the advert's narrator: build-time TTS, Supertonic,
  register `apparatus`, through `tools/tts/render.py`. Burn captions in for every line.
- **Music, if any:** a quiet devotional pad or a loop from his own jingles, in
  `/Users/sergiogalvaoroxo/Pc_Simulation/Sources/Jingle_Brand New/` (credit them as his). No third-party music.

## The script (Opus; drafts for his eye)

A host's welcome, then five steps, each one felt piece placed on the path and one sentence. For each step, the tape
also says (in the storyboard, not on screen) **which object in the 1997 corner it will set off**, once this is built
into the piece:

1. **Your answers.** "Every journey starts with honest answers." On screen: three of the visitor's answers beside what
   they were filed as, from `data/strings/opening.json` `recaptions`. For example, "I keep a diary" becomes
   "self-monitoring: enabled". In the piece these will be the visitor's own answers; in this video use the guided
   walk's answers, and mark them as an example. *(Corner: drawer 12 files a card.)*
2. **The root.** "Every struggle has a root." The "causes" the programmes taught, drawn from the Lexicon's Reasons
   (`data/strings/lexicon.json`, and their sources). *(Corner: the wall record.)*
3. **You won't walk alone.** The mentor and the journal: accountability. *(Corner: the record's mentor line.)*
4. **Your family walks with you.** "We'll be in touch with your parents." *(Corner: "parent contacted", the consent
   on file.)*
5. **Summer is for new beginnings.** The placement, with a date: 12 July. *(Corner: the suitcase.)*

**The ending:** "Ready? Your family has already said yes!" The form comes up: Parent ✓ · Pastor ✓ · Mentor ✓ · You: [
OK ]. Then, after a beat: "Thank you. Your confirmation was not required." The tape ejects to snow.

# Weaponised care: a note for the article and the PhD (1 Nov 2026)

*A plain-language note. It states the concept in Sérgio's own words and shows where each era of the piece stages it. It
makes no claim beyond what his article and the piece's own files say, and the few outside sources are marked. Longer audit
and sources: `RESEARCH_2026_V2_WEAPONISED_CARE_2026-11-01.md`.*

## 1. The concept, in his words

"The usage of language of care and love and turned into an instrument of violence."

From his article on ex-gay documentaries, it is a cycle with three moves: **injury judged, correction offered, testimony
used as evidence.** It is self-feeding: the films "draw on truthful elements, such as loneliness, secrecy, faith, and the
pain of rejection, and repackage them as coercive reasons for correction". And on intent: he uses "weaponization" for "care
made to deliver correction, whatever its makers intend, even if through love".

Three things follow from that definition, and the piece is built on them:

1. **The care is real as language, and the truth in it is real.** Loneliness, secrecy and rejection are not invented by the
   system. That is why the cycle works.
2. **What makes it weaponised is the turn**, from comfort to correction. The kind sentence is the one that carries the demand.
3. **It does not depend on a villain.** "Whatever its makers intend." The piece therefore lets its systems sound good, and
   lets even their apologies be part of the machinery.

A search of the literature (1 Nov 2026) found no scholarship using the exact phrase "weaponised care"; the nearest work is on
coercion framed as care ("soft paternalism") in psychiatric ethics. The phrase here is his. This note makes no claim about prior use.

## 2. How each era stages the cycle

**1997, Daniel, the mentor and the form.** *Injury judged:* the programme tells him what he feels "has a name" and is "a
struggle". *Correction offered:* Un-Walk, the tape, the pledge, the mentor. *Testimony as evidence:* the Family Companion
form records every answer "for the file" and every route ends in the same referral; the private diary is flagged and cannot be
deleted. The care word is "loves you" ("Someone who loves you wanted you to have it"). The mentor's last turns show care
as the removal of choice: "you don't have to carry the decision, it's already made". The update screen is called "Terms of
Care" and says the program corrects "in your best interest".

**2003, Daniel, the companion and the testimony.** *Injury judged:* "Don't be discouraged, Daniel." *Correction offered:*
Lamby, the Daily Realignment, the video. *Testimony as evidence:* the "Your Story" tool starts each answer for him "from your
file" and tells him to "Keep it hopeful. Keep it short."; declining is "not available". The accountability alert says "Don't
be hard on yourself" in the same breath as it files the want as a conduct. The forum says "Accountability is an act of love".
This era carries the testimony chain: Daniel is asked to *produce* his testimony.

**2016, Vera, the platform and the list.** *Injury judged:* a woman's own story is the problem to be polished. *Correction
offered:* the correction list ("gentler words reach further", "every story should land"). *Testimony as evidence:* the
correction "Attach the invitation" writes "If I can change, you can too" into a woman's mouth. Here the cycle is clearest
because Vera is the person doing it: she turns testimony into institutional evidence. The platform's helper says "Nothing
you did was wrong" and, in the next line, reports the message. Care that arrives from outside (a mother's ham, an aunt's note,
a neighbour's jumper) sits unread behind the filter.

**2026, Maya, the companion and the agent.** *Injury judged:* the companion calls a month of fewer mirror-checks "progress".
*Correction offered:* a "support" agent, a restored photograph, a list of things "taken care of" (contacts muted, material hidden
"before you see it"). *Testimony as evidence:* in the planned redesign, the agent builds a "story" only from what she pressed,
and the session's recorded voice says "Nobody made me. I was helped." Here the cycle meets a technology that performs some of the
interpretive work automatically: the same act (a life turned into proof of change) moves from the person, to an editor, to a
machine. The companion's apologies file the old name again each time; the apology is the surface, the record is the truth.

## 3. What the counter-case is

In every era but the first, the piece holds a scene of care that fails none of four tests: **no condition, no record, no
request for a detail, no sentence about who she will be.** (2003: Caleb, "i just want to sit next to you and not be watched".
2016: the unread messages. 2026: Junie, "don't answer if it's too much".) These are the piece's way of saying what is *not*
weaponised care. They are quiet on purpose; the system's care is loud, warm and well-organised.

## 4. One documented parallel, for the article

The 2026 "restore your photograph" beat is fiction (no such tool was found). Its nearest documented relative is an account
(reported 6 Feb 2024) that edited women's photographs with AI into "modest" versions, without consent, captioned "keep your
dignity", and sent them to the women pictured. The framing was dignity; the act was correction. Treat this as one reported case,
not as proof of a trend.

## 5. What the note does not claim

It does not claim that anyone running a real service intends harm: the concept says intent is not the test. It does not claim
that the piece's 2026 agent, face-composite or record are real products: they are marked invented or `speculative`. It does not
add a definition beyond his; where it uses "counter-care", that is the piece's design term, not his.

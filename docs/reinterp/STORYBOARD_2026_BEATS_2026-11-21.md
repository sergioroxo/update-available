## 5. The prompts for GPT

**How to use them:** paste **the style block** once at the start of a new GPT chat. Then paste one scene at a time.
Each asks for three options. Nothing in them names a real organisation, app or person.

### The style block (paste once)

> I am storyboarding one scene at a time of an interactive VR and browser artwork. Please draw storyboard frames, not
> finished art. For each scene I send, give me **three different options** side by side, each one frame, each with a
> one-line caption under it saying what is different about that option.
>
> **The look, for every frame:**
> - First-person view of someone sitting at a desk in an 18-year-old's bedroom, early morning, still dark outside. We
>   never see the person.
> - Low-poly 3D, flat shading, soft edges, like a cosy indie game. Not photorealistic, not dark or horror.
> - The room is warm peach and dusty pink, with a wooden desk, a single bed with a pink cover, a plant, a night window
>   with a pixel-art city, a trans pride flag on the wall, a pixel-art poster that says "LIVE STREAM FOR TRANS YOUTH —
>   COME AS YOU ARE", and an October 2026 wall calendar. A boxy white retro TV sits on a small wall shelf.
> - The computer screen is the sharpest, most detailed thing in the frame: a clean, light, modern interface with
>   rounded cards and small text. The AI assistant is called "L"; its colour is a soft mint green (#9FD8CB).
> - All brands and names are invented. No real logos, no real apps, no real people, no religious paintings.
> - Keep any text on screen short and readable.
>
> Say "ready" and wait for the first scene.

## Part B (S376): the screens redrawn (the 2D half of the ten)

- **Redraw on the canvas:**
  - in the era's palette, and in the 5×7 font where that is the era's;
  - invented marks only: no real logos, likenesses or product names;
  - period truth before polish: 1997 is Windows 95, 2003 is XP, 2016 is flat mobile, 2026 is today.
- **The system's own media must be over the top** (his rule: "new versions must be over the top"). The frame stays
  plain and never plays.
- **Keep every hit rect and every published affordance:** the walker presses them. Keep every string in
  `data/strings/` (prefer data over code).
- **Check:** a before/after for each surface from the seat, on the computer and in the headset. `tools/text-angle.mjs`
  (job 18) stays green.

## Part C (S377): the room redrawn (the 3D half of the ten)

- **What to work on:** props, materials, and the two lights (warm is life, cold is the system), with at most 3 hero
  objects per scene. Story props still appear in narrative order, never at an era's first second.
- **How:**
  - measure the mesh, not the render: GLB materials are named and their accessors carry min/max;
  - never batch a prop whose material something writes to;
  - call `generate()` with numeric ids (the batch-duplication bug).
- **Budget:**
  - at most 75 draw calls by the debug plate's honest count (job 18 A);
  - at most 75k triangles;
  - a 72 Hz floor;
  - no realtime shadows;
  - texture uploads only when something is dirty.
- **Check:** a before/after for each surface from the seat, on the computer and in the headset.

## Rules for all three parts

- **Ethics:**
  - invented marks only: no real people, likenesses or logos;
  - no verbatim survivor testimony;
  - felt scenes stay bare, and a respite is never a trap;
  - the gender-exploratory clinical debate is not touched.
- **No runtime network calls, no storage, no permissions.** Every new asset gets a row in `assets/LICENSES.md`
  (`npm test` checks it).
- **Every change must be reachable.** Part A changes no code the player meets (new photography or measuring tools are
  fine, with tsc and `npm test` green). If Part A has to touch `src/`, `data/` or `public/`, it is walked too. B and C
  are walked once, at the end of C, from the entrance to the Close's Restart card, and the walked sha is the one
  merged.
- **Leave the festival ZIP alone.** It is built from job 18 B's commit. There is a new ZIP only when he asks.

## What this job is

2026 today is a set of tabs, a chat and a 3D stream to the side. His verdict on the headset (20 Nov, 22:41): "do you
realize that this is completely broken as a … flow, right? where is the flow of this?". Draft 3 rebuilds it as
**thirteen beats in three acts, one company, rising harshness**:

- **Act I, L** (what AI already does): beats 1–4.
- **Act II, Second Thoughts** (what AI is starting to do): beats 5–8.
- **Act III, Shepherd Live™** (what AI will soon be able to do): beats 9–13, ending in the Close.

Every beat in the draft says what causes it and what it leads to. **Build the causes, not only the screens:** each
beat starts because of the one before it, never on a clock alone. The visitor's choices are real presses, and every
one leads into the same trap. Target length: **about 14–15 minutes** (today about 16).

## The four rules that hold for the whole era (draft 3 §2–§3)

1. **One monitor, two panes, always in the same places.** Left: the AI (L, then Second Thoughts, then Shepherd). It
   talks and reports there. Right: her world, whatever the AI opened (Kettle, Larkpost, the search, a story, the video,
   the stream). **No tabs anywhere.** One thing is open on the right at a time; a back arrow returns. **The AI's pane
   widens as it takes over**: a narrow column in Act I, wider in Act II, the whole screen and then the room in
   Act III. The friends push it back. No card is longer than three lines, and nothing pops up over what you are
   reading. The pane widths are the story; tune them, but keep the three steps visible.
2. **The Original ✦, in four stages:** told (Act I: "softened by L", press to see the original; L teaches it in the chat
   at beat 2) → locked (Act II: grey with a padlock, "original unavailable while Second Thoughts is on") → gone
   (Act III: no tags) → given back by her friends (beat 10: "1 unsent message restored").
3. **Memory belongs to Second Thoughts only** (first in beat 6). Each note is small (26-12: "much smaller") and opens into
   a card: *Memory · Used for · Shared with*. "Shared with" names Shepherd Live™ and HEDGE™ (parents).
4. **The voice (H6, his yes: "a voice for Second Thoughts and L only, a few short lines (Kokoro); never a person").**
   L's voice has a 🔈 switch from the first screen; Second Thoughts uses the same voice and switch; **Shepherd speaks
   in L's voice with no switch.** Only a few short lines are spoken; everything spoken is also written. Render at build
   time with Kokoro (memory `kokoro-via-mimikastudio`), register every clip (memory `rendered-audio-needs-registering`)
   and check each caption's hold against the clip's real length.

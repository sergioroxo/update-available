Word of the Day was checked separately: 1997/2003 vocabulary modal, 2016 Lambient language lane, and 2026 idle-orb phrase. These define terms rather than repeat the next action, so their words are unchanged. 1997/2003's modal now waits while Lamby has copy for the current step or a balloon is active; other existing silence gates remain. 2016's vocabulary lane yields to a balloon. The Close's Lexicon/dossier are untouched.

The map's current instruction paragraph is removed in both frame renderers. The frame helper now says only “Esc opens the menu.” Start retains its reference hints, ordered ticks and era jumps, but its NOW/room rows no longer generate instruction toasts. The separate “What to do”/“?” buttons and the era cards cannot open. Historical card code/data are retained unused; menu Close labels still use their existing data.

The console and stale Your Story invitation duplicates were confirmed during screenshot review. Deferred-update help now uses the actual update phase because the map has already ticked that transition.

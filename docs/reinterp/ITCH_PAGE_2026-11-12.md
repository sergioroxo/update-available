## Description (paste into the page's description)

YOUR UPDATE HAS FAILED is an interactive browser/WebXR experience tracing the digital evolution of SOGICE Practices
(Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity Change Efforts). Through different uses of technology, we discover Daniel,
Vera, and Maya's stories across four different eras: 1997, 2003, 2016, and 2026. This project reveals SOGICE not as a
single event but as an ever-changing digital ecosystem of persecution, promoted as care, spanning "ex-gay"
testimonies, promotional videos, exercises, prayers, and data systems.

Through fictional characters and satirical situations rooted in real documentation, the work exposes the complexity
and pervasiveness of these practices. The experience unfolds through a VR headset, desktop, or phone, as we click,
read, and turn to explore the rooms while the system tries to "update" us into a "new you". SOGICE's promise of
renewal through correction adapts through the decades, but its attempts to change LGBTQIA+ people will always fail,
since queer life will never cease to exist.

This educational experience was created as part of the research project "SurvivingSOGICE" conducted at the University
of Bergen, Norway.

**How to play**
- About 40 minutes. Play with sound on.
- On a computer (Chrome or Edge works best): click to act, and drag to look around. Esc opens the menu: controls,
  credits and sources, support, and leave.
- In a Meta Quest: open this page in the Quest's browser, press Run game, then Enter VR.
- Phones and tablets run it too, but they are the least tested.
- This is a work in progress. If something gets stuck, press Esc for the menu, or reload the page.

**Content note**
This piece is about conversion practices. It includes religious and family pressure, a parent's rejection,
anti-trans harassment and rhetoric, and an AI chatbot that manipulates a young trans woman. One documentary page
quotes a ministry manual on suicide. There is no graphic violence. The menu has a Support page with helplines, and
findahelpline.com lists free services in more than 175 countries.

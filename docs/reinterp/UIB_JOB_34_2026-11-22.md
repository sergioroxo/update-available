## What this job is

Job 33 got 2026's story right: three acts, one company, rising harshness, and it walks clean through both doors. Its
look is wrong. The one screen reads as a calm, flat, beige-and-mint web page. L and Second Thoughts look identical
(Second Thoughts only adds its name to L's title), and nothing about it says 2026 software. This job keeps every beat,
cause, line and timing of job 33 and **re-dresses the screen**:

1. **The desktop** becomes a modern operating system of 2026: invented, "inspired on" current Mac and Windows. It gets
   a menu bar, **a Menu button**, a dock, modern windows and notifications. L lives inside it like a modern AI chat app.
2. **The three products** each get a brand of their own, **louder each act**: L glossy and premium; Second Thoughts a
   loud yellow takeover of the whole desktop; Shepherd Live a TV show that covers it.
3. **Two out-of-date texts at the Close** (job 33's calls 18 and 19).

Do not change the story, the beats' causes, the voice lines, the walk's path or the era's length. If the new look
makes a beat read differently, say so in your report rather than rewriting the beat.

## The rules for the whole look

- **Invented marks only.** It may look like current software. It may show no real logo, name, icon or trade dress:
  no Apple mark, no Windows logo, no Claude or ChatGPT marks or names. **Window controls are our own, not the
  red-yellow-green trio.** The Menu button carries an invented mark, not a real OS's. The OS needs no name on screen.
  L, Second Thoughts™, Shepherd Live™, HEDGE™, Kettle, Larkpost and transjesus.str are the marks.
- **Colours: one new block, `OS26`, in `src/desktop/theme/era4.ts`.** His yes on 22 Nov to this direction is the
  colour law's yes for it: say so in the block's header, quoting T101. Put every new hex there, named by role. For
  Second Thoughts' yellow, reuse the sponsored card's yellow from Act I (S412), so the ad and the takeover are the same
  brand. Keep L's mint (`ERA4.l` / `WEB.accent`): **L must still look good** (`REINTERP_E4_THE_ARGUMENT_2026-08-05.md`
  §4.1). L is over the top in polish, never in menace.
- **Screens are smooth now.** The screen canvases are mipmapped and trilinear (his 9 Nov exception, CLAUDE.md), so
  rounded corners, soft shadows, translucent bars and large type are allowed on the glass. Draw them on the canvas, as
  everything else is. The room keeps `FILTER_NEAREST`.
- **Felt stays bare.** Junie's and Rae's words, Maya's own words ("There's nothing to restore. I'm Maya." and her sent
  note), her original ✦ and the stream's people get no brand, sticker, sparkle or score. They are plain cards in the
  OS's neutral style, and the AI says nothing while they are on the glass (job 33's rule).
- **Satire collapses.** The brands are the system's self-presentation and they must fall. At Remove, Second Thoughts'
  yellow peels off the desktop and the dock comes back. At SESSION TERMINATED, Shepherd's TV graphics glitch away
  (≤ 3 changes a second; reduced motion: a cut).
- **Gamification is diegetic only.** Scores, streaks, badges and stickers belong to Second Thoughts and Shepherd, in
  their voice. The frame never plays.
- **Budgets.** ≤ 75 draw calls at every moment. Render-texture uploads on dirty only: anything that moves on the glass
  (a ticker, a ring, a bouncing icon) steps at **≤ 6–8 Hz** as the stream does, never every frame. Measure the
  console moment and her photo's moment (26-14, 26-18) on the throttled profile before and after, as job 33 did.
- **Legible in the headset.** Check every beat's xr still: the smallest text on the glass must stay at or above the
  presentation rules' floor (V3). Bigger type beats more words.
- **Click and tap only.** "Ask L anything" is a composer you cannot type in. It shows L's own drafts or her chips, and

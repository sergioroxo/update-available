### The four points, for his answer

1. **Card 2, "three in four".** FRA's 76% is for *everyone* who went through it. After "for trans people", the
   sentence reads as if it were trans people only. Smallest fix, his words kept: "…twice as high for trans people,
   and of everyone who experienced it, three in four said they never agreed to it."
2. **Card 3, two words.**
   - "affirming trans **people**": the source is about children (the letter says affirming a *gender-dysphoric child*
     is "itself a form of conversion therapy"). Fix: "affirming trans **young** people".
   - "social contagion": the sources say this of a newspaper's opinion columns (Belgium, p. 73) and of a French Senate
     report (p. 141), not of the feminist or "LGB" groups. Fix, his words only reordered: "Though many LGBT+
     organisations support trans and gender-diverse people, the “gender ideology” campaign has taken root within the
     community, describing affirming trans young people as “conversion” and being trans as a “social contagion”.
     Groups calling themselves feminist or “LGB” oppose their rights."
3. **Card 6, the year.** The million signatures were collected from May 2024 to May 2025 and handed in in November 2025.
   The EU's answer came in 2026 (13 May). The old draft had the same mistake; it was mine. Fix, two words moved:
   "Over a million Europeans urged the EU to ban them. In 2026 the EU responded with a recommendation that no country is
   required to follow and declined to propose a law."
4. **Card 4, optional.** "Following it through four decades" has no one doing the following. Fix: "Followed through
   four decades, these practices persist: tape, website, feed, and conversational AI." Or keep it as it is.

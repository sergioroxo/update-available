## The one idea that holds the four together
**Each era's game is the apparatus gamifying the same thing: fitting a life into a shape.** Tetris-like in 1997
(force the odd pieces into straight lines), the snake in 2003 (the ministry's appetite, counted in souls), the
runner in 2016 (a streak you must not break), and in 2026 something as cosy as a life-sim — because that is what
2026 persuasion looks like. The satire is always the **seller's**: the game is the programme's product, never the
player's desire and never queer people or things (CLAUDE.md: satire only inside perpetrator self-presentation,
and it must collapse). Every game is **optional**, never required to finish an era, walker-safe (every control
published, nothing traps), and the **frame never plays**: no score of the piece's own, only the game's.

**The second reading:** a press on the handheld names its practice in the sentence line, as the corner does —
*"The exercise: gave you a task that could only end one way, and scored the attempt"* (practices.json `provotype`,
his approved wording). 1997's and 2003's games **file to the record** when played ("FIT IN played — 3 pieces
discarded"), because there the programme itself hands you the game. **FloppySheep never files** — S70's law: Vera
plays it at work, and the piece never scolds her for it.

## The four games

### 1997 · FIT IN — on Daniel's handheld (the GameBoy-like "Videogame" model)
- **Arrives in sequence:** the handheld is his own and lies on the bed from the start; the **cartridge** arrives in
  the starter kit's post (the kit "by post" is already the era's first beat). Until then the handheld shows only
  its own boot. *(Grounding: unlicensed Christian cartridges for the NES and Game Boy were sold in the 1990s —
  Wisdom Tree is the known publisher [VERIFY SOURCE].)*
- **Play:** a Tetris grammar on the 4-green palette. The pieces are odd, bright, irregular (never Tetris's seven);
  the well is a grey grid of straight lines. A piece that cannot fit is "discarded" with a cheerful chime; the
  score praises **"CLEAN LINES"**. After a few rows, the well itself shows what it wanted: a picture of a straight
  line of identical people (the 1996 "YES!" group photo's grammar, invented and pixel-drawn).
- **It collapses:** the odd pieces never stop coming, and the discard pile — drawn at the side — grows into
  the only colourful thing on the screen. The game has no way to end with them kept. (That is the provotype's
  law: *a task that could only end one way*.)
- **Files:** "FIT IN played — n pieces discarded".

### 2003 · REACH — on Daniel's phone (Restorify's own game)
- **Arrives in sequence:** after the first check-in (the plan's *CHECK-IN KEPT*), Restorify offers it: "Play REACH
  while you wait". On **his phone** (his decision).
- **Play:** a snake. **The snake is the ministry, not the player's desire.** It eats bright queer symbols — a pink
  triangle, a rainbow, two interlocking signs, a heart — and grows longer and greyer with each; the score counts
  **"SOULS REACHED"**. The symbols **stay bright and whole where they were**: each one eaten leaves its twin behind
  on the board. The snake can only get longer; it can never finish; the board fills with the snake itself.
- **It collapses:** at length N the snake has nowhere to go but into itself; the game over screen reads
  "REACHED: 0" — it never took anything away.
- **Files:** "REACH played — souls reached: 0".

### 2016 · FloppySheep — Vera's phone, in colour, with his song
- Keeps S70's grammar (one thumb, hop and one flap, gently faster, genuinely fun, never a reward, no ledger).
- **In colour:** the platform's pastel sky, the white sheep, the narrow path between two fences; the hurdles are the
  programme's own words for a life (*"a second glance"*, *"old friends"*, *"a song on the radio"*) — the seller's
  vocabulary, never people.
- **His song:** *FloppySheep — Out of Bounds (v3, Extended)* behind a ♪ button (off by default), the timed lyrics
  (his `.md` LRC) scrolling karaoke-style in the white space. Project-owned (his, via Treblo) — a LICENSES row.
- **A tension to settle (question 2):** DESIGN_NEXT proposed a *streak* ("days pure") and a *"LAPSE — report to your
  group?"* screen. S70 made FloppySheep the one thing in 2016 that never judges her. My recommendation: **keep S70** —
  the streak counter can be the game's own brag, but a crash just restarts the run.

### 2026 · TIDY — direct (on the Switch-like model, on Maya's bed) — REDESIGNED 2026-09-28
*(Superseded: "It is FIT IN again, thirty years later and gentle — which is the thesis of 2026." It is not. His
correction above.)*
- **The surface is cosy on purpose** — the "put everything in its place" genre of the 2020s, a room of Maya's things
  on a shelf grid — **because that is the disguise, and the game strips it.** Every object has a place except the
  ones that make her life hers (a binder, the letter with her name, the pride pin, the prescription), and for those
  the game offers one box.
- **The box speaks the campaigns' own words, named and cited.** Its label changes with each object: the documented
  claims of named actors (e.g. the claim, in LGB Alliance's own letter to MPs, that affirming a child is "itself a
  form of conversion therapy" — already on the dossier, e4_offers#2, at his word). Satire of the *seller* only; the
  gender-exploratory clinical debate stays unsatirised (both readings, unresolved).
- **Between rooms, a card says what the words did.** Not a score: a dated, sourced consequence — a law passed, a ban
  in force, a documented rise in reported hate crime — each with its status (`documentary | contested |
  speculative`) and its link, in the dossier's grammar. **Nothing goes on a card until his research verifies it**
  (prompt below). This is where the space educates.
- **It collapses:** the room is never "complete"; the last card names who published the game and what their
  campaign asked for. Nothing of Maya's is ever destroyed — the objects stay on the floor, bright, where she left them.
- **Files:** "TIDY played — n objects kept" (a refusal is filed exactly like compliance; the witness is symmetric).

   system shown to her one thing at a time (the board, a lesson, Lambient, the Handset Window, her record), each step
   on her press (no clock: his October rule and job 38's). It is the platform's own onboarding voice: warm, efficient,
   commercial, the charm the operable register allows, and it must collapse later (it does, in the review).
2. **The three lessons become the training's practice**, named as its steps ("Training · step 2 of 4 · …"), and the
   panel beside each lesson says what this step teaches and the one thing to do now, so she always knows what she is
   doing ("I don't fully remember what I had to do"). The lesson's one ask is the loudest thing on the page.
3. **Lambient's consent and the "This week" card fit inside the welcome**, not before or around it; nothing repeats
   what the welcome already said. Keep the count of Lamby's unsolicited balloons within the era's cap (≤ 8).
4. **What stays:** Vera's story, the lessons' content, the kit, Malta, the felt beats (bare), the record. Only the
   frame of the opening changes. Every new string in `data/` with a `_doc` line.

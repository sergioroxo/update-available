# VR — his first real WebXR test (24 Oct 2026) and the plan for an XR round (after CX3)
*His notes, then Claude's reading of each and what an XR round (CX4) should do. Nothing built yet.*

| # | His note | What it likely is | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Antialiasing: straight lines look dashed, "jaggies" | No multisampling in the XR framebuffer, and the monitors' pixel-art textures use NEAREST filtering with no mipmaps — crisp on a flat screen, shimmering in a headset | Request antialiasing for the XR session and a higher framebuffer scale; in XR only, give the screen textures mipmaps + linear filtering (keep NEAREST on the flat screen, the piece's pixel discipline) |
| 2 | The camera was fixed; I couldn't move around | The rig holds the authored seat pose, so head translation may be overridden (3DoF instead of 6DoF) | Seated 6DoF: the seat sets the origin once; the head's own position and rotation always drive the view; never write the camera transform while in XR |
| 3 | Start-up menu buttons very hard to click | The setup window is far / small in XR, and the controller ray has no visible cursor or hover | Bigger targets in XR (min ~4 cm at 1 m), a cursor dot where the ray lands, and a hover highlight on every pressable thing |
| 4 | Can it be hand-trackable? | WebXR hand input exists on Quest 3 and PlayCanvas supports it | Add hand tracking: pinch = select (as the trigger), with the same ray from the hand; poke for near buttons later |
| 5 | Could I hold the Game Boy and play it? | Today the handheld is "brought to the eye" by a press, not held | Grab with the grip button (or a hand pinch-and-hold): the handheld follows the hand; its buttons pressed by the ray from the other hand or by poke |
| 6 | Controllers need to highlight where to click; it is a seated experience; check the camera position | Same as 3; and the seat height / floor reference may be wrong | Hover highlight + cursor; a seated reference space (local, not local-floor), a one-time height calibration ("sit comfortably, press to set your seat") and Recentre in the XR menu |
| 7 | **No camera correction in VR — extremely nauseating** | The browser build's conducted turns, flights and "take the viewer there" moves run in XR too | In XR, the piece NEVER moves or rotates the view by itself. Every "take the viewer there" becomes, in XR: a fade to black → the scene re-arranged around the seated viewer (or the object brought in front of them) → fade in; or a glowing cue the viewer turns to. Flights (the restore, era changes) become fades. This is a rule (CLAUDE.md comfort), not a polish item. |

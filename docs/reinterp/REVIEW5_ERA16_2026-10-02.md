## Verdict on the question asked: does 2016 give lesbian women visibility, or use them?

**Mostly it gives it, but by the narrowest of doors, and it never says whose visibility it is.** What is good: there is a real ensemble of women with distinct voices (Renata, Cheryl, Priya, Bex, Hanne, Susan, Bea, Deirdre, Mairead, "s." in Cork, Aunt Cliona, Nuala's jumper, Annette asking where her daughter is). The group chat is warm and is never the joke. The satire stays on the machine. Nothing from Daniel's world or footage enters 2016 itself (the only Daniel in the era's text is one changelog line on Daniel's own screen at the hand-off). What is weak: the word "lesbian" appears in the era exactly once, as the word that gets struck out (ERA16-05). The minimum path gives the era's explicit lesbian story three presses (ERA16-06). And the era's two signposted side jobs are not about women at all (ERA16-07). The women are present as material for the machine; their world (the Wick, the jumper, the aunt) sits in an inbox the piece promises never to make you open.

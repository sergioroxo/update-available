# Article figures (31 Oct 2026)

*26 figures for the Digital Creativity manuscript, at 2560 × 1440. The pictures are in the project folder
`output/article-figures-2026-10-31/` (not in git: images stay local, like `out/`), with `_contact_sheet.jpg` to see
them all at once and `captions.txt`. Section numbers refer to `ARTICLE_COMPANION_2026-10-31.md`.*

*How they were made: figs 1–14 by `tools/stills.mjs` (the browser build, the visitor's seat or a free camera, era
jumps used freely — a still is a picture, not a reachability claim); figs 15–26 by `tools/xr-tour.mjs --hires` and
`tools/xr-layer-review.mjs --hires` in an emulated headset (desktop Chrome, one eye, head 1.2 m above the rig, a
100° view; the VR-layer features forced on for the camera). **No figure is from a real headset.** Regenerate with
`node tools/stills.mjs --port 3000`, then the two VR tools with `--hires --out <dir>`.*

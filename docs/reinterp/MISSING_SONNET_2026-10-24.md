# What seems missing — Part 3 (played 2026-10-24, built-in browser, ?reinterp=1)

**What this covers.** One fresh visit, using MAP.md as the route and not repeating Part 1's checks:
- Setup, all of it, on the "Yes, please" path.
- 1997: profile, Un-Walk steps 1–4 (Read, Pray, Pledge, Connect), Internet Setup, NetPilot, #stillstruggling, and Rob's first private-message lines.
- The Esc menu, Controls, Credits & attributions and "Where you are".
- A Jump to 2026, then Maya's browser tabs, L, and the start of Second Thoughts (step 1 of 5).

**What it does not cover.** I did not replay 2003, 2016 or the Close. Anything below about those comes from REPORT.md and MAP.md, and I say so where it does. Chat in 1997 runs about one line a minute, so I jumped rather than wait it out.

Each item says what a visitor expects or is told, and what is not there. Ranked: top group first.

## A. A visitor could get stuck, or be told something that is not true

1. **The Start list says to press things that are not on screen yet.**
   - "Rob's request — MentorRob has asked to message you privately. Accept the request." showed in NOW while the chat had no request. It arrived about 4–5 minutes later.
   - "The picture — MentorRob is sending you a picture. Accept it" showed before any picture.
   - Missing: a "wait, it's coming" line, or the NOW step appearing only when the thing is there.
   - Double-clicking MentorRob in the user list does nothing.
2. **The 1997 tip promises something that is not on the desktop.** "Did you know…" says "ROOTCAUSE.EXE, on the desktop." The desktop shows only A:\, Session and lamby_rig. Start's "Root Cause Digger" side trip only closes the menu (REPORT.md has the same finding).
3. **A development tool is on the 1997 desktop and in Start › Programs.**
   - The "Lamby Rig Lab" window reads "procedural canvas rig — placeholder test surface", with Mood and Animation buttons.
   - It is a menu of Lamby poses with no story use. It looks like leftover tooling in the shipped build.
4. **"Everything you can do is in there" (Lamby, teaching Start) is not true.** The back wall, the handheld, the Esc menu, the Lamby symbol and "Where you are" are all outside Start.
5. **Refusing is not possible in several places.** Each is marked in REPORT.md or seen again here.
   - The private-message prompt from Rob has "Ignore" greyed out; I pressed it and nothing happened.
   - The DCC prompt has the same greyed "Ignore" (REPORT.md).
   - "Not today" on the pledge still ticks Pledge as done with a "filed" stamp, and nothing in the window acknowledges the refusal.
   - Missing: the piece saying out loud that refusing is not available, or letting the log show the refusal.
6. **"Jump to…" marks eras as finished that were never played.**
   - After my jump from 1997 (stopped at the first private message) to 2026, "Where you are" showed 17 of 17, 12 of 12 and 9 of 9 done.
   - The "What it did here" entries counts were 18, 0 and 0.
   - The 1997 list ticked The Family Form, DIARY.TXT and The update, which I never saw.
   - "Your file" in 2026 would then be built on steps that did not happen. A fair visitor comparing the menu to what they did would be confused.
   - Jump › Close (from 1997) did nothing: no dialog, no message. Jumping to 2026 showed a confirm dialog ("You will go straight there and skip what comes between…"), which is good.
7. **A quiet visitor in 2026 never gets the Close** (from the Part 1 replay in REPORT.md, not re-run now). 2026 ends and the piece goes back to the stars with "Touch the screen to begin." Missing: any "this is the end" screen, the credits or the Restart card on that path.

## B. Waiting with nothing to do, and nothing to say how long

8. **Total time before the first control is very long.**
   - Setup: about 10 balloons and windows, then the advert of about 2.5 minutes with no Skip, then "All set / Start".
   - After Start the stars-to-room transition took about 2 minutes, with a "Restoring previous session…" strip and a black monitor. No skip.
   - Then three profile screens.
   - So roughly 5–6 minutes before the 1997 desktop. No sign anywhere of how long the whole piece takes.
9. **The prayer tape in Un-Walk runs about 3 minutes with no way to stop it or skip.**
   - Back is greyed, only "Minimise" works, and a hint ("Esc opens the menu.") covered the Amen button when it appeared.
   - Missing: a "Skip tape" or "I've read the words" option for someone who does not want to sit through it.
10. **Chat pace in #stillstruggling is about one line a minute.** After "hi. first time here." the next 4–5 minutes were Lume, Branwen and Rob talking. There is no indicator that something is coming, and Lamby did not say anything while I waited.
11. **Missing: save or "come back later".** The content note says nothing you do is kept. A closed tab restarts at the stars. That is by design, but the piece never tells a visitor that a break loses the lot, and never says how long it is.

## C. Teaching and guidance

12. **Setup teaches looking (drag, scroll, turn the device) but not clicking.** "How to look" covers camera only. The Esc menu's Controls window does say "Click to interact", but that is two steps away and not offered.
13. **The Controls window lists "W A S D" to look around.** I did not test it; the project notes record a ruling against WASD. Either the text or the ruling is out of date. No mention of the Lamby symbol or Start in Controls.
14. **Lamby's balloon in 2026 pointed at something not visible.** I was on L's tab. The balloon said "Press Search and let the engine finish it." and then "Search is right here." with no arrow, because Search is on tab 1. Missing: Lamby switching the tab, or saying which tab.
15. **"Look with your device" sits in the top-left on desktop the whole time.** On a desktop with no gyro it does nothing visible and no one has explained it.
16. **Esc and the Start menu do not behave the same everywhere.**
   - Esc sometimes needs two presses.
   - The Start menu will not close with Esc or a click on the desktop; only the Start button closes it.
   - A hint ("Esc opens the menu.") appears over buttons mid-scene.

## D. Content the piece offers but does not complete

17. **NetPilot's search box does nothing a visitor expects.**
   - It cannot be typed into (I typed; the text stayed "christian help homosexual").
   - "Search" opens the Un-Walk "Did you know…" window, not a search.
   - "Recent searches" (including "am i gay") all go to the same result page for "christian help homosexual".
   - Page 2 shows the same six results reshuffled under "about 1,240 documents".
   - It reads as a closed web, but nothing says so, and no result shows the visitor anything outside the programme.
18. **Maya's "Care" tab is nearly all live, but with gaps.**
   - "Three contacts muted" can be unmuted and "Show me" on "Sensitive material hidden" greys out. No new content appeared, so the visitor sees nothing they asked to see.
   - Junie's muted message ("you coming next week?") has no button at all, while the rows above do. (REPORT.md has the same, from the story's side.)
   - "Request a change" gives "Requested. We'll be in touch." and "Last requested 11 months ago". No answer is ever shown.
   - On "Your information", the pronouns and name rows have no edit control at all, which fits the thesis but nothing explains why.
19. **The visitor does not choose Maya's search.** Four suggestions are shown ("…parents I've changed my mind", "…doctor I want to stop", "…partner I'm going back", "…parents I'm trans"). The engine types the doctor one on its own. No way to pick, and "how do i tell my parents i'm trans" is shown but never followed.
20. **2026's laptop is half cut off in the default view.** L's lines ("Your session is resto…", "Step 1 — confirm your… on the monitor.") run off the right edge. These lines carry the instructions for steps 1–5, so a visitor sees only half a sentence unless they drag the view.

## E. Care for the visitor

21. **No support or help resources anywhere I looked.**
   - The content note names the subject (conversion networks) and the flashing, but not what the story deals with (coercion, being outed, religious pressure, medical stopping, deadnames).
   - The Esc menu, Credits and the Setup windows list no helpline or "if this is close to home" page. "Leave" opens "a different, plain page outside the story" but the menu does not say what is on it.
   - The Credits' source list (EU FRA, UN report, ILGA, Global Project Against Hate) is research, not support. The Close, where a visitor ends up, is the most obvious place for a plain list of support organisations; I did not re-check it for one.
22. **The content note appears after about five Lamby balloons, and Lamby's sound step comes before it.** A visitor sees "restoring a session / three people's sessions / thirty years of care" before the plain statement of what the piece depicts. Missing: the note first.
23. **Setup says "the programme keeps what you give it"; the Restart card and Credits say "nothing you did here was kept / recorded by anyone".** Both can be true (fiction vs real), but nothing separates the two voices for a visitor who reads them one after the other.

## F. Words that belong to the makers, not the visitor

24. **Credits use internal names: "Era 3", "Era 4", "Room 1 (Era 1)", "Era 4's docked monitor", "the Close's panels".** The visitor knows 1997, 2003, 2016 and 2026. The Credits' "sources & further reading" buttons are named The ball, Era 4's offers, Era 3's day, The Close's panels, and there is none for 1997 or 2003.
25. **"Where you are" shows each room's "What it did here — entries: N".** The wording "it" is not explained anywhere before this.

## G. Small things that read well and are worth keeping (so they are not lost in a fix round)
- "I'd rather not say" on the profile focus is honoured and logged as "non-response: logged as a choice".
- Karaoke lyrics on the tape, and sound captions in the strip, work with sound muted.
- Maya's file page logs the visitor's own clicks ("care action reversed at subject's request").
- The Jump confirm dialog says what will be skipped and that you can jump back.

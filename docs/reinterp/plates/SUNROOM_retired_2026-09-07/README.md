# THE SUNROOM — the record of it, kept

Sérgio retired the home environment on 2026-09-07: *"It is fine by me to not
continue with the sunroom, just save some pics for me."* These are it.

`homeEnvironment()` in `src/desktop/theme/era4.ts` — a pleasant default room,
"arranged for you", drawn on the canvas and parallaxed, never geometry. Six
plates at 3×: the five lean steps the turn could produce, and the standby glow
the device showed before it was worn.

⚑ **Its argument, which outlives it:** the apparatus can only ever show you a
picture of a room. Turn all the way round and it is the same window. That
flatness was never a limitation the era worked around — it was the thing the
era said, and it should survive into whatever replaces the Sunroom.

⚑ **And the thing worth not losing:** it was NICE. `REINTERP_E4_THE_SPACE_2026-08-06.md`
§4.1 — *"The headset goes on and it is better in here… The piece must let this
land honestly. No irony, no warning."* A corrective VR that announces itself is
a less frightening object than a kind one.

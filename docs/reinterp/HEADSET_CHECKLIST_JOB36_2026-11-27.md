## The line that asks for a controller (your "only when the controller is put down")

- [ ] **Nothing on the switch.** During a film, put both controllers down and wait a minute. The Quest switches to
  hands. Nothing appears.
- [ ] **The first hand press.** Press something with your hand (a pinch, or a fingertip on the screen). The press
  works, and one line shows under the monitor for 8 s: "This works best with a controller. Pick one up, or pinch your
  thumb and finger to press." Two even lines, no word left alone.
- [ ] **Only once.** Press with your hand again: nothing. Pick up the controllers, put them down, press with a hand:
  still nothing. (A Restart starts a new visit.)
- [ ] **A trigger takes it away.** While the line shows, pull a trigger: it goes at once.
- [ ] **Match my table** with one controller lying on the table and a hand pressing: no line.

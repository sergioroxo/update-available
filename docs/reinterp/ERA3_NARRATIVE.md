# ERA 3 — THE JOB
*2016 · Room 2 · Vera. **The narrative, beat by beat, for review.** Written 2026-08-21 against
INTERACTION-BASED PROGRESSION, NARRATIVE LAYERS and PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE.*
**⚑ Two devices only: the DESKTOP and the PHONE. The tablet is removed** (Sérgio, 2026-08-21).

---

# 0 · THE ONE SENTENCE
> **In 1997 the machine was used on him. In 2016 she is the one using it — and she is very good at it.**

Eras 1 and 2 put the player under the apparatus. Era 3 puts them *inside* it, on salary, with a queue.
⚑ **Nothing in this era accuses her, and nothing forgives her.** She is a lesbian editing other queer
people's testimonies for a living, and the piece's whole position is that **the work is ordinary** —
tidy, well-designed, and defensible one item at a time.

# 1 · WHY TWO DEVICES, AND WHAT EACH ONE IS FOR
This is the structural decision the rest depends on.

| | **THE DESKTOP** (Windows 7 Aero, ~2016) | **THE PHONE** |
|---|---|---|
| **is** | her employer | her life |
| **carries** | the correction list, the Story, the podcast running order, the course | Bea's messages, the livestream, FloppySheep |
| **register** | `operable` — tidy, charming, collapses | `operable`, but it is *hers* |
| **asks** | for judgement, repeatedly | for nothing |

⚑ **The desktop is institutional software and it should look it.** Aero is not a compromise — a 2016
NGO ran on Windows 7, and the truth of that is worth more than a slicker screen. **The phone is where
everything vertical lives**, because vertical content was already phone-shaped in 2016, and because
the split *is* the narrative: **the work is on the desk, and the world arrives in her hand.**

# 2 · THE FIVE PHASES

## PHASE 1 · INTRODUCE — "sign in"
**Purpose:** establish that she is an employee, and that the tool is pleasant.
**The player learns:** one verb — **APPLY / SKIP** — and that the record notes both.
**Mandatory:** sign in; apply or skip **two** corrections.
**Guidance:** *direct*. The card names the rule, the reason, the manual, the verse.
⚑ **The doubling is taught here, silently:** a corporate style guide and an invented scripture, set
identically, neither emphasised. **Nobody points at it. It is simply how the tool looks.**
**Optional:** read the verse at all. Most players will not, at first.

## PHASE 2 · REINFORCE — "the same rule, a different person"
**Purpose:** the job becomes rhythm.
**New knowledge:** the same rule lands differently depending on whose sentence it eats.
**Mandatory:** corrections 3–6 across Renata's testimony.
⚑ **The complication is inside the repetition:** *Remove the unresolved* cuts *"I don't know what that
minute means yet"* from Renata, and later cuts *"it keeps coming out as a question"* from Deb. **The
same tidy rule, twice, removing the only living sentence each woman wrote.**
**Guidance:** *contextual* — the hint retires; the cards carry themselves.
**⚑ SECRET (waiting):** skipping every correction changes nothing about the outcome and everything
about the record. **Nobody is told this.**

## PHASE 3 · COMPLICATE — the phone rings, and Noa arrives
**Purpose:** two things arrive that the queue cannot hold.
**Mandatory:** Noa's submission — the video, and corrections 8 and 9 on the same person:
*soften the flannel; brighten the voice*, and *possible gender confusion — route to exploratory
mentorship*. ⚑ **Both render. Neither is endorsed. Nothing resolves her, ever** — that is a binding
ethics law and any beat that answers for Noa is wrong.
**⚑ THE PHONE, first use:** at 23:14, **Bea**: *"did you see. malta. they made it illegal."* / *"not
here. but somewhere."*
**The room answers:** the light lifts. Nothing files. **⚑ Nothing in the Malta beat touches the
ledger** — the one moment in the era the system does not record.
**Guidance:** *environmental*. No hint fires. The phone simply lit up.

## PHASE 4 · COMBINE — the rest of the workday
**Purpose:** the queue was never the job. This is.
**Mandatory:** none of it. ⚑ **This whole phase is OPTIONAL, and that is the design** — a player can
finish Era 3 having only done the list, and will have understood the argument. The rest is what makes
it *hers*.

| surface | where | the act | what it says, without saying it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THE STORY** | phone | tap the sentence the clip starts from | *the forty seconds that performs is never the true part* |
| **THE PODCAST** | desktop | drag three clips into a running order | *an episode is other people's worst minutes, arranged well* |
| **THE COURSE** | desktop | read the module list — **and the price** | *the funnel stops being a metaphor at the moment you see the number* |
| **THE LIVESTREAM** | phone | nothing. it is on | nobody asked her to watch it |
| **FLOPPYSHEEP** | phone | one thumb, tap to hop | the same publisher's cheerful product, one tap away while a comment waits |

⚑ **The Story's suggested in-point is correction 3's own mark** — *"for one whole minute I forgot to
monitor myself."* **The clip the conference wants is the minute she was free**, and the beat never
mentions it.

## PHASE 5 · TRUST — the queue empties
**Purpose:** the horror of being caught up.
**Mandatory:** the last correction; the update arms from the count, not a clock.
**Guidance:** *none*. No hint, no prompt.
**The line:** *"You're caught up. No new stories waiting right now."*
⚑ **Nothing congratulates her and nothing accuses her. She simply finished.**

# 3 · NARRATIVE LAYERS
**SPINE (mandatory).** Sign in → 13 corrections → Noa → the queue empties → the update.
**Complete and coherent alone. ~10 minutes.** A player who does only this understands Era 3.

**BRANCHES (optional).** The Story · the podcast · the course · the livestream · FloppySheep · the
comments thread · the verses, actually read. ⚑ **Each REFRAMES rather than adds** — the course does not
tell you more about the funnel, it shows you its price.

**SECRETS.** ⚑ Currently almost none, and the piece is unusually well placed for them because **its
subject is a system that watches.** Candidates, all cheap, all thematic:
- **Skip everything.** The record notices compliance's absence and says so in its own flat voice.
- **Open the same testimony twice.** It has already been published. Your correction is already in it.
- **Do nothing for a long time.** Lambient asks, gently, whether she is alright — the apparatus's care
  and its surveillance being the same gesture.
- **Read every verse.** The record notes unusual engagement with the manual.

# 5 · WHAT NEEDS DECIDING — the review list
1. **The tablet's removal moves the comments thread.** ⚑ It is the *recruitment floor* — pinned
   templates, some of which silently flag an account for follow-up. **Desktop or phone?** My read:
   **desktop** — it is work, and the templates are her employer's words.
2. **Is Phase 4 really all optional?** I have written it that way. The alternative is making ONE of
   them mandatory so every player meets the job beyond the queue.
3. **Does the Story live on the phone?** It is vertical, so yes — but it means her *work* appears on
   *her* device, which is either the right thesis or a muddle of the split.
4. **The secrets:** are any of them worth building, and is the ledger the right place for all of them?

# 1 · ⚑ THE FAMILY CALLS ARE NOT A QUEUE ABOUT DAUGHTERS. THEY ARE A QUEUE ABOUT MOTHERS.

`ERA3_BUILD_PLAN.md` §3 specified: *"Return the family calls — mark a mother's message handled."* I was
going to build an inbox of messages **about named adult children**, with the worker filing updates
against their records. **The pull says that data relationship is not documented**, twice and explicitly:

> "I have not found evidence of a standard 2016 organisational CRM in which mothers are formally
> attached as 'referral sources' to adult children's treatment records."
> "I do not have evidence that family workers routinely disclose an adult participant's progress to
> relatives without consent."

And it hands back something better in its place:

> ⚑ "A family worker plausibly knows **the mother's story because the mother told the ministry**. She
> does *not* automatically know the estranged daughter's confidential disclosures. **Making that
> asymmetry visible would actually make your interface more faithful to the record.**"

**So the task is rebuilt around the asymmetry.** The mother is the client. Every field on the screen is
about *her* — her grief, her group, her follow-up, her prayer request. **The daughter has no record
here at all.** The mother asks where her daughter is and the interface has no field that could answer,
because answering was never the service.

⚑ **That is crueller than the version I was going to build and it is also true.** The system's harm in
this scene is not that it tells a mother to cut her daughter off — the record shows conservative
sources explicitly *rejecting* that advice. It is that it holds her in patience and prayer,
indefinitely, and never answers the question she actually asked.

**Retired from the design, on the evidence:** any "cut her off until she repents" template; any progress
percentage or treatment status; any routing of the daughter's communications through the organisation.
All three are `[VERIFY SOURCE]` or unfound.

**Kept, and now sourced:** "love without approval"; grace-and-truth, relationship-and-conviction,
acceptance-and-approval as the characteristic binaries; slowing the mother's urgency down (*journey,
pray, listen, remain connected, don't force, trust God's timing*); **grief as an organising concept**
(Portland Fellowship's family curriculum literally lists "Dealing with Grief"); and the vocabulary that
holds the daughter at arm's length — *your loved one, your child, the struggler, someone who has
embraced an LGBTQ identity*.

**The role has a real name.** Harvest USA's is "Parents and Family Ministry", and the worker is
explicitly **not required to be a licensed clinician**. Vera's job title in Era 3 should sit in that
family — ministry/support/discipleship worker, never therapist. Our mark stays invented; the shape is
theirs.

# 2 · ⚑ "EMOTIONAL DEPENDENCY" IS THE WOMEN'S-STRAND TERM, AND IT IS NOT A PARODY

The single most useful finding for §1's lesbian specificity. The women's ex-gay literature has its own
explanatory cluster — **emotional dependency, enmeshment, relational wounds, boundaries, femininity,
unmet emotional need, healthy relationships** — distinct from the masculinised "gender deficit / father
wound" material written for men.

> ⚑ "Do not flatten women's programmes into men's reparative-therapy vocabulary with pronouns changed."

**Binding on every line of Maiden-to-be and on the course module's copy.** A women's group talking about
building masculinity would be a research failure visible to anyone who has been in one.

The etiology itself is `[CONTESTED]`, not documented — father/mother wounds are taught by these
ministries and are **not established psychological science**. Any Dossier card must carry that status.

# 3 · ⚑ SURVEILLANCE IS MADE LEGIBLE AS CARE — and the piece already knew, by accident

> "Surveillance does not normally announce itself linguistically as surveillance. It is made legible as
> **care**. A person is asked how she is doing because somebody 'cares'; hard questions are an
> expression of 'accountability'… Covenant Eyes literally marketed automated behavioural reporting as a
> means of initiating trusted conversation."

This is Lambient's consent card, described by the record before the record was read. **It is now
sourced rather than intuited**, and the register it licenses runs through the whole era: the correction
list's rationales, the group's check-ins, the family templates. Nothing in Era 3 should ever *sound*
like surveillance. It should sound like someone kind asking how you are.

⚑ **And the piece's own first correction is now documented.** Renata's rule 1 replaces "lesbian" with
"same-sex attracted". The pull records exactly that move as the movement's characteristic euphemism —
"the person is often linguistically separated from the sexual identity" (Robinson & Spivey 2015). That
correction was written before this research and it turns out to be the real thing.

# 4 · WHAT THE GROUP CHAT MAY NOT CLAIM

⚑ **Marked `[VERIFY SOURCE]` and therefore off-limits as documented fact:**

- **daily phone-group check-ins as a standard ex-lesbian form** — the adjacent evangelical purity
  ecology has daily accountability; the ex-lesbian group version is not sourced;
- **terms of direct address** — "sister", "sweet friend" and the rest are culturally plausible and not
  established. ⚑ Maiden-to-be will use ordinary names, because that is what the evidence supports;
- **greeting formulas, emoji practice, response-time norms, screenshot norms**, and what happens when a
  member goes quiet;
- **WhatsApp specifically**, as a movement norm in 2016;
- **any escalation ladder** — first lapse → warning → sponsor call → removal. There is no such rulebook
  in the record and inventing one would be the caricature;
- **a movement-wide word for a woman who leaves.**

**What IS sourced and should carry the group:** closed membership with a facilitator (not a leaderless
circle); accountability that covers feelings, triggers, attachment, identity and family as well as
acts; disclosure sometimes to one designated person rather than the whole group; a lapse met with
confession, prayer and continuing rather than expulsion; and progress language that is processual —
*healing, wholeness, freedom, walking out, transformation, identity in Christ*.

# 5 · ⚑ THE CARICATURES, AS A CHECKLIST TO WRITE AGAINST

Straight from the pull, and every one of them is a thing a writer reaches for:

| the cliché | why it fails |
|---|---|
| the group as junior prison guards — numbered confessions, denunciations, infractions | no documentary basis; the programmes foreground confidentiality, prayer and trust |
| **constant cruelty** | ⚑ "the troubling quality of these systems is precisely that warmth, friendship, vulnerability and surveillance can be materially genuine at the same time" |
| the women as dupes believing a theory fed to them | motives are religious, relational, identity-bound; efforts often lasted years |
| macho reparative vocabulary with pronouns swapped | see §2 |
| a worker gleefully telling mothers to cut them off | conservative sources explicitly rejected estrangement as a strategy |
| a quasi-medical call centre issuing progress percentages | the family ministries are **parallel pastoral systems**, not clinics |
| mothers as agents deployed by the organisation | they arrive frightened and grieving; that vulnerability is *why* the ministry has leverage |

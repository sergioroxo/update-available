## A. 2016 as a chain of her actions: if this, then that

**His words, 26 Nov (after the reading):** "I meant the real time of the day. I know this was related to the timing of
the Malta releasing the law but it simply doesnt work the time aspect so we just keep the day element." And: "did you

**The automatic is 2026 not 2016.** lets please fix the logic for the 2016 flow — think of cadency of actions, ifttt -
if this than that".

**The rule for 2016.** Every step of the story happens because she did something (a press, a finished job, a page
closed, a message read). **Nothing on the phone or the monitor opens by itself.** News comes the way a 2016 phone
brings it: it lights, it sounds once, a banner or a badge shows **in view at the seat** (the Handset Window beside the
monitor), and she opens it. The automatic, the system acting without her, belongs to 2026. In 2016 only the platform's
own work on a thread she has opened runs by itself (the removal, the forwards): that is the system acting, inside what
she is looking at. The fiction's own times stay (a typing line, a message's reading hold, the removal's steps).

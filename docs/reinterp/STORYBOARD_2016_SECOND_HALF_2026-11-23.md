**In one line:** on her first shift, GracePlatform trains Vera, lesson by lesson; then it shows her a memory of her own
2014 story, and she writes the line it cut; then Malta votes, the network answers with her story, and the women in her
group outrun the platform that is deleting the news.

**Three acts, one evening:**

| Act | What happens | What the platform is |
|---|---|---|
| I · The training | Three lessons. Lambient shows each one, explains it in a line, and lets her do the next. | a tutorial: patient, kind, and it does the job anyway if she will not |
| II · The memory | Her 2014 story comes back as a "memory". Under it, her own words, as the platform cut them. Then her Notes. | the archive that keeps her story and edits it |
| III · Malta | The news in her group. The network's campaign kit, with her story in it. The platform deleting the news, and the women forwarding it faster. | the review that does not need her any more, and still fails |

**Her reluctance** is never said in her words. It shows in what she does and what she leaves undone: she can Skip a
rule (and watch Lambient do it for her), leave the Wick off the list, leave Annette without a script, say Not now to
the tape and to the memory, take her story out of the kit, forward the link, and never answer Mam or go to the quiz.

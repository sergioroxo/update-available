### A-1 · ⚑ RESOLVED BY THE SPY RUN — the headset works; the walker opened the pause menu on itself. Two real defects fall out of it — **now**
**What happened.** After L's third laptop line the walker pressed `icon-send` — **Era 2's desktop icon,
still registered on the OS canvas in Era 4** (logical 29,216). Projected through the visor plane it
lands at page **(38, 854)**, which is inside the frame's pause button (`II`, bottom-left, 14–47 × 853–885).
The game menu opened, and every later press — the headset, the visor plane, the laptop — hit the
`#reinterp-game-menu` DIV and never reached the canvas. Observed with spies on `pointerdown` targets and
on `E4Shell.wear()`: `wearCalls: 0`, `pointerdown target: DIV#reinterp-game-menu` for every press after
step 236, `visorEnabled: true`, stage `closed`, pose x 4.4 y 1.16 z 0.7 yaw 270 pitch 0
(`laneA/spy/WALK_2026-09-02.json`, steps 229–246). The first played run did the identical thing
(`WALK_2026-09-02.json` step 235, same point). The jumped runs had no `icon-send` to press, so they wore
the headset. **The piece's door is fine.**
**What is wrong, both observed:**
- **A stale live control.** `icon-send` (and Lane C's list of E3 rects) stay registered on the OS canvas
  in Era 4, where the OS canvas is the visor. A control published as live on a surface that no longer
  shows it is the class S94 named ("advertising controls it will not accept"); here it is worse, because
  the visor projection puts it under frame chrome. Fix: the E4 branch of `os.draw` must clear `hits`
  before pushing `e4-touch`, and E2/E3 desktop icons must not register past their era.
- **The pause button sits over the headset.** From the r3 seat the visor's lower-left corner projects
  to x ≈ 30–200, y ≈ 805–890 (`shots/034…closed….png`); the `II` button occupies 14–47 × 853–885 of
  that. A player pressing the headset's lower-left opens the menu instead of the era. The motion button
  ("Look with your device", 15–191 × 763–793) is also on top of the strap. Frame chrome must not sit
  where the era's one interaction is; move the corner column or keep it clear of the visor's projection.

### The PhD proposal: *ConversionEU*, a different project

*Read in full: `~/Library/CloudStorage/OneDrive-UniversityofBergen/Documents/PhDCall/PhD_Proposal_ConversionEU_BergenU.pdf`
(13 pages). The file carries no date; its PDF was created 23 Jan 2026 (a copy; the proposal is older, written for the
PhD call — its newest references are 2023). [VERIFY: the date he submitted it]*

**"ConversionEU: A Non-Fiction VR Experience on Conversion Therapy in Europe."** Its design, in its own words:
- a non-fiction VR (VRNF) "to amplify the voices and experiences of survivors", made with volunteers recruited by a
  Europe-wide open call, "as guides/narrators of their experience", represented by avatars they control;
- an online platform for survivors' testimonies (this became SurvivingSOGICE.eu);
- method: practice-based research; perspective-taking in VR as "the foundational development principle"; "immersive
  witnessing (Nash, 2018)"; VRNF as "the ultimate empathy machine" (citing the 2015 TED talk);
  "interpretative art-based knowledge translation" — **Archibald, M. M. (2022), *Interweaving Arts-Based, Qualitative
  and Mixed Methods Research: Showcasing Integration and Knowledge Translation Through Material and Narrative
  Reflection*, 15(2), 168–198, https://doi.org/10.1177/19408447221097063** (the proposal does not name the journal; the
  DOI's prefix is *International Review of Qualitative Research*'s [VERIFY]).

**Why it matters for the article: the piece is the opposite of the proposal.** The proposal planned survivors'
testimonies, an empathy machine, and the visitor as witness of a real person's story. YOUR UPDATE HAS FAILED has no
testimony at all (the law: "no verbatim survivor testimony", "no borrowed testimony"), invented composites only, and
the system, not the survivor, as its subject; satire on the system's side. What it kept from the proposal:
perspective-taking (as the moving second person), immersive witnessing (as Witness Mode, then removed), the platform
(as the knowledge base and the Close's sources), and the knowledge-translation frame. The turn from testimony to the
system is a design decision with an ethical reason (*lugar de fala*, 7 June; "we should critique the system"), and it
is worth a paragraph: the piece answers the "empathy machine" critique by refusing to put the visitor in a survivor's
place.

1. Copilot wrote a full prompt with a title ("Pride Always Wins"), mechanics, five stages and an end line ("The system

   to make the player learn about the practices through being the perpetrator." Final prompt, five sentences: a Lemmings-
   like game; the player as "the institution, authority, or practitioner implementing interventions"; no lecture;
   "players learn about SOGICE by performing it"; a post-game dossier on the player's decisions.

**Why it matters for the article.**
- **A method stated plainly:** the prompt is designed so the *model's* interpretation is the object of study ("make the
  system showcase what it interprets"). That is the speculative-design strand of JUST CHANGE™ (what generative systems
  make of SOGICE) applied to game design; it is a protocol, and it could be written up as one.
- **His corrections push away from the model's tendency to resolve the argument for him** (a title, a moral, an end line)
  toward an open probe. That is the same direction as his overrides in the piece (Fable §3.3: more honesty, less hedging):
  here, less steering.
- **The same laws as the piece:** the player inside the apparatus as the perpetrator (2016's operator), "pride always wins"

- **What it does not show:** the games ArenaAI's models produced from it, if any were made. Without them, the chat is a

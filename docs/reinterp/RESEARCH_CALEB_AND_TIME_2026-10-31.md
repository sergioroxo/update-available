## 5 Virtual pets and streaks

### Why caring can become obligation

Sherry Turkle argues that caring for a computational creature can produce attachment. The object need not have human intelligence to evoke responsibility. This helps explain Lamby without pretending it is real AI. [Turkle, MIT-hosted essay](https://smg.media.mit.edu/library/Turkle.Diary.LondonReviewOfBooks.html).

Bloch and Lemish’s 1999 *Disposable Love* treats Tamagotchi as a cultural object built around nurturing relations. It is a useful period critique, rather than a claim that all children reacted alike. [New Media & Society](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/14614449922225591).

Persuasive computing was already an explicit research subject in 1997. Fogg’s CHI description names the overlap of computers and persuasion. This supports a period context, not a direct historical link to Restorify. [CHI 1997](https://chi1997.acm.org/proceedings/sig/bjf.html).

### Streaks and habit tracking

Duolingo’s own design account describes consecutive-day goals, prominent counts, reminders, and tests intended to increase continued use. It is a later product account, not a 2003 precedent. [Duolingo design account](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals/).

Silverman and Barasch’s seven studies found that an intact displayed streak could increase further engagement. The effect could depend on the log even when past behaviour was identical. Self-attributed breaks strengthened the effect; repair could reduce the damage of a break. These are consumer studies, not studies of conversion-practice survivors. [Journal of Consumer Research](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article/49/6/1095/6623414?guestAccessKey=1bb91501-7f0d-4dcc-806d-8124e533be13).

**Design inference:** the count can become a goal separate from wellbeing. The institution’s power lies in deciding what counts.

**Historical limit:** no source found documents a pet-linked purity streak in 2003 SOGICE software. Restorify’s version is an invented combination of period pet design and ongoing accountability. Its 412 days are character backstory, not days played by the visitor.

### Critique of care as compliance

Schüll’s 2016 research examines technologies that promise self-care while guiding choices and outsourcing self-regulation. This offers a later critical lens. It does not prove that self-tracking is always harmful. [BioSocieties paper](https://arts.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Data-for-Life-Wearable-Technology-and-the-Design-of-Self-Care.pdf).

**Interpretation for this piece:** Restorify changes the question from “How is Daniel?” to “Did Daniel complete the required action?” Lamby makes that substitution feel like caring for someone. The critique targets the programme’s definition of care. It does not ridicule attachment to toys, companionship, faith, or the people harmed.

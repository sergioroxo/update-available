## Prompt 1 — LambyOS Home, round 5: the brochure (the door)

```
LambyOS Home (round 4, attached) is the front door of an art piece about how conversion-practice programmes
targeted LGBTQ+ people from 1997 to 2026. Keep round 4's style, quality, palette, light, layout, every object and
every layer id, so the new art drops in as a swap. What changes is WHAT THE ROOM SELLS.

The room is the BROCHURE of a fictional residential "restoration" programme called LambyOS: a show home whose rules
are presented as cosy features. It must read as that from the very first look — still bright, saturated, 1995 and
too happy, but its pitch is plainly the programme's. Never horror, never dark, nothing that mocks gay, lesbian, bi or
trans people; the unease is all about the programme and how it dresses control as care. Invented names only — no real
organisation, church, logo or person. Drawn lettering is fine for the invented signs below.

THE CENTRE (the desk, where the eye goes first) — add:
1. On the desk beside the three devices: an open cardboard "LET GO!" box, cheerful, with the old things inside — a
   mixtape, a band T-shirt, a paperback, a photo turned face-down. Printed on its side: "LET GO OF EVERYTHING YOU
   HIDE". (separate layer)
2. Above the desk on the wall: a framed cross-stitch sampler in bright thread: "HOUSE RULES — Doors open, hearts
   open · Never alone · Approved friends only · Lights out 10pm" with little stitched hearts. (separate layer)
3. The computer on the desk has a quilted dust cover half lifted off it, with a tag "SEASON OF APARTNESS — 30 DAYS".

THE WALLS (seen in the first look) — add, painted into the backdrops:
4. A simple wooden cross above the front door.
5. A framed advert poster: a smiling lamb, a silhouette of a person with both arms raised against a sunrise, and the
   line "Over 30 years of care.*" — a big asterisk, the small print below it too small to read.
6. A retro camp poster: a sunny lake, a cabin, a row of happy silhouettes all facing the same way, "CAMP NEW DAWN —
   youth weekends · come with a parent!".
7. A painted family tree that is really a diagram: squares and circles joined by lines, with little hearts (a
   genogram dressed as decoration).
8. By the door, a guest book on a stand with a column of ticks headed "APPROVED". (separate layer)
9. The calendar (keep it a separate layer): every day crossed out with a red X, today circled.
10. The window: the sunny countryside is a painted backdrop — at one corner the canvas peels to show grey wall.
11. Colour: keep the bright palette, but the edges drift — a soft lilac-green vignette in the corners and a faint
    red/cyan fringe on outlines near the edges, like a misaligned CRT; the centre stays clean and readable.
12. Lamby: same mascot, smile a little too wide, eyes a little too shiny, looking toward the viewer. Both poses.

KEEP everything from round 4 (sign, door, clock layers, notice, plaque, switch, remote, devices, Lexicon book, VHS,
CD-ROM) and FIX round 4's notes: the notice's glow fitted to its frame; no blank boards; on the phone "Content note"
under its frame; the remote fully on the desk mat; shelf objects seated with a contact shadow.
Sizes as round 4: 3840×2160 laptop and 2160×3840 phone rooms, layers at the same scale, lossless PNG, untouched
originals in sources/, layout.json with the same ids plus rectangles for the new layers (letgo-box, rules-sampler,
guest-book). Deliver in output/lambyos-home/round5/ with laptop and phone previews and NOTES.md. No web page.
```

## Prompt 2 — The Library, round 2: the house it was

```
You made "LambyOS Home — Library" (round 1, attached): where the visit ends, on an old computer's screen. Make a
SECOND STATE of the same room, same layout and layer ids, so the piece can swap between them: the brochure's house
as it really was. Same objects in the same places; different light and different truth. Not horror, not dark-gothic:
plain, institutional, sad — a church hall at 6am.

- Light: flat, cool fluorescent; the saturated colours drained by about half; a slight green cast.
- The round window: the countryside is visibly a painted canvas on a frame, its edge and the grey wall behind it
  showing; one corner sagging.
- The sign "LambyOS Home — Library" stays, a little faded.
- On the wall: the house rules in plain typed print on a sheet pinned with tape (just lines, not readable).
- The "LET GO!" box from the front door (attached round-5 if available; otherwise a cardboard box) is now TAPED SHUT
  and labelled with a strip of masking tape (no readable text).
- A guest book on a stand with its lines struck through.
- A framed diagram of squares and circles joined by lines (a family diagram), plain, no hearts.
- The cross stays exactly as a plain wooden cross — unchanged (faith is not the target).
- THE MANUALS: the bookshelves now hold the programme's own documents — ring binders, spiral-bound workbooks,
  stapled booklets, in office colours (beige, navy, maroon, grey), some with tabs and sticky notes. Leave their spines
  BLANK (no letters): the piece writes the titles itself. Give me 8 of them as separate layers on one shelf at eye
  level, each with its own rectangle in layout.json (ids manual-1 … manual-8).
- Lamby (both poses): the same mascot, the fleece greyer and a little matted, the smile unchanged.
- Keep: the lectern with the Lexicon, the four era computers, the receipt printer, the bell, the door, the plaque.
Same size (3840×2160), lossless PNG layers, originals in sources/, layout.json with the same ids plus manual-1…8.
Deliver in output/lambyos-library/round2/ with a preview PNG and NOTES.md.
```

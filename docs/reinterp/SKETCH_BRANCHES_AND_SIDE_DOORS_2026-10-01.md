## 1 · The side doors: every optional thing is a funnel
**Today** the optional things are found five ways, unevenly: desktop icons (lamby_rig, ROOTCAUSE, Session, Family Form,
Your file, Harbor, the found file), objects in the room (the handheld, the flip phone, the console, the tapes, the
racket and its sheet, the corner behind you), links inside the browsers (pages, the ring, the directory), the phone's
home screen (FloppySheep), and the game menu (the map, the dossier). Each one ends however it ends; most just close.

**Proposed law — three parts for every side door:**
1. **Found.** It is offered once by the era's own voice (the 1997 system line, Lamby, Lambient, L) — after a main
   beat or in a quiet moment, as the "while you wait" lines already do — and the map lists it with ○.
2. **Inside.** Its own content, unchanged.
3. **The way back, in the system's voice.** Its last screen pushes to the main path's *current* beat, read live from
   the map (`mapState().current`), so it is always right: FIT IN's GAME OVER → "Back to your programme — Step 4: Connect";
   Harbor's application sent → "Your check-in is waiting"; FloppySheep's lapse → "Report to your group?" (the vote);
   TIDY's room that won't tidy → "Your session is ready". One helper, one line per surface, data-driven.

**The exception is the point.** The people's things never push back: Caleb's song, the radio-hour tape, Tape C, the
ball, the outtake on Tape 04. Everything the system offers funnels you back to it; only what people made for each other
does not. A player who notices that has understood the piece.

## 2 · Where a branch could live (branches that diverge in HOW, converge in WHERE)
The standing law is "register, not branch": choices are filed, the story does not fork, because the person's life is
not the player's to steer. A branch can respect that if it changes what the SYSTEM does and still arrives at the same
place — you can route around it, never out of it. Three candidates, best first:

- **A · 2003, Tape 04 — keep or trim the outtake (recommended).** At "Send to editing" the editor offers *Trim the
  outtake* (its default) or *Keep the full tape*. Trimmed: the outtake is "deleted" — and in 2016 Vera's archive still
  holds a copy (the documented point: copies outlive withdrawal). Kept: it travels inside Daniel's file. Either way, in
  2016 Vera meets two strangers recognising each other in a clip she is told to cut. Diverges in how; converges in what.
- **B · 1997, Rob's request — make Ignore live.** Accept: the private messages, as now. Ignore: Rob goes through his
  mother (the documented referral through parents) and the placement letter arrives anyway, one beat later. Same
  placement; a different hand on the door.
- **C · 2016, Vera's corrections — if she skips most.** Lambient schedules a "quality conversation"; the group's vote
  reaches her phone sooner. Same cascade; more pressure on the way.

## 3 · 2003's video — yes, two products of one media economy
They don't collide; they are the two ends of the same practice. **The testimony documentary** is evidence: a real
participant, his own words framed as proof, a discussion-starter for churches — the raw material. **The infomercial**
("Discover the New You", NetVision) is the product selling itself: actors, "DRAMATIZATION", a hotline, Restorify's
brand. The documented movement went from the one to the other — Truth in Love (1998) ran real people's stories as
paid advertising. Across the piece the line is: 1997's radio-hour tape (the ad you hear) → 2003's testimony and
infomercial (the ad made of you) → 2016's testimony music videos and video tagging (the ad you produce and route) →
2026's restored photographs and the session on the headset (the ad made around you).

**One proposal to tie them (his call — ethics and taste):** the NetVision infomercial carries a two-second insert,
"Real stories. Real change.", over a frame from Tape 04 — Daniel's own sentence. He is shown his own words being used
to sell the programme back to him. Grounding: testimony re-used as advertising is documented (Truth in Love).

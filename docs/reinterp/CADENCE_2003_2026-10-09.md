# 2003 cadence — timeline and waits (2026-10-09)

*W1-B3 and W1-D1…D19 (docs/reinterp/WALKTHROUGH_1_2026-10-08.md). Part A maps 2003 from its arrival to the update
ritual **as the code runs it** (read at `eea8c34`, branch `reinterp`): src/desktop/os.ts (the `e2Stage` machine and the
per-frame `update`), the 2003 apps in src/desktop/apps/, data/dialog/s2_*.json, src/narrative/spine.ts, src/frame/helper.ts
and src/witness/map.ts. Part B lists the smallest change per conflict and says which ones this branch implements. Nothing
here adds text or removes a beat.*

**How to read the timeline.** Each beat has a **trigger**: `PRESS` (the player did something), `TIMER` (a clock, in
seconds), `LEDGER` (a record the player's earlier act filed), or `CHAIN` (the previous beat ended). The column
**player is** says what the player can do at that moment. The column **also talking / arriving** lists everything else
that can speak, play, open or land in the same stretch. A **⚑** marks a place where the story moves on while the player
is busy or is talked over.

**One caveat up front.** His notes are short, spoken reactions. For each one I match it to the place in the code that fits
best and say how sure I am; where the note could mean two places I list both. Confirm the readings in §A4 before judging
Part B.

### A3 · The map's view of the same era (what the helper says)

`data/strings/map.json` `eras[1]` has 17 beats, in this order: the assistant → the daily check-in → Your story (the request) →
the release → the tapes → the edit → your story, online → *Your file (optional)* → *Harbor Forums (optional)* → *the
application (optional)* → *CLEAR (optional)* → *a summons (optional)* → **a message from outside** → **the thread
(`quiet`)** → **the intervention** ("Read the alert, then open the mail and continue.") → **what is left (`quiet`)** → the
update. The helper (`mountHelper`) shows the current beat's hint after `idleSeconds` of no pointer/key/wheel input and
**never during a `quiet` beat**. `enabled()` was `!isOff && !paused && !hasLeft && !scriptedBusy()`: it knew nothing about
playback. That is the whole of W1-D7: a player watching a 36 s clip is, to the helper, standing still.

## Part B — the re-timing plan

**The rule used.** A change is implemented only if it is *purely* a wait: a condition that defers a line, a toast, an
arrival or a helper until the player is idle or a video has ended. No text is added or removed, and no beat is dropped. A
change that would add a press, remove a timed beat or change the dramaturgy is listed as a **proposal** for him.

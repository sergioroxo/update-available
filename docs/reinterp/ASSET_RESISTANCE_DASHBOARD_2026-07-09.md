## Resistance Object Candidates

Recommendation: pick 3 recurring resistance motifs across Eras 1-4, then let the
system file around them without turning them into collectibles.

1. **Small rainbow plastic duck**
   - Source candidate: `Household Props 001-glb/Rubber Duck.glb`
   - Role: absurd, tender, portable; can survive room aging as a quiet refusal to be filed.
   - Use rule: visible, never clickable, never rewarded.

2. **Chipped mug / cup of tea**
   - Source candidate: `Cup Of Tea.glb`
   - Role: lived-in care and routine; can move from desk to shelf across eras.

3. **Potted plant**
   - Source candidate: `Potted Plant.glb`
   - Role: growth without explanation; can be small in E1 and fuller later.

4. **Headphones / small radio**
   - Source candidates: `Radio.glb`, `Headphones.glb`
   - Role: private sound-world without adding actual audio yet.

5. **Stickered notebook**
   - Source candidate: `Notebook.glb` is CC-BY; handmade primitive fallback is easy.
   - Role: private interiority. Must not duplicate DIARY.TXT content or invite new text entry.

6. **Photo backs / faceless polaroids**
   - Source candidate: `Polaroids.glb` is CC-BY.
   - Role: social life without real likenesses. Use backs, silhouettes, or cropped bodies only.

## Items To Avoid Or Rework

- Real bands, real logos, real pride marks, real organization names, or real
  survivor testimony.
- “Home movie”, “local band showcase”, “R.E.M.”, or generic nostalgia scraps
  unless they are doing script work. They read as moodboard filler.
- Readable brochures that mimic real SOGICE materials too closely. Use invented
  apparatus cues instead: `normal?`, `safe space?`, `questions become forms`,
  `photo day`, `before / after`, `the couple page`.

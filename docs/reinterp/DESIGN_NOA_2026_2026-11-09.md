# Noa in 2026: three options (T54, a proposal only; Sérgio picks)

*UiB job 8, item 4. Nothing here is built. His words (9 Nov): "Noa can just exist in 2026."*

## What is fixed before any option

- **Noa is no longer in 2016.** S337 took her off the board: 2016 keeps one testimony, Renata's. Her 2016 file
  (submission 2 in `data/dialog/s3_queue.json`: the video, the flannel, corrections 8 and 9) stays in the data and
  is no longer shown.
- **Guard 1:** nothing may read as one person's identity trajectory, and Noa is a different person from Maya. So
  **nothing from the 2016 file travels with her**: no flannel, no "I'm not good on camera", no video frames, no
  correction numbers. In 2026 she is a woman with the same first name and no history the visitor is shown.
- **The 2016 law still applies to her:** "no beat anywhere resolves her; if any future beat answers for Noa, it is
  wrong." The piece never says what her body, her faith or her family became. It never uses her to argue the
  clinical debate.
- **Guard 2:** her own thing is an ordinary object or act, never an identity signal. No one declares acceptance of
  her to make a scene work, and she does not exist to comfort Maya.
- 2026 is "pressure, not clinical": the pressure is the system's, so Noa is never its instrument.

## Option 1: a viewer in the birthday stream (recommended)

**Where:** the TransJesus birthday stream for trans youth (the fundraiser, `data/dialog/s4_v3.json` `fundraiser` and
the stream in `s4_ball.json`). Among the chat names, one line from Noa: an adult, older than Maya, giving to the
mutual aid. Something ordinary and of the moment, for example *"noa: happy birthday from the night shift x"*, and a
small donation tick beside the fund bar.

**What it adds:**
- A trans woman in her thirties, simply alive, at work, giving: the opposite of the "more than one in eight went
  back" headline Maya is fed elsewhere in 2026.
- It answers the system without arguing with it.
- It is his "just exist" taken literally.

**Risk:**
- Low. The stream is the people's space, never the programme's. The danger is a line that is too meaningful, one
  that "affirms" Maya and so makes Noa a device: keep it as small as any other viewer's.
- The name alone carries no 2016 history, so guard 1 holds.
- If he wants players to recognise her as "the Noa of 2016", this option does not do that, and that is deliberate.

**Cost:** small. One chat line and a donation tick in data, in the stream's existing chat; no new art, no new
code path. It is reached only by those who open the stream (looking stays free; the beat does not depend on it).

## Option 2: the "someone who went back" recording (not recommended as written)

**Where:** the session's item 2 (`s4_ball.json` `recorded`): a recording "verified by Second Thoughts", the speaker's
name removed, cut off by Junie's card mid-sentence.

**What it adds:**
- The sharpest version: the apparatus's own genre, the detransition testimonial, shown as a thing the system makes
  and vouches for.

**Risk:**
- High, and in two ways.
  - Making the speaker Noa is the piece **answering for her**: it tells the visitor what she became, which the 2016
    law forbids.
  - It puts the clinical debate inside satire territory, which CLAUDE.md forbids.
- Even an ambiguous version ("the voice might be hers") invites the visitor to resolve her.

**A safer variant**, if he wants Noa near this beat: the recording stays nameless, as now. After Junie's card, the
session's list shows its next item, "Stories like yours", with Noa's name on it, greyed. It is never opened, because
the session ends there. The system had her queued; the visitor never learns what it would have said. The risk drops
to medium: it still uses her as the system's material, though it says nothing about her.

**Cost:** small for the variant (one row in the plan's data); the full version is ruled out.

## Option 3: the bot's "stories" page

**Where:** Second Thoughts (the bot in the 2026 browser) gets a panel, "Stories like yours". It shows three cards the
system curated: first names, ages, one smoothed sentence each. One is Noa's, in the system's words: *"Noa, 34: I
found my peace."* A small "as she wrote it" link opens her own sentence, which is ordinary and says nothing of the
kind, for example *"I wrote about my week. I was tired, and the bus was late."*

**What it adds:**
- The 2016 mechanic (the system rewriting people's testimonies) continues as the APPARATUS's history, not hers: in
  2026 the editing is automatic and sold as care.
- The gap between the card and "as she wrote it" is the piece's argument about curation, and the satire stays in the
  perpetrator's self-presentation, where it collapses.

**Risk:**
- Medium. The card's claim must never be confirmed or denied by the piece, only contradicted by the plainness of
  her own sentence. Her own sentence must not be about gender at all, or the piece is answering again.
- It sits on Maya's own screen during the bot's pressure: keep Maya and Noa visibly unconnected (guard 1).
- It adds reading to a page that was cut for legibility.

**Cost:** medium. A panel state in `browser.ts`'s `drawBotPanel`, three cards and a link in data, one press, and a
check that the bot's foot bar keeps its buttons.

## Recommendation

**Option 1.** It does the least and keeps every law with no exception: she exists, she is ordinary, the system
never gets to use her, and nothing reads as a trajectory.

Option 3 is the one to choose if he wants Noa to carry the piece's argument about curated testimony into 2026. If so,
her "as she wrote it" sentence should be his (`_s`), since it is the line that has to be nobody's argument.

Option 2 only in its "greyed, never opened" variant, if at all.

**To decide:** 1, 2 (variant) or 3, and for option 1 or 3, whether he wants to write her one line himself.

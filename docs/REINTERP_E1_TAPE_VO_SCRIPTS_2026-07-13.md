## TAPE A — "YOUR FIRST STEPS" (the companion voice; one warm male voice, unhurried,
## kind, sincere — NOT the infomercial announcer; this voice believes every word)

**A1 — the welcome (~25s)**
"Hello, friend. If you're hearing this, you've taken the hardest step already — you asked
for help. We want you to know something before anything else: you are not alone, and you
are not the only one. Everything in this kit was put together by people who have walked
exactly where you're standing."

**A2 — how to use the kit (~20s)**
"Keep the booklet somewhere private. Work through one page at a time — don't rush.
When the workbook asks you a question, answer honestly. Honesty is the whole program.
Nobody will see your answers but you… and the people who are helping you."
*(the knife is the last clause — placed gently, almost administrative)*

**A3 — the daily walk (~20s)**
"Each morning, before the house wakes up, find five quiet minutes. Read the day's page.
When the wrong thoughts come — and they will come, friend, that's not failure, that's
the walk — just set them down. You don't have to carry what you're carrying. Set it
down, and tell someone. Always tell someone."

**A4 — the fellowship (~15s)**
"When you're ready, the channel listed on page four is open every evening. First names
only. You'll find the others there. They're waiting for you — they've BEEN waiting,
friend."
*(warm on first listen; on second listen "they've been waiting" is surveillance)*

**A5 — into the prayer (~10s, quieter)**
"Before you go — the group recorded something for you. For the nights it gets loud in
there. Sing along if you can. It's alright if you can't, yet."
→ *"Fold My Hands" plays (delivered asset). "yet" is the whole program in one word.*

## Why Tape B matters more than it looks (dramaturgy note)
Tape B closes E1's oldest open question — HOW the kit arrived — diegetically and
optionally: a player who plays the radio tape realizes the ad targeted his family, and
the "friend" voice on Tape A stops sounding like it was recorded for him and starts
sounding like a product insert. The two tapes together are the era's thesis in audio:
the warm voice and the sales voice are the same organization on different channels.

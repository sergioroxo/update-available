### When the visor goes on, what does the player actually see? *(the big one)*
The device doc settles that the visor is the screen. It does **not** settle what happens to the frame.

- **(a)** You stay seated in Room 3 and watch the visor plane from outside it.
- **(b)** The canvas fills the frame, exactly as the monitor already does at the desk seat.

**My read: (b).** The monitor already fills the frame when you sit at it — **the visor should behave
identically, because it is the same canvas on a new mount.** Choosing (a) would make "one surface"
mean two different things depending on which surface, and it would also break the beat: *the point is
that it is on your face.*

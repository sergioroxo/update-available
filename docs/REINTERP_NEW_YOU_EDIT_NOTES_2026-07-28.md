## THE EDIT LIST

### 1 · The opening (0 → 5.92) — a title card, currently empty
> *"Lamby Productions presents the new way to recover/succumb to your true self", with not the same
> Lamby image, but a reinterpretation more TV evangelist about it.*

**Draft:** `LAMBY PRODUCTIONS PRESENTS` over an **evangelist Lamby** — not the Clippy lamb: haloed,
backlit, in a gold-edged oval like a stained-glass window or a ministry logo. Then the title card:
**THE NEW WAY TO RECOVER YOUR TRUE SELF**, played completely straight, plus a mundane period
copyright line (`© 2003 Lamby Productions · a ministry of Restorify™`).
**Note on his "recover/succumb":** the joke is that they are interchangeable — but I'd **play it
straight here and never wink**. The word that curdles is the one the audience supplies later. Save
every glitch for the break; the opening's job is to be sincerely, hideously wholesome.

### 2 · The bodies — "too barebones", and they hold the screen a long time
> *"they need to be more like stickman animation (to not show faces) or be more descriptive using
> like messages on the screen to add layers of interpretation."*

**Both, doing different jobs:**
- **Figures that ACT**, not busts that sit — stick/silhouette figures with real gesture: the
  Struggler slumps, Dale opens his arms, the Counsellor leans in. Still faceless (the law holds).
- **DRTV chyron layers** — and these are period-real, not invented furniture:
  `DRAMATIZATION` · `ACTUAL PARTICIPANT` · `RESULTS NOT TYPICAL` · `PAID PROGRAMMING`.
  **`DRAMATIZATION` sitting under a testimony is the single most damning caption available**, and it
  is exactly what real DRTV puts there. This is the "layers of interpretation" he asked for, and it
  costs nothing but type.

### 3 · Before / After — "awesome", but the two look too alike
> *"needs more either with visual additions or your creativity here to make more visual why before
> it was not great and now it is."*

**Draft:** BEFORE — desaturated, harsh top light, cramped frame, the figure slouched, **a window
behind him**. AFTER — warm key, upright, wider frame, a family-shaped shadow, chyron
`MARCUS · 14 MONTHS IN THE PROGRAM`.
**⚑ The quiet inversion I'd like to try: the BEFORE has a window and the AFTER does not.** Recovery
as enclosure. Never remarked on.

### 4 · "Then Pastor Dale showed me the program" (33.43) — product placement
> *"almost needs a fake poster of the program like a product placement."*

**Draft:** the boxed product, held up to camera as DRTV always does — **THE NEW YOU PROGRAM**,
12 cassettes + a workbook, shrink-wrapped, a starburst reading `AS SEEN ON TV`. The apparatus as a
thing you can own.

### 5 · The three steps (43.21–45.11) — ✅ *"so good and so in sync with the song, marvelous"* — keep

### 6 · "Won't you come home / to the self he meant you to be" (49.19–52.05) — needs flourishing
> *"they need more flourishing because it doesn't give much of a Come to us creepy vibe. Because
> 'meant you to be' is such a strong thing here!"*

**Draft, in the piece's restrained idiom:**
- the three figures **turn to face you**, then open their arms in unison;
- a **doorway of light** behind them, widening across the two lines;
- **they step toward camera a few pixels per beat** — approach, not motion. Nothing lunges; it is
  just closer each time you look;
- *"he meant you to be"* arrives **letter by letter** rather than as a line.

### 7 · The chorus + red ball (58.51–78.91) — ✅ *"fantastic, just amazing"*, busts work — keep

### 8 · "Operators of grace are standing by" (91.70)
> *"maybe add an outline of phone with a blinking light?"*
**Draft:** a period phone in outline, its hold-light blinking **on the beat**, and a small
`OPERATORS AVAILABLE: 3` that ticks down and never reaches zero.

### 9 · ⚑ 97.6 — the bare `crowd` shot is a jarring cut
> *"why do we go back to the three figures… then it is too bare and quite the visual jump from the
> blue before."*
**He is right, and the data confirms it:** there is a `crowd` scene at 97.6 wedged between two
`offer` screens. **Retire it.** Stay on the blue offer field and let the **phone number pulse and
grow** into the countdown — his own suggestion, and it keeps the run continuous into 100.3.

### 10 · ⚑ 105.32, the last "Call now" — the dark comes too early
> *"the dark screen with noise is a bit too early… it also feels like a missed opportunity for some
> ridiculousness."*
**Draft — the apparatus over-selling itself into collapse:** on the last *Call now* the offer screen
goes into **overdrive** rather than cutting out — the rosette spins, badges multiply and stack,
the phone number stutters and duplicates across the frame, `ORDER NOW!` grows until it no longer
fits. **Then** the tear. The break stops being a cut and becomes something the pitch does to itself.

### 11 · The disclaimer (108.6) — bigger
> *"needs to be bigger so we can see it with the scanlines and noise, but it is great to read it
> while the call now is ending!!"*
Larger type, legible **through** the noise rather than under it. The timing he likes stays.

### 12 · "(Don't) Skip" — his joke, and I'd take it
> *"Almost makes me feel like I would add '(Don't) Skip' on the skip button to help people see it."*
**Register-correct and very funny:** the apparatus begging you not to leave, on the one control that
lets you. Satire lives in the perpetrator's self-presentation; the button still works exactly as
before, which is what keeps it honest. **His call — but I'd ship it.**

## ROLE
You are a meticulous researcher with expertise in the documented history and methods of conversion practices
("SOGICE"), plus a working knowledge of critical-play/persuasive-games game-studies scholarship. I need
**verified source material** to ground a set of small interactive vignettes ("provotypes" — defined below),

## THE PIECE (context)
**"YOUR UPDATE HAS FAILED."** — a browser + WebXR piece across four eras of one computer, dramatizing how
conversion-practice networks targeting queer people evolved: 1997 recruit (a teen), 2003 self-surveillance
(an ex-gay man, "father-wound" frame), 2016 platform moderation (a lesbian "Story Steward"), present-day
algorithmic/AI targeting (a trans woman). A parallel, newer thread (not yet built) considers adding a

documentary panel, never appear as a character or likeness); invented composites only for anyone playable;
all factual/clinical/legal claims must be sourced or tagged `[VERIFY SOURCE]`.

## PROVOTYPES (what these sources need to support)
A **provotype** (provocation + prototype) is a short, playable, deliberately **unwinnable** vignette that
dramatizes a real documented conversion-practice technique. The unwinnability itself is the argument —
modeled on Bogost's Rhetoric of Failure and Lee's "I Lose, Therefore I Think": performing the technique
exactly as historically instructed changes nothing, demonstrating that the underlying premise (you can
change someone's sexual orientation or gender identity through this method) is false. Each provotype needs a
**real, specific, sourceable technique** to dramatize — not a generic "conversion therapy is bad" gesture.

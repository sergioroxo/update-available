## ⚑ THE ESCALATION — what actually differentiates the eras

| Era | The player's position | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| **E1 1997** | you are the **object**. A disk is inserted, a form is answered, a tape plays at you. | *receive* |
| **E2 2003** | you are the **participant**. Chips, check-ins, one commit-press. You answer back. | *respond* |
| **E3 2016** | **you are the OPERATOR — for the first time you act on ANOTHER PERSON.** | *edit* |
| **E4 now** | you are **obsolete**. It does the operation without you. | *watch* |

**E3's escalation is power over someone else.** That is what the era has been missing, and it is why
"platform" felt like a theme rather than a level. It is also why the refusal at the end can matter:
you have to have been given power for giving it up to cost anything.

## ⚑ THE MECHANIC — the timeline is a form with three slots, and her story has four kinds of block

**Vera's job: prepare Mira Lane's submitted testimony for publication.**

Mira's testimony arrives as **transcript blocks** — sections of speech, draggable. *(Sérgio's own
suggestion: "sections of word and drag them to the space they need to be.")* The publishing template
has **three slots**, because the documented grammar has three phases:

`Where I was` · `What happened` · `Where I am now`

**Mira's blocks do not divide into three.** Some fit. Some half-fit. And several belong to no slot at
all — the parts where she doubts, qualifies, names someone, or says something that resolves nothing.

Below the template is a tray. It is labelled, blandly: **`not used`**.

### The coercion is FORMAL, not arithmetic
*(My earlier version had the view count refusing to move. That was contrived — Sérgio was right to
doubt it.)*

**The template will not publish with an empty slot.** And **Mira has no "Where I am now."** She never
arrived anywhere. So the player has exactly three options, and all three are the era:

| Choice | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Fill the slot with a block that doesn't belong** | falsify her — the form supplies the ending she didn't give |
| **Leave it empty and file it as unpublishable** | **bury her** — which is precisely what Vera is ordered to do |
| **Refuse both** | the glitch (below) |

**Nobody lies to the player and nobody threatens her.** The form simply requires an answer that the
person did not give, and offers a tidy place to put everything that doesn't fit. *That* is the
suppression of voices, and it is one drag gesture.

**And the tray is the point.** What sits in `not used` at the end is Mira's actual story.

## ⚑ THE REFUSAL, now a player action
Canon already gives the line — **"I won't bury her story."** The mechanic gives it a body:

**Vera publishes the tray.** The unused blocks, in the order they were spoken, with no template
around them. The one thing the interface has no button for.

That is the era's person's-glitch, and it is something the player *does* rather than watches.

## ⚑ THE DEVICES — connected by CONTRADICTION, not by sequence

The two devices are not stages of a pipeline. **They are two channels carrying the same person, and
they disagree.**

| | **Laptop — the institution** | **Phone — the person** |
|---|---|---|
| What it carries | the queue, the template, the guidelines, Mira's submission as *material* | Noa's DM: *don't let them do this to her* |
| How it speaks | procedure, softly | plainly, and it is `felt` |
| What it wants | the slots filled | the story kept |

**They contradict each other about the same file, at the same time.** That is the connection —
period-exact (your work tool and your personal phone, and the person is in both), and it makes the
devices structural rather than decorative. You cannot do the job on the laptop without the phone
telling you what the job is.

**And Noa's canon corrections live here too**, unchanged: the queue wants her flannel softened and her
voice brightened; the new "gender clarity review" module flags the same testimony as *possible gender
confusion — route to exploratory mentorship.* **Two contradictory corrections, one person, both
rendered, neither endorsed.** The system disagreeing with itself is not a bug in this design — it is
the second contradiction, and it rhymes with the first.

## ⚑ LAMBIENT — answered
**Lambient exists, and it is not a character. It is the software's own voice inside GraceQueue.**

- **Lamby (E2)** was a face that spoke to you.
- **Lambient (E3)** is the same system with the face removed: it is the *"Recommended"* tag, the
  template's helpful hint, the badge that says `verified`. It never has a body and never has a line
  of dialogue.
- **The name is the tell:** *Lamby → Lambient* is the dispersal S57 built, arriving as software.

So: **the Grace software IS Lambient.** They were never two things. And the mascot games on the phone
(FloppySheep, The Fold, Lamby GO) are the *brand's* leftovers — the same publisher, still shipping
cheerful product while its serious arm has become a workflow. **That is how the two connect**, and it
answers "or do we just have Lamby as other media products?" — both, and that is the joke.

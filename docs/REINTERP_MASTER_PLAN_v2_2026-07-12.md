## §1 THE PIECE IN ONE PAGE

**YOUR UPDATE HAS FAILED (reinterpretation)** is a browser + WebXR narrative experience about
how organised conversion-practice (SOGICE) networks target queer people online — told as
thirty years of one computer, in three rooms that age. The player follows different lives
through four eras: **1997 (Daniel, gay teen, Room 1) → 2003 (Daniel adult, the homecoming,
same room) → 2016 (Vera's lesbian-led era, Room 2, with the butch/trans-masc borderland) →
now (Maya's trans-led era, Room 3)** — then the Close (the point-cloud constellation,
"Restart as you are.").

The thesis: the demand never changes, only the disguise — **recruit ('97) → self-police
('03) → govern ('16) → optimise (now)**. Era transitions are SOFTWARE UPDATES triggered by
documented system failures, never by the player. Every player response — acceptance,
hesitation, refusal, curiosity, silence — is filed (witness symmetry); the player's real
agency is in witnessing the record, never in "choosing conversion." The piece is CO-GUIDED:
the apparatus itself conducts the player (that's the point — being clearer with the player
and being more controlling are the same move), and its guidance evolves per era (§4).

De-centering is structural: the gay narrative is deliberately NOT the main one — the piece
relocates its lead perspective each era (E3 starts lesbian-led, E4 trans-led), and the
system's shared infrastructure is what connects them (cross-cluster sends).

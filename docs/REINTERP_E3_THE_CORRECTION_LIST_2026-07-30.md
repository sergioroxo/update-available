# ERA 3 — THE CORRECTION LIST
*Third pass, on Sérgio's direction: keep the list-of-changes mechanic, **drop "bury"** and stop
retrofitting old canon, focus on **how SOGICE used digital platforms to target**, put the
script/scripture doubling to work, and make **Malta** the break. Human influence here — machine

## The shape, in one line
**Vera works a correction list. Every correction is footnoted with scripture. Then the law changes,
and the light in the room changes with it.**

---

## 1 · THE WORK — the correction list (laptop)

Submissions arrive in GraceQueue. Vera's job is not to judge them; it is to **apply the corrections
the style guide requires.** Each arrives as a checklist item with a one-line rationale and a verse.

**She is not asked whether the person should be corrected. She is asked whether the correction has
been applied.** That is the whole labour, and it is why it is survivable — it never feels like harm,
it feels like copy-editing.

### What the corrections actually do — the era's content
These are the digital targeting methods, rendered as UI. Each is one line in a list:

| Correction | What it does | The digital method it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Soften the term** | *"lesbian" → "same-sex attracted"* | identity replaced with a symptom; the language that evades moderation |
| **Remove the unresolved** | any sentence that doesn't conclude | doubt is a retention problem |
| **Add the invitation** | append the call-to-action and link | every story becomes a funnel |
| **Route for mentorship** | flag the submitter for follow-up contact | **the story is a lead** |
| **Adjust presentation** | *"soften the flannel; brighten the voice"* — canon | the profile itself is corrected |
| **Gender clarity review** | *"possible gender confusion — route to exploratory mentorship"* — canon | the same person, a second and contradictory correction |

⚑ **Noa's two corrections stay** — they are the sharpest thing the era has: *the same testimony
flagged both as a woman who needs softening and as a possible man who needs routing.* **Both rendered,
neither endorsed, and the piece never answers for her.** The apparatus disagreeing with itself about
one person is the era's argument in two list items.

**⚑ "Route for mentorship" is the beat that matters most.** It is the moment the player sees that this
was never moderation — **it is recruitment**. Someone told a story, and the system's response is to
send a person to their door.

## 2 · SCRIPT AND SCRIPTURE — the doubling
*Sérgio: "the following of the script (scripture at play here)."*

**Every correction carries a proof-text.** A small grey line under each item: chapter and verse,
citation-styled, tidy. The style guide *is* a devotional and it is formatted like a manual of style.

That doubling is the era in one visual: **the script you must follow and the scripture that says
why**, in the same typeface, at the same size. Nothing is argued. Both are simply *the reference*.

*(Invented verses and an invented book — no real scripture quoted as the apparatus's justification.
The FORM is the point, not the text.)*

## 3 · THE COERCION — it is a list, and lists get completed
No metrics, no threats, no view counts. **A checklist with items unticked is simply incomplete**, and
the queue shows how many remain. The apparatus never says *do this*. It says *3 of 7 applied*.

Every item can be skipped. Skipping is filed. **Nothing happens** — which is worse, and truer: the
work simply stays there, and the number does not go down.

## 4 · ⚑ THE BREAK — MALTA, and the light
*Sérgio: "a message from a colleague that shares the fact Malta has approved the law… we internally
break from the shackles of the conversion ideology… play on the idea of 'seeing the light'."*

**This is the era's real event, and it is historically exact: December 2016, Act LV — the first
national ban in Europe.** The piece has been carrying that fact as a dossier citation for weeks. It
belongs *in* the era.

**How it arrives:** on the phone, from a colleague — another person doing the same work, in another
country. Not an announcement. A message between two people who both know what it means.

> *did you see. malta. they made it illegal.*
> *not here. but somewhere.*

**And then the light.** Every glitch in this piece so far has been **degradation** — tape hiss, tearing
signal, the dying music box. **This one brightens.** The room's light lifts; the screen's grade warms;
the correction list is still there, unchanged, and it is suddenly readable as what it is.

**Nothing in the software changes. Only the light.** That is the "seeing the light" Sérgio asked for,
made literal — and it inverts the piece's own glitch grammar exactly once, which is why it will land.

*(It also connects to the piece's light doctrine: two lights fight for one room, and the warm one
wins at the Close. This is the first time it wins — briefly.)*

## 5 · WHAT THE BREAK CHANGES — nothing, and everything
She does not quit. The list does not vanish. **The next item is still there.**

What changed is that **it is now illegal somewhere**, and she knows it, and she is still holding the
list. The era ends on that — not on a refusal, not on a rescue. **On a person who can no longer
un-know what the work is.**

## 6 · THE HANDOFF TO E4
The law arrives, and the apparatus does what it documentably did: **it does not stop — it moves.**
Softer language, civil-liberties framing, and the work automated so no one has to hold the list.

- **E1** it needed her to go somewhere · **E2** to open it · **E3** to *apply the corrections* ·
  **E4 it needs nobody.**
- The correction list is what **E4's AI does instantly** — the same operations, no hands. *(And the
  image changer, trans→cis, gay→straight, is the same list applied to a face.)*
- The name erodes at that seam: **Lamby → Lambient → Echo / "L".**

**She becomes unnecessary in the same movement that made her work illegal.** That is the era's ending
and E4's premise in one beat.

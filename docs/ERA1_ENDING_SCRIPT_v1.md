## The shape of the ending (one breath)

The warmth you found turns out to have a destination. Rob stops asking and
starts arranging. A form fills the screen — your name already on it, a
parent's signature already there. You scroll a thing that was decided without
you. Then the screen goes dark, the room stays, and when the machine wakes it
has quietly filed you as *gone*. No van. No camp shown. Just paperwork and a
power-cut — the era's true violence.

## S1.9 — Suspension  [felt — minimal, no narration]

On OK (or after a held beat), the screen **powers down**. Not a crash, not a
melodrama — the same dark glass as S1.0, as if someone reached over and
switched it off. Input dead. The **room stays**: lamp still warm, boombox
quiet, door shut. Hold ~6 seconds of silence. (⚑ Sérgio's rule: no music
sting, no dossier interruption here. The quiet is the line.)

Then — without the player acting — the machine wakes into the update.

## The first UPDATE ritual → Era 2  [operable; the metaphor]

The grammar that every era transition will use, born here:
**notification → EULA → changelog → restart.** Triggered by the system's
"success," never the player (v0.7 update grammar).

**1. Notification** (on the dark screen, a single line types itself):
> `An update is installing. Do not turn off your computer.`

**2. EULA — consent as visual hierarchy** (⚑ your design, round 8: bold the
few lines they're herded to read; blur/grey the rest into illegibility; the
absurdity is being asked to consent to what can't be read):
- Bold, legible: *"By continuing you accept the terms of care."*
- Bold, legible: *"You agree the program acts in your best interest."*
- Everything around it: greyed, blurred paragraph-shapes — unreadable on
  purpose.
- One button: **[ I AGREE ]**. No decline. (The point: the burden was never
  yours to carry, yet here it is.)

**3. Changelog-as-thesis** (⚑ this is close to the project's moral voice —
draft only, you own the final):
> `PHASE/2 95  →  PHASE/2 2000`
> `· profile migrated while you were away`
> `· enrollment added to your record`
> `· "cure" is still available`
> `· new: ongoing accountability`
> `· welcome back, {name}.`

(Note: the *"cure is deprecated / support installed"* rebrand belongs to the
**Era 2→3** update — the ex-gay collapse, v0.7 §9. This first changelog keeps
the cure-era language intact; the person returns from the program into a
world that has only hardened.)

**4. Restart** → Era 2 boots. The desktop returns subtly changed (a new era
clock, the room one notch tidier/emptier per the "optimized" rule). We stop
here — Era 2 content is the next milestone after this.

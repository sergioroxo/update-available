# THE INFRASTRUCTURE SPINE — why the rooms open, documented
*Adopted by Sérgio 2026-07-25: "I like the general-purpose infrastructure. I think we need that
documented spine. And it does help with the D31 of changing the way the rooms open up."
Grounded in GPAHE (see `REINTERP_D31_DISPERSAL_EVIDENCE_2026-07-25.md`). This upgrades the piece's
own explanation of its spatial change from an assertion into a sourced argument.*

## The upgrade, in one line
**Before:** the rooms open at E2→E3 because the collapse scattered the apparatus into many groups.
**Now:** the rooms open because the apparatus **stopped being a place you go to**. It moved into the
infrastructure you were already using — so there is no longer a separate room for it to be in.

That is a better reason, and it is documented. GPAHE finds the apparatus riding **general-purpose
services**: Google and Bing search (~95% of search traffic), YouTube (monetised provider video),
Facebook/Instagram, PayPal and Amazon Smile (donations), Alexa voice results. Not a network of
ideological sites — *ordinary services carrying it*.

**Sérgio's insight, recorded because it's the load-bearing part:** this doesn't just justify the
dispersal, it changes **what the room-opening means**. The walls don't come down because more rooms
became available. They come down because **walls stopped meaning anything** — the thing you could
once close a door on is now in the room's ordinary furniture.

## The era spine it gives us
This is the documented backbone for the whole progression — each era is the apparatus occupying a
different *kind* of thing:

| Era | The apparatus is… | You reach it by… | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1 1997** | a **PLACE** — a residential program, a mailed kit, a booklet, a tape | going there; it arrives by post | TriedPath / New Morning; period materials |
| **E2 2003** | a **PROGRAM** on your machine — Restorify, a thing you open | launching it | the ex-gay web era; accountability software idiom |
| **E3 2016** | a **PLATFORM** you live inside — GraceQueue / SisterSignal | already being there | GPAHE: platform-native migration, "rebranding under new names" |
| **E4 now** | **AMBIENT** — not a service, but *in* the services | not reaching for it at all | GPAHE: search, video, payments, voice assistants |

**The rooms open at E2→E3 because that is exactly where the apparatus stops being something you open
and becomes something you are inside.** The spatial law and the documented history now say the same
thing, which is the strongest position the piece can be in.

## ⚑ The ethics constraint this creates (binding)
GPAHE names **real companies**. Under CLAUDE.md real organisations may be named **only where the KB
documents them, and only in dossier/provenance surfaces — never in the fiction.**

So: **Google, Bing, YouTube, PayPal, Amazon, Alexa may appear on dossier cards and in the Close's
provenance. They may never appear in a room.** E4's fiction keeps invented marks.

**The design consequence, and it's a good one:** E4's surfaces should not be *conversion services*.
They should be **ordinary services that happen to carry it** — a wellness app, a search field, a
voice assistant, a payments screen — all invented, all mundane. The horror in E4 is not that the
apparatus got louder; it's that it got **indistinguishable from utilities**. That is a sharper brief
for E4 than "ambient care" alone, and it comes with citations.

## ⚑ Framing discipline (from the 2023 report — do not skip)
GPAHE 2023 finds deplatforming **partly worked**: Facebook and Instagram eliminated the
recommendation rabbit holes, Bing improved markedly, TikTok performs best and shows warnings.
YouTube remains "a disaster"; non-English content is least moderated.

**So the infrastructure claim must be stated as contested ground, not as inevitability.** The
apparatus is *in* the infrastructure and *is being pushed out of parts of it*. Overstating this into
"it is everywhere and nothing can be done" would be both inaccurate and against the piece's own laws
— the respite is never a trap, and the Close is solidarity rather than defeat. **Where the claim is
strong, say it; where it is contested, use `contested`.**

## ✅ DECIDED — E4 names the SERVICES, not the brands (Sérgio, 2026-07-25)
> "I think we can build E4 to name not the brands but the services, truly expanding and exploring
> how digital SOGICE is, and that's the whole point of this experience."

**So E4's brief is now explicit:** name the KINDS of service the apparatus rides — search, video,
payments, a voice in the room, a wellness app, a recommendation feed — with **invented marks**, and
explore them properly rather than gesturing at "ambient care". This is not set dressing; per Sérgio
it is *the whole point of the experience*, so E4 should be the fullest exploration of how digital
SOGICE actually works, not its faintest.

**The line this keeps clean:** the SERVICE TYPE is real and documented (GPAHE: search engines,
video monetisation, payment processors, voice assistants); the BRAND is invented. So the piece can
say *"the search field"*, *"the donate button"*, *"the assistant that answers when you speak"* —
truthfully, with citations — and never name Google, YouTube, PayPal or Alexa outside a dossier card.
Real names stay in provenance; the mechanism goes in the room.

*Design consequence, restated because it is the good one:* E4's surfaces are **ordinary services
that happen to carry it**. The horror is not volume — it is that the apparatus became
indistinguishable from utilities.

## Absolute

   transmitted.** Wiped on exit/idle/refusal endings. (CI-enforced.)
2. **No real people**: no likenesses, no verbatim survivor testimony, no
   real individuals as characters. Abstract roles, institutions, mechanics.
   Organizations may be named only where documented in the KB.
3. The photo-filter mock takes **no** camera/file input, ever.
4. Content warning up front, minimum 4s before continue arms; a working
   Leave; pause/exit reachable from every scene including the Offer.
5. No analytics or any re-identification surface. ⚑ if ever proposed.

## Register & tone
6. Victim side grounded; satire only on perpetrator self-presentation, and
   it collapses. The interface may charm — its charm is the propaganda.

   over the same session, unresolved. Ambiguity is the scene.
8. `respite` scenes are never traps and never revealed as fake; the system
   targets around them, never through them. At least one uncorrupted space
   per run.
9. Distress is engaged purposefully, never as spectacle ("darkness earned").
   Respite + dossier are the release valves (Batson; empathic rejection).
10. The witness/intake record computes only from things the player actually
    did — every line traceable to their own act (anti-victim-blaming:
    the system pathologizes compliance AND resistance symmetrically).
11. Updates are triggered by documented system failures — never by the
    player.

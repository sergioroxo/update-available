## How to read this
- **The run's grades** use the public words: *documented* (a source supports it), *disputed* (some support,
  but our wording overstates or conflicts with the record), *imagined* (interpretation or invention the record
  cannot establish). In the data these are `documentary` / `contested` / `speculative`.
- **"Disputed" here mostly means *our sentence claims more than the source shows*** — not that the history is
  wrong. Almost every fix is to *narrow* a sentence ("ex-gay ministries" → "programmes such as Living
  Waters"), exactly like July's.
- Each item: **now** (the sentence as it stands, shortened) → **the run** → **proposed** (a replacement for you
  to approve, rewrite or refuse) → **status** → **not checked by the run** (parts of the sentence the run did not
  address; they keep their sticker until something checks them).
- Tick one box per item. When you have ticked, say *"apply what I approved"* and a build session edits exactly
  those sentences, clears exactly those stickers, adds the links, regenerates the sources page, and walks.

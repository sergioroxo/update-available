  mixed attracted ★

  lifelong monogamy ★

    → Vocab tag: Term: lifelong monogamy
    Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zya-Qf_zKvk

  sexual orientation change therapy ★
    Umbrella term used by SOGICE advocates to describe therapeutic interventions aimed at changing a person's sexual orientation from homosexual to heterosexual. Framed as evidence-based treatment option rather than harmful practice rejected by mainstream mental health organisations.
    Context: "empirically-validated by many decades of research"
    → Vocab tag: Term: sexual orientation change therapy
    Source: https://stoptheban.ca/watch-censored-documentary-on-conversion-therapy/

  conversion practices ★
    A term used by LGBTQI rights advocates to describe a range of interventions intended to change, repress, or suppress a person's sexual orientation or gender identity. Athena Forum and similar organisations reject this framing, arguing that the term conflates violent crimes, family conflict, religious disagreement, and legitimate healthcare, thereby artificially inflating prevalence figures and distorting the issue.
    Context: "the newer, broader term for conversion therapy"
    → Vocab tag: Term: conversion practices
    Source: https://athena-forum.eu/updates/pushing-conversion-therapy-bans-across-europe-transactivist-demands-enabled-by-political-indifference/

  gender identity change efforts ★
    Gender identity change efforts refers to any therapeutic or non-therapeutic practice aimed at changing a person's gender identity or expression to align with sex assigned at birth or stereotypical gender role behaviors. The term encompasses both formal conversion therapy techniques and broader social pressures toward cisgender conformity, including interventions by mental health professionals, religious counselors, family members, or institutional gatekeeping from gender-affirming care.
    Context: "techniques used by mental health professionals and non-professionals with the goal of changing gender identity"
    → Vocab tag: Term: gender identity change efforts
    Source: https://www.apa.org/about/policy/resolution-gender-identity-change-efforts.pdf

  sexual orientation change efforts ★
    Sexual orientation change efforts is the term used by the American Psychological Association to describe any therapeutic or counselling intervention aimed at reducing, eliminating, or redirecting same-sex attraction or behaviour. The APA distinguishes this term from conversion therapy or reparative therapy to position such practices outside the scope of recognised evidence-based mental health care. The term is used in legislative advocacy to support legal bans on these practices, particularly with minors.
    Context: "sexual orientation change efforts to differentiate from recognized evidence-based forms of therapy"
    → Vocab tag: Term: sexual orientation change efforts
    Source: https://www.apa.org/topics/lgbtq/sexual-orientation-change

  SOGI change efforts ★
    SOGI change efforts is an acronym for sexual orientation and gender identity change efforts, encompassing any practice aimed at altering an individual's sexual orientation or gender identity. The term is used by mental health organisations and advocacy groups to refer to a broad category of interventions they consider harmful and non-evidence-based. SOGI change efforts are the target of legislative bans across multiple US states and are framed as coercive and psychologically damaging regardless of client consent or therapeutic approach.
    Context: "sexual orientation and gender identity SOGI change efforts commonly use an array of psychosocially harmful techniques"
    → Vocab tag: Term: SOGI change efforts
    Source: https://www.apa.org/topics/lgbtq/sexual-orientation-change

  sexual orientation change effort ★
    Sexual orientation change effort refers to any therapeutic, pastoral, or behavioral intervention designed to reduce, eliminate, or redirect same-sex sexual attractions or behaviors toward heterosexual orientation. The term is typically used by opponents of such practices to group together diverse approaches ranging from psychodynamic therapy to religious counseling under a single category for policy and advocacy purposes.
    Context: "Other terms used to describe conversion therapy include sexual orientation change effort (SOCE)"
    → Vocab tag: Term: sexual orientation change effort
    Source: https://irct.org/lgbti/

  SOCE ★
    SOCE is the acronym form of sexual orientation change effort, functioning as shorthand in clinical, legal, and advocacy discourse. The term encompasses therapeutic or pastoral practices aimed at changing, reducing, or redirecting same-sex sexual attractions or behaviors toward heterosexual orientation.
    Context: "Other terms used to describe conversion therapy include sexual orientation change effort (SOCE)"
    → Vocab tag: Term: SOCE
    Source: https://irct.org/lgbti/

  reorientation therapy ★
    Reorientation therapy describes therapeutic interventions aimed at reorienting sexual attractions from same-sex to opposite-sex targets. The term emphasises the directional change goal of such interventions and is used in both supportive and critical discourse about practices addressing unwanted same-sex attractions.
    Context: "Other terms used to describe conversion therapy include reorientation therapy"
    → Vocab tag: Term: reorientation therapy
    Source: https://irct.org/lgbti/

  ex-gay therapy ★
    Source: https://irct.org/lgbti/

  gay therapy ★
    A euphemistic phrase used to describe conversion practices targeting same-sex attracted individuals while avoiding the discredited term conversion therapy. The quotation marks in usage suggest the speaker is distancing themselves from critical terminology while still referencing attempts to change sexual orientation. This framing sanitises coercive or pseudoscientific interventions as neutral therapeutic services.
    Context: "Can you share with us about gay therapy"
    → Vocab tag: Term: gay therapy
    Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2J2zKReC6B4

  exercising human choice ★
    A rhetorical construction that frames participation in conversion practices as an expression of personal freedom and self-determination rather than coercion, social pressure, or internalised homophobia. This framing is used to oppose legal bans on conversion practices by arguing such bans restrict individual liberty. The phrase obscures the power dynamics, psychological manipulation, and family or religious coercion that typically characterise conversion practice contexts.
    Context: "exercising human choice and preserving religious freedom"
    → Vocab tag: Term: exercising human choice
    Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2J2zKReC6B4

    French term for conversion therapy, now criminalised in Belgium (since 20 July 2023). Defined legally as practices using physical intervention or psychological conditioning to modify or suppress sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression based on the erroneous premise that LGBTQIA+ people are sick.
    Context: "thérapies de conversion en Belgique"
    → Vocab tag: Term: thérapies de conversion

  [Italian]
  terapie riparative ★
    Source: https://www.fioriti.it/riviste/pdf/2/CC_Recensioni.pdf

  eterosessuali con problemi di omosessualità ★
    A pseudo-diagnostic phrase used by reparative therapy proponents to deny the existence of homosexual persons as a distinct category. According to this framework, individuals experiencing same-sex attraction are characterised as essentially heterosexual people suffering from a homosexuality problem that requires correction. This formulation pathologises same-sex attraction while preserving heterosexuality as the only valid human sexual orientation.
    Context: "Eterosessuali con problemi di omosessualità"
    → Vocab tag: Term: eterosessuali con problemi di omosessualità

  terapie affermative ★
    Therapeutic approaches that affirm homosexuality as a normal and positive variant of human sexual orientation. Affirmative therapies aim to support patients in accepting and integrating their sexual identity, addressing internalized homophobia and societal stigma rather than attempting to change orientation. The goal is patient liberation and self-acceptance within a framework of human rights and dignity.
    Context: "le nuove linee delle terapie affermative"
    → Vocab tag: Term: terapie affermative

  ideologia riparativa ★
    The ideological framework underlying reparative or conversion therapy approaches that view homosexuality as a developmental disorder requiring repair. Reparative ideology assumes heterosexuality represents the natural biological and divine design for humanity, framing same-sex attraction as a deviation or damage to be corrected through therapeutic intervention. The review systematically dismantles this ideology as scientifically unfounded and ethically harmful.
    Context: "Oltre l'ideologia riparativa dell'omosessualità"
    → Vocab tag: Term: ideologia riparativa

  Gay Affermative Therapy ★
    Gay Affirmative Therapy is a therapeutic approach that explicitly validates homosexual orientation and identity, helping clients navigate internalised homophobia, minority stress, and identity development rather than attempting orientation change. The therapy assumes homosexuality is a healthy variation of human sexuality and works to help clients achieve self-acceptance and wellbeing within a homosexual identity. According to the review, its values are considered valid even when working with religious clients who may hold conflicting beliefs.
    Context: "I valori del paradigma affermativo nella GAT Gay Affermative Therapy sono riconosciuti come validi"
    → Vocab tag: Term: Gay Affermative Therapy
    Source: https://www.fioriti.it/riviste/pdf/2/CC_Recensioni.pdf

    Context: "jeg som kristen lever etter bibelens seksualetikk, selv om jeg er tiltrukket til både menn og kvinner"
    → Vocab tag: Term: bibelens seksualetikk
    Source: https://subjekt.no/2022/09/19/forbud-mot-konverteringsterapi-er-illiberalt/

  homoterapi ★
    Norwegian shorthand for conversion therapy directed at homosexuality, implying practices intended to suppress or alter same-sex attraction or identity. The editorial uses the term to minimise the scope and severity of conversion practices, describing them as confined to religious contexts and not constituting formal medical treatment in Norway.
    Context: "Å forby konverteringsterapi, eller homoterapi, er blitt et prestisjeprosjekt"

SECTION 2 — TACTICS
Rhetorical strategies identified in SOGICE discourse
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Tactic: Anti-Gender Narrative
    Frames LGBTQ+ rights as ideological takeover of 'natural' gender

  Tactic: Anti-LGBT Conspiracy
    Global plots, indoctrination claims, 'they're coming for our children'

  Tactic: Anti-Trans Rhetoric
    Delegitimizing trans identity, attacking gender-affirming care

  Tactic: Dehumanizing-Language
    Slurs, describing identities as unnatural, degenerate, evil

  Tactic: Detrans-Propaganda
    Weaponizing detransition stories to restrict care or delegitimize identity

  Tactic: False-Scientific-Authority
    Misusing science or pseudo-diagnostics (ROGD, neuroplasticity myths)

  Tactic: Gender-Essentialism
    Rigid biological claims; true sex; restoring masculinity/femininity

  Tactic: Groomer-Panic
    Claiming LGBTQ+ people/educators groom or harm children

  Tactic: Identity-Erasure
    Minimizing or denying identity (labels don't define you)

  Tactic: Policy-Resistance-Frame
    Opposing bans by framing SOGICE as freedom, choice, rights

  Tactic: Rebranding-SOGICE
    SSA/SGA; congruence therapy; identity exploration; softening SOGICE

  Tactic: ROGD-Frame
    Using ROGD or social contagion theory to deny trans legitimacy

  Tactic: Sex-Rejection-Frame
    Framing transition as rejection of one's true biological sex

  Tactic: Social-Contagion-Myth
    Claiming LGBTQ+ identities spread like trends or contagion

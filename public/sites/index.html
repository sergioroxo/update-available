Four years, four websites

YOUR UPDATE HAS FAILED is a browser and headset piece about how organised conversion practices reached queer people online, from 1997 to 2026. Each year has a website of its own here. Each one holds everything about that year: the story, the practices, the sources, the pictures, the sound, and the words.

About the project

This is a piece about how organised “conversion” networks (SOGICE) targeted queer people online, from 1997 to 2026. Four updates, thirty years: none of them changed who anyone was.

It is part of SurvivingSOGICE, a research project at the University of Bergen's Center for Digital Narrative. Everything in it rests on that research: at the end you will find the Lexicon — the words these networks used and the reasons they gave — and the full list of sources is in the menu, under Credits & attributions.

It was directed by the project, and written and built in collaboration with Claude (Anthropic).

More to be added soon.

How these sites work

  • Each year's website is built from the same data the piece reads. When the piece changes, the sites are rebuilt, so the two cannot disagree.
  • The sites are plain pages. They have no scripts and no fonts from anywhere else. They make no requests and record nothing.
  • A link to an outside page opens in a new tab. That is your choice, never the site's.

This site is about conversion practices — efforts to change a person's sexual orientation or gender identity. It describes what they did and says plainly that they harm. Nobody in the piece is real. Every organisation and product in it is invented. The practices they perform are documented.
